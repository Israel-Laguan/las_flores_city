# Mercado Popular Las Flores

> Tags: `#landmark` `#industrial_zone` `#commercial` `#working_class`

**Location:** Las Flores City
**Type:** Public Market

## Overview

The Mercado Popular Las Flores predates the free city’s formal constitution by more than a decade — a sprawling open-air and semi-covered market that grew organically from the Industrial Zone’s labour camp supply networks.

---

## Sections

| Place | Description |
|---|---|
| **Eastern Protection Section** | The market’s eastern quarter, where La Familia-affiliated vendor protection schemes operate openly. Regular collection of “security contributions” from vendors; city enforcement absent. |
| **Water Vendor Row** | A row of water vendors required to display water-source provenance cards under a 2052 post-Leak health advisory. A 2075 university study found contamination readings above advisory threshold at three stalls. |
| **Wholesale Dawn Zone** | The market’s pre-dawn operational core, where produce and protein wholesale transactions take place before the retail day opens. |

## Daily Life

### Daytime

The Mercado Popular operates from before dawn to late afternoon on weekdays. By 07:00 the retail phase is running — hundreds of stalls across a seven-block footprint selling fresh produce, household goods, salvage electronics, and hot food.

### Nightlife

After the retail day ends, the Mercado Popular does not so much close as change register. The legitimate vendor stalls pack down and the eastern section’s informal economy operations expand into the space.

---

## Conclusion

The Mercado Popular Las Flores is the city’s most honest commercial space in the sense that it prices its goods at what Industrial Zone residents can actually afford and makes no attempt to disguise the informal systems that make its operation possible.
