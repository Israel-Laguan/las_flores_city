# Línea 2 Azul

> Tags: `#transport` `#industrial_zone` `#infrastructure`

**Location:** Las Flores City
**Type:** Metro Line

## Overview

The Línea 2 Azul was not built by civic ambition but by contractual obligation. Its construction was a condition inserted into LW Group’s 2043 Port Area expansion deal — a transit link the city extracted as partial compensation for land-use concessions.

---

## Stations

| Place | Description |
|---|---|
| **Eastern Factory Terminal Cluster** | A group of three adjacent stations serving the Industrial Zone’s largest factory complexes. Factory-operator cameras at platform exits record worker egress under an access arrangement not disclosed to workers. |
| **Port Connection Stations** | Western terminus stations linking the Blue Line to the Port Area dock network. Serve a primarily port-labour ridership; operational around the clock for dock workers. |
| **Contamination-Zone Tunnel Sections** | Underground sections traversing the Industrial Zone’s classified contaminated groundwater table. Active seepage management infrastructure at three stations. |

## Daily Life

### Daytime

The Blue Line carries the Industrial Zone’s shift-change rhythm across its twenty-three stations. At 05:30 eastbound trains run standing-room capacity; at 14:00 the pattern reverses. Tunnel sections show their environmental context in persistent seepage management.

### Nightlife

After the evening shift change the Blue Line’s ridership drops sharply. Night-shift factory workers board the eastbound service in low numbers; the Port Area terminals generate a trickle of westbound traffic through the small hours.

---

## Conclusion

The Línea 2 Azul is the city’s most functionally honest transit line: it connects the places where industrial labour is performed to the places where that labour is delivered, without the civic-mobility framing that the Red Line carries.
