# Las Flores Free City

> Tags: `#city`

**Location:** Las Flores City
**Type:** Metropolis

## Overview

Las Flores Free City stands as a sprawling metropolis of approximately 270,000 residents, spread across roughly 150 square kilometers between the Pacific coastline and the Andean Mountains.

---

## Districts

| Place | Description |
|---|---|
| **Pacific Coastal District** | The western urban edge where the city meets the ocean, featuring ports, beaches, and coastal businesses. |
| **Andean Mountain Corridor** | The eastern boundary where urban development meets mountain foothills, offering both residential expansion and natural reserves. |
| **Old Las Flores** | The historic southern heart of the city, preserving colonial architecture and cultural traditions near the river valley. |
| **Northern Elite Enclaves** | Upscale neighborhoods in the north and northeast, home to modern infrastructure, luxury residences, and exclusive amenities. |

## Daily Life

### Daytime

During daylight hours, Las Flores Free City reveals its layered socioeconomic geography. The northern and northeastern sectors showcase the city’s wealth, while industrial complexes and working-class neighborhoods dominate the landscape further south.

### Nightlife

After dark, Las Flores Free City’s nightlife mirrors its daytime inequalities. The wealthier northern and northeastern neighborhoods offer exclusive entertainment venues, while the south becomes more grounded and accessible.

---

## Conclusion

Las Flores Free City is a microcosm of geographic and social diversity, spanning from ocean to mountains and from desert to swamps. Its 270,000 inhabitants occupy a landscape where wealth and poverty, history and modernity exist in constant dialogue.
