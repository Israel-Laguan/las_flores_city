# Línea 1 Roja

> Tags: `#transport` `#central_city` `#infrastructure`

**Location:** Las Flores City
**Type:** Metro Line

## Overview

The Línea 1 Roja — the North–South corridor of the Metro de Las Flores — was the first metro line to achieve full operational status, inaugurated in 2042 as the new city’s demonstration that it could build civic infrastructure commensurate with its constitutional ambitions.

---

## Stations

| Place | Description |
|---|---|
| **North Las Flores Terminus** | The northern terminal station serving the enclave district, built to premium finishing specifications and maintained on daily cleaning cycles. Ridership is significantly lighter here than at the southern terminus. |
| **Central Interchange Stations** | Mid-line stations connecting the Red Line to the Blue and Green Lines. Primary bottleneck during peak hours; delay propagation originates here. |
| **Old Las Flores Terminus** | The southern terminal station serving Old Las Flores. Built to standard specification in 2042; on deferred maintenance since 2059. |

## Daily Life

### Daytime

During morning peak hours the Línea 1 Roja runs at four-minute headways, its carriages at full capacity through the central stations. Journey times on the full north-south run average twenty-two minutes outside peak hours and thirty-six minutes during the morning surge.

### Nightlife

After 22:00 the Línea 1 Roja shifts to ten-minute headways, its late-night ridership composed primarily of shift workers, service industry personnel, and the city’s precariat class navigating the distance between where they work and where they can afford to sleep.

---

## Conclusion

The Línea 1 Roja was built to connect the city’s economic poles and has succeeded in that function while making the distance between them more efficient to traverse but no shorter in social terms.
