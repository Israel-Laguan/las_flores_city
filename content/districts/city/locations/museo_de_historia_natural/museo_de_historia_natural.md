# Museo de Historia Natural

> Tags: `#landmark` `#central_city` `#education`

**Location:** Las Flores City
**Type:** Natural History Museum

## Overview

The Museo de Historia Natural opened in 2039 as a cornerstone of Las Flores Free City’s commitment to scientific literacy and cultural memory. Its founding collection was assembled from donated specimens and a contested transfer of pre-Columbian artefacts.

---

## Facilities

| Place | Description |
|---|---|
| **Atrium & Megatherium Display** | The museum’s central vaulted hall, dominated by a reconstructed Megatherium skeleton suspended above a holographic paleoecological floor installation. The focal point of every school-group visit. |
| **Geological Wing** | Eastern gallery dedicated to Andean mineral geology, featuring interactive extraction route maps. Sourced exclusively from LW Group-approved public datasets; the 2048 and 2055 suppressed fault-line reports are not among them. |
| **People of the River Valleys Exhibit** | A permanent anthropological exhibit presenting pre-colonial indigenous cultures. Contested by indigenous community councils since 2061; a two-sentence displacement acknowledgement was added in 2063. |

## Daily Life

### Daytime

Visitors enter through a vaulted atrium dominated by a reconstructed megafauna skeleton suspended above a holographic rainforest floor. Holographic docent projectors deliver narration in Spanish, English, and Mandarin, their scripts reviewed by a curatorial committee including two LW Group representatives.

### Nightlife

After 18:00 the museum transitions to its members-and-events register. The public galleries close in sequence; the atrium remains accessible for private bookings beneath the Megatherium skeleton.

---

## Conclusion

The Museo de Historia Natural occupies the uncomfortable position of being Las Flores’ most authoritative scientific institution and simultaneously one of its most structurally compromised. What it excludes is as informative as what it displays.
