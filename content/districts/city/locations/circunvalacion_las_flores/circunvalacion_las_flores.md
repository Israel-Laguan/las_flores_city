# Circunvalación Las Flores

> Tags: `#transport` `#infrastructure`

**Location:** Las Flores City
**Type:** Ring Road

## Overview

The Circunvalación Las Flores was planned as the city’s outer boundary — a ring road encircling the urban core, intended to absorb through-traffic that would otherwise penetrate the central district and to define a physical perimeter between the city and the periphery beyond.

---

## Sections

| Place | Description |
|---|---|
| **Northern Section** | The ring road’s highest-specification quadrant, serving the enclave district. Maintained to 2074 standard; traffic cameras equipped with vehicle registration scanning that routes to both city authority and the enclave’s private security network. |
| **Southern Section Informal Settlement Zone** | The outer edge of the ring road’s southern quadrant, where informal settlements have grown in the space between the carriageway and the city’s official boundary. No formal address, no municipal service connection, no planning record. |
| **Deferred Maintenance Stretches** | The patched and below-standard sections of the southern and eastern ring road, classified as stable-pending-replacement in the infrastructure authority’s deferred maintenance registry. |

## Daily Life

### Daytime

The Circunvalación carries its primary function during daylight hours: freight vehicles diverting from the congested central avenues, airport transfer coaches, construction logistics, and continuous port-bound cargo. Section condition varies significantly by quadrant.

### Nightlife

After 22:00 the Circunvalación’s freight traffic reduces but does not stop. The informal settlements along the outer edge are most legible after dark: light from cooking fires and personal devices is visible above the sound barriers in the southern section.

---

## Conclusion

The Circunvalación Las Flores encircles the city and, in doing so, defines what the city considers inside and outside its care. The outer edge of the southern section is the ring road’s most politically legible feature.
