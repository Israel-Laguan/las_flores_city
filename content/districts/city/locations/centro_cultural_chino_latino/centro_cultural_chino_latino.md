# Centro Cultural Chino-Latino

> Tags: `#landmark` `#central_city` `#culture`

**Location:** Las Flores City
**Type:** Cultural Centre

## Overview

The Centro Cultural Chino-Latino was inaugurated in 2060 — two years after the industrial riots that had convulsed the city’s working-class districts — as a shared civic building housing programming from three communities under one roof, funded through a corporate grant.

---

## Facilities

| Place | Description |
|---|---|
| **Main Hall** | Four-hundred-capacity event space with adaptive holographic décor configurable per community booking. The most contested resource on the joint management board’s calendar. |
| **Shared Community Café** | A central café intended as the building’s primary integration space. In practice, tables self-organise by language affinity by midmorning. |
| **Multilingual Reading Room** | A library space with materials in Spanish, Cantonese, Mandarin, and Dutch, supplemented by holographic text interfaces. The most consistently low-conflict space in the building. |
| **Holographic Community History Corridor** | A connecting corridor displaying rotating holographic narratives from the Chinese, Latino, and Afro-Dutch communities. Each narrative was produced by the relevant community group within parameters reviewed by the LW Group cultural liaison; the corridor is experienced as a sequence rather than a synthesis. |

## Daily Life

### Daytime

Morning and afternoon programming rotates by community group on a monthly schedule negotiated by the joint management board. The common spaces are designed for intermingling; in practice, café tables are often sorted by language affinity by midmorning.

### Nightlife

Evening programming is where the booking disputes tend to surface most visibly. The main hall is in peak demand for evening cultural events, and the allocation schedule has never stabilised into a format all three communities find equitable.

---

## Conclusion

The Centro Cultural Chino-Latino is the city’s most honest institutional embodiment of its three-community tension: a space that exists because the tension required a physical response and that reproduces the tension within its own operations every month.
