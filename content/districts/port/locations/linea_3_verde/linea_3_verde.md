# Línea 3 Verde

> Tags: `#transport` `#port_area` `#infrastructure`

**Location:** Las Flores City
**Type:** Metro Line

## Overview

The Línea 3 Verde is the Port Area loop — a surface and elevated rail circuit connecting the dock terminals to the Central district interchange, running through the Port Area’s residential quarter and the waterfront logistics zone.

---

## Stations

| Place | Description |
|---|---|
| **Port Dock Station** | The elevated station near the port dock terminal where the 2071 derailment occurred. Operational since 2072 after eight months of closure; interim repairs are visible on the track bed support structures. |
| **Central District Interchange** | The Green Line’s highest-ridership station, connecting to the Red and Blue Lines. Operating at six-minute peak headways; the practical hub for the line’s commuter traffic. |
| **Elevated Port Loop** | The elevated track section traversing the waterfront logistics zone. Interim repair patches visible from ground level bear date stamps from 2072 to 2076. |

## Daily Life

### Daytime

The Green Line’s service frequency varies across its loop: the Central district stations run at six-minute headways during peak hours. The dock terminal stations run at nine-minute headways. The elevated sections carry visible signs of their maintenance history.

### Nightlife

After 21:00 the Green Line reduces to fifteen-minute headways and runs with reduced staffing at its surface stations. The dock terminal stations close at 23:00 on weekdays, leaving overnight port workers dependent on surface bus connections.

---

## Conclusion

The Línea 3 Verde is the city’s most functionally compromised public transit asset: a line with an irregular service record, a known and unresolved structural audit gap, and a defining incident whose full accountability remains embedded in unpublished internal documents.
