# Acuario Las Flores

> Tags: `#landmark` `#port_area` `#entertainment`

**Location:** Las Flores City
**Type:** Aquarium

## Overview

The Acuario Las Flores stands at the mouth of the Río de las Flores delta, a monument to the city’s complicated relationship with its own waters. When the facility first opened in 2041 it was conceived as a conservation showcase.

---

## Facilities

| Place | Description |
|---|---|
| **Holographic Coral Hall** | The facility’s centrepiece gallery, where floor-to-ceiling holographic projectors reconstruct reef formations destroyed by the 2052 Lithium Leak. The role of LW Group in the contamination event is not mentioned. |
| **LW Group Marine Research Wing** | Corporate-sponsored genetics laboratory and bioreactor suite accessible on the standard visitor route. Researchers develop heat-resistant coral strains under LW Group IP agreements. |
| **Resilience Wing** | Permanent exhibition framing the post-2052 rebuilding effort as a story of institutional perseverance. The suppressed environmental-liability report is not on display. |
| **Bioluminescent Jellyfish Gallery** | Evening-hours installation featuring genetically modified jellyfish cultures in wall-length tanks. Designed for atmospheric effect rather than scientific interpretation. |

## Daily Life

### Daytime

Under the aquarium’s diffused-glass canopy the daytime experience is engineered calm. Holographic coral reef displays stretch across the main hall, rendering in photorealistic detail the reef formations that no longer exist outside.

### Nightlife

After hours the aquarium reinvents itself. The main corridors are bathed in blue and violet bioluminescent lighting sourced from genetically modified jellyfish cultures. Ticketed evening visitors move through near-darkness punctuated by softly glowing tanks.

---

## Conclusion

The Acuario Las Flores occupies an uncomfortable position in the city’s cultural landscape: a public institution that would not exist in its current form without the corporate patronage of the entity most responsible for its original decline.
