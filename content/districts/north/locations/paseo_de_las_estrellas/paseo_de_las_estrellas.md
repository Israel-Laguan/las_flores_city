# Paseo de las Estrellas

> Tags: `#transport` `#north_las_flores` `#luxury` `#infrastructure`

**Location:** Las Flores City
**Type:** Boulevard

## Overview

The Paseo de las Estrellas is North Las Flores’s answer to every complaint about Avenida Independencia — a boulevard designed from inception in 2053 to function as both upscale commercial frontage and premium urban infrastructure.

---

## Features

| Place | Description |
|---|---|
| **Holographic Storefront Mile** | The main commercial frontage of the Paseo, where brand-exclusive retail units operate holographic product environments calibrated for pavement viewing. Tenancy managed through the LW Group mall management subsidiary. |
| **EV Charging Lane Network** | Embedded carriageway charging infrastructure serving the enclave’s electric vehicle fleet at near-zero wait times. Built under a public-private partnership that charged construction cost to the city’s infrastructure authority. |
| **Private Security Patrol Route** | The rolling patrol circuit maintained by four-person security units contracted through an LW Group security subsidiary. Interval: ten minutes daytime, tightening after 23:00. |

## Daily Life

### Daytime

The Paseo operates as a premium pedestrian and vehicle environment. Wide, landscaped pavements accommodate the enclave’s professional and diplomatic class on foot; holographic storefronts project product environments into the pavement space.

### Nightlife

After 22:00 the Paseo de las Estrellas maintains a curated evening atmosphere. Restaurant terraces remain operational until midnight; private security patrol frequency increases after 23:00.

---

## Conclusion

The Paseo de las Estrellas is Avenida Independencia’s counterpart in a city that has made explicit decisions about which infrastructure deserves investment. The eleven-minute drive between them is the city’s most efficient measurement of inequality.
