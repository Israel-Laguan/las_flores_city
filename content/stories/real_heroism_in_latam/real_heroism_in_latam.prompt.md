## Prompt — Base (Kitchen Scene - Origin Moment)
A cinematic comic-book panel, modern colorful graphic novel style. A small working-class South American home, early morning. Warm kitchen-window light with cooler shadows; a modest lived-in room—magnet-covered refrigerator, worn tablecloth, school photos, a small TV showing football. Focus: an 18-year-old Latina woman, ponytail, casual clothes, finally eating, quiet exhaustion forcing a smile. Her mother watches nearby; her father, dressed for work, pauses; her younger brother sits frozen. No one argues; sadness is subtle and domestic. Strong facial acting, DC/Marvel-quality comic illustration, vibrant grounded colors.
