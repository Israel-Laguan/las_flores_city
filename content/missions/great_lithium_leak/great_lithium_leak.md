# The Great Lithium Leak

Decades after the catastrophic 2052 lithium leak poisoned the Rio de las Flores, buried evidence surfaces showing that the disaster was not an accident. The LW Group staged an electrical failure, silenced witnesses, and relied on Minera Estrella's influence to keep the cover-up intact.

The investigation follows the evidence from the damaged river infrastructure to the people who were pressured into silence. Lina Kim of La Prensa helps frame the stakes: exposing the truth may restore a measure of accountability, but it can also put witnesses and investigators in the path of the corporation that profited from the disaster.

The mission is about recovering a record of what happened and deciding what justice can look like when the institutions responsible for protecting the city have already chosen their side.
