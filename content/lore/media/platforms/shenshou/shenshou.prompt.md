## Prompt
Flat vector logo emblem for "ShénShǒu (神兽)" — "Divine Beast" — a Chinese
social and e-commerce and AR platform in 2077. Distinct mark derived from its
name and concept: a stylized geometric divine-beast crest (qilin/dragon head)
framed by subtle AR-corner brackets, evoking AR filters, holographic
backgrounds, influencer microstores, and the cultural bridge between Chinese
and local content. Imperial-red base with gold and jade accents,
monochrome-to-two-color palette fitting the premium, cross-cultural brand. Clean
geometric shape, minimal, scalable, centered on transparent background,
transparent PNG, 8k. Graphic novel / editorial flat-vector style consistent with
the project.
