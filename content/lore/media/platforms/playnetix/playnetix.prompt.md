# Prompt: PlayNetix logo

## Prompt
Flat vector logo emblem for "PlayNetix", a North American gamified
video-streaming platform in 2077. Distinct mark derived from its name roots
"play" and "Netix" — a play-triangle fused with a game-pad directional chevron
and a small "stream points" diamond, evoking game rooms, leaderboards, and the
competitive youth-gamer culture. Electric-green base with a violet accent,
monochrome-to-two-color palette fitting the energetic, competitive brand. Clean
geometric shape, minimal, scalable, centered on transparent background,
transparent PNG, 8k. Graphic novel / editorial flat-vector style consistent with
the project.
