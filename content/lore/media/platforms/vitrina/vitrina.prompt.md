# Prompt: Vitrina logo

**Tool:** MidJourney --v 6 --ar 1:1 --style raw

## Prompt
Flat vector logo emblem for "Vitrina", a Latin American video and image-sharing
community platform in 2077. Distinct mark derived from its Spanish name
"vitrina" (showcase / display window) — a multi-pane display-window frame with
an asymmetric slightly-chaotic grid and a small play/scroll glyph, evoking meme
generators, viral challenges, DIY tutorials, and the vibrant, chaotic energy of
Latin American street culture. Coral base with a turquoise accent,
monochrome-to-two-color palette fitting the warm, community-driven brand. Clean
geometric shape, minimal, scalable, centered on transparent background,
transparent PNG, 8k. Graphic novel / editorial flat-vector style consistent with
the project.

## Negative Prompt
--no photorealistic photo, 3D render, text inside emblem, literal letters
replacing the mark, gradients, drop shadows, crowded detail, anime, cartoon,
watermark
