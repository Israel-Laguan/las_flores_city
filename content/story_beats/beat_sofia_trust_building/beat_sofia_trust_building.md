# Beat 3 — The Confession

When Sofia trusts the player enough, the relationship moves away from the street and into a quiet conversation at a local food stand. She reveals that Sofia Mendoza is an adopted name: she was Graciela Ramirez before leaving her old life behind.

She tells the player about Camilo Orozco's death, the grief that drove her toward vigilante ideas, and the damage caused by trying to protect people through violence. An empathetic response and a reciprocal moment of vulnerability can change her status to romanced. At high trust, Sofia becomes a permanent ally, offering safehouses and intelligence.
