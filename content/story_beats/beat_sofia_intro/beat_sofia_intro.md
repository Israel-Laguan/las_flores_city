# Beat 1 — The Encounter

In South Las Flores, the player spots Sofia handing out community flyers while low-level gangers harass her. The first response establishes the relationship and the story's central question: whether protection can remain humane when the city rewards force.

Violent intervention raises Sofia's trust, but leaves her grateful and disturbed. A diplomatic or intimidating response impresses her more deeply. Walking away leaves her disillusioned, applies the harshest trust loss, and locks the questline until the player pays a hospital bribe.
