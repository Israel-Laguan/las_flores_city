# Prompt: Beat 1 — The Encounter

Grounded graphic-novel realism in South Las Flores: Sofia Mendoza, a young community organizer, holds a stack of flyers while low-level gangers crowd her on a worn neighborhood street. The player is arriving at the edge of the confrontation. Working-class homes, faded walls, motorcycles, and a community center sign establish the setting. Tense but restrained, human expressions, no glorification of violence, no text or logos.
