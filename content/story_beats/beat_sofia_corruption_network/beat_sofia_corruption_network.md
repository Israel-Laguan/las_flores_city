# Beat 4 — The Machine Exposed

Sofia discovers that Los Culebras is being funded by Minera Estrella to destabilize South Las Flores and make cheap real-estate buyouts possible. The gang's violence is part of a larger machine, and the community center is one of the places placed directly in its path.

The player can help Sofia leak the data to the press and defend the community center from corporate thugs, earning a major reputation boost in South Las Flores. Or the player can steal the data and blackmail the corporation for credits. The mercenary choice may pay immediately, but Sofia is disgusted and cuts ties.
