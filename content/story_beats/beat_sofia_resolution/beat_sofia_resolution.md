# Beat 5 — Finale

The finale reflects every earlier choice. With high trust and a romantic relationship, Sofia remains in Las Flores and builds a resilient community network; the player gains a loyal faction leader. If she was ignored or betrayed, she becomes disillusioned. She may leave the city or die trying to confront the corruption alone, and the player loses access to civil-society perks.

There is no single clean victory. The resolution measures whether the player helped Sofia preserve her humanity and whether the city gained a durable way to protect its people.
