# Beat 2 — The Brother's Orbit

After the player helps Sofia, she makes contact with a more personal emergency. Her younger brother Alberto has been running packages for Los Culebras. Sofia asks the player to intercept him without killing him, because Alberto is being treated as a disposable kid rather than a committed gang member.

The player can talk Alberto down and save him, accept a gang bribe and sell Sofia out, or use lethal force and let Alberto be caught in the crossfire. Each choice reshapes Alberto's status and Sofia's trust, opening the romance path, failing the quest, or turning Sofia into a permanent enemy.
