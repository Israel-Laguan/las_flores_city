# Estación Central — Metro Platform

> Tags: urban

**District:** City

## Overview

The main interchange platform of Estación Central, the hub where Líneas 1, 2, and 3 converge beneath the city's commercial district. Surveillance arrays line the platform ceiling at a density exceeding city average. Automated turnstiles track every entry and exit. Cool, harsh platform lighting under a tense, transient mood with a train arriving and platform wind stirring the air.

- Long interchange platform beneath converging metro lines
- Surveillance arrays lining the platform ceiling
- Automated turnstiles and a holographic route map
