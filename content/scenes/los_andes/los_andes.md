# Los Andes Heights

> Tags: urban

**District:** Los Andes

## Overview

The elevated neighborhoods overlooking Las Flores, where wealthier residents enjoy cleaner air and sweeping views of the valley below. The elevation provides a clear view of the entire city, air quality sensors indicate optimal levels, and security drones patrol the perimeter. An exclusive, pristine atmosphere under bright clear mountain light with the glittering city spread far below.

- Sweeping valley-view terrace overlooking the city below
- Pristine modernist residences with clean architecture
- Distant city lights glittering across the valley
