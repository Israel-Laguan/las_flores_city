# Northern District

> Tags: urban

**District:** North

## Overview

A mixed-use area with residential blocks and small commercial zones. The middle class lives and works within walking distance. Residents move through their daily routines, small shops line the main avenue, and the neighborhood watch app shows normal activity levels. An ordinary, familiar atmosphere under even daylight with warm evening storefront glow.

- Residential blocks lining a wide commercial avenue
- Small storefronts with modest signage along the street
- Neighborhood parks and daily-routine street scenes
