# Far South Outskirts

> Tags: urban

**District:** Far South

## Overview

The rural edges of Las Flores, where the city gives way to agricultural land and scattered homesteads. Quiet and isolated. Wind moves through the crops, air quality is significantly better than the city center, and solar panels on distant rooftops catch the afternoon light. A serene, open landscape under soft natural sunlight.

- Dusty unpaved road through agricultural fields
- Scattered homesteads with rooftop solar panels
- Open farmland stretching toward the city's distant glow
