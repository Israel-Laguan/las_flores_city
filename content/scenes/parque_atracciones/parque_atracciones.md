# Parque de Atracciones — Midway

> Tags: urban

**District:** Port

## Overview

The central midway of the Parque de Atracciones Las Flores, where holographic ride-overlay projectors transform aging mechanical frames into immersive spectacle. Crowd noise, synthesized music, and the periodic thunderclap of a holographic sequence make quiet observation difficult. A stimulating, saturated atmosphere under bright carnival lights and projected spectacle.

- Central midway lined with holographic ride overlays
- Bright carnival lights and fairground stalls on both sides
- Aged mechanical ride frames glowing beneath projected scenes
