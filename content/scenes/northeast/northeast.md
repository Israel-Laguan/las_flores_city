# Northeast Quarter

> Tags: urban

**District:** Northeast

## Overview

A rapidly developing area with new construction alongside older buildings. Change is the only constant here. Construction equipment dominates the skyline, old and new architecture clash at every corner, and property values are rising. A transitional, uneven atmosphere mixing raw concrete, scaffolding, and glowing new facades.

- New construction towers rising beside aging buildings
- Scaffolding and construction cranes along the skyline
- Old and new façades clashing on every corner
