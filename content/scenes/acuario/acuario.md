# Acuario Las Flores — Interior

> Tags: urban

**District:** Port

## Overview

The main gallery floor of the Acuario Las Flores, where holographic coral displays stretch across the dimly lit hall. The hum of filtration systems and the glow of bioluminescent tank installations create an atmosphere of engineered tranquility, distant from the contaminated delta outside. Dim teal bioluminescence and soft blue aquarium light wash over curved glass tanks and corridor walkways.

- Holographic coral reef displays lining the central gallery
- Bioluminescent tank installations with soft teal glow
- Curved glass exhibits and filtration equipment in dim light
