# Industrial District

> Tags: urban

**District:** Industrial

## Overview

Factories and warehouses line the streets, their smokestacks mixing with the humid air. The backbone of Las Flores' manufacturing economy. Machine hum dominates the soundscape, filtration systems cycle, and workers move between buildings with practiced efficiency. A gritty, hard-edged atmosphere under industrial lighting and steam-lit haze.

- Smokestacks and factory silhouettes against a hazy sky
- Warehouse loading bays and humming machine halls
- Steam venting over rain-slicked industrial pavement
