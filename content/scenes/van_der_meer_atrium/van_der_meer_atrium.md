# Van der Meer Tower — Sky Atrium

> Tags: `#private` `#van_der_meer` `#amor_verdadero` `#city`

**District:** City (Central City)

## Overview

A glass-roofed garden on the upper floors of the Van der Meer tower. It is the family's private hall for dinners, charity previews, and the Amor Verdadero youth salon, where Peter van der Meer Jr. spends many of his afternoons. Visitors come up a private lift and are screened by a greeter and security staff who know exactly who is expected.

- Real soil, real trees, controlled humidity
- A long table of hand-ribboned Amor Verdadero flyers and pins
- A skyline terrace overlooking the districts the family's money touches

## Why he is here

Peter Jr. is a Van der Meer heir. Nobody simply bumps into him in the plaza or at a classroom door. His days are shaped by security, schedules and his aunt Anna's staff, and the Sky Atrium is where those two worlds overlap with his activism. Reaching him is part of the story: the player has to be let in, vouched for, or noticed.
