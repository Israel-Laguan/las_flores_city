# Pacific Coast

> Tags: urban

**District:** Pacific

## Overview

Where the city meets the ocean. Fishing boats, seafood markets, and the constant rhythm of waves define this district. Salt air mixes with fish and diesel, the tide is coming in, and fishermen are securing their vessels for the evening. A maritime, salt-worn atmosphere under a misty coastal sky with harbour lights beginning to glow.

- Fishing boats moored in a busy harbour as the tide rises
- Seafood market stalls along the waterfront
- Coastal mist and salt-worn docks catching evening light
