# Central Plaza

> Tags: urban

**District:** Central

## Overview

The bustling heart of Las Flores, where old colonial architecture meets neon-lit modernity. Street vendors and holographic billboards share the same sidewalks. Holographic advertisements flicker above the fountain and the crowd density is moderate, producing a vibrant, energetic atmosphere lit by a mix of warm afternoon sun and bright neon signage.

- Fountains surrounded by colonial façades and neon signs
- Street vendor stalls along the plaza edges
- Holographic billboards flickering above the square
