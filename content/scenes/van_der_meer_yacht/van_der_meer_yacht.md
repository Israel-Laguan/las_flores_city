# Van der Meer Yacht — Sundeck

> Tags: `#private` `#van_der_meer` `#influencer` `#port`

**District:** Port

## Overview

The front stage of Peter van der Meer Jr.'s public life. On weekends the family's yacht hosts "Amor Verdadero Sunset Socials": a hired photographer, ring lights, a speaker rig, and a curated crowd. His manager Adrián Beltrán schedules every shoot. The deck is loud, bright and generous with free drinks and free attention, and it is where the "friends" in his feed are photographed.

- Ring-light stations and a hired photographer
- A crowd that always knows where the cameras are
- A stern rail, out of frame, where he sometimes stands alone with his phone

## Why it matters

It is the contrast with the Sky Atrium. On the deck he is the host; in the Atrium he is a boy with a schedule. The player is meant to feel the gap.
