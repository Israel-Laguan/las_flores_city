# Welcome Center

> Tags: urban

**District:** City

## Overview

The official welcome center for new arrivals in Las Flores. A sleek, modern facility designed to process and orient newcomers to the city. A holographic map cycles through district data, the monorail station glows beyond the glass, and rain on the glass creates prismatic patterns. A neutral, polished atmosphere under clean overhead lighting with soft electronic music.

- Sleek modern reception hall with a holographic map
- Processing desks and orientation kiosks under clean light
- Monorail station visible through rain-streaked glass
