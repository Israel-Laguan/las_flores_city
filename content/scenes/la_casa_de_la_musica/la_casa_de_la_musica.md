# La Casa de la Música — Main Floor

> Tags: urban

**District:** City

## Overview

The main performance floor of La Casa de la Música, where the holographic light rig hangs in standby configuration above the crowd. The converted textile warehouse's acoustic panels absorb and redirect sound with a precision that its improvised origins do not suggest. Energetic atmosphere under a dim, warm stage glow and the dramatic contrast of a single performance spotlight.

- Converted textile warehouse with exposed steel rafters
- Holographic light rig in standby over the dance floor
- Acoustic panels along the walls catching stage light
