# Old Town Cafe

> Tags: urban

**District:** South

## Overview

A cozy cafe tucked away in the old quarter. The smell of fresh coffee mingles with the sound of jazz playing from an old vinyl record player. Local regulars nod hello as you enter. Warm, amber lighting, worn wooden tables, and the soft crackle of a vinyl record create an intimate, nostalgic mood.

- Worn wooden tables in a warm amber-lit room
- Old vinyl record player beside the counter
- Coffee bar with roasting beans and damp-brick walls
