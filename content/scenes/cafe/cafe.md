# Old Town Café

> Tags: urban

**District:** South

## Overview

A warm, slightly worn coffee shop in the historic district of Las Flores. Smells of roasted beans and damp brick. The espresso machine releases pressurized steam while a slow synthesized Andean melody plays over the speakers. Soft warm lighting, exposed brick, and a worn wooden counter create a cozy, inviting mood.

- Exposed brick walls and a worn wooden counter
- Espresso machine venting steam in warm light
- Cozy seating with slow ambient music
