# The Apartment

> Tags: urban

**District:** City

## Overview

A small, sterile apartment in the N&M LTD residential block. The walls are white, the furniture is minimal, and a single window looks out over the neon-lit streets of Las Flores. This is home for the duration of the experiment. Rain streaks the window, casting shifting neon patterns across a tense, minimalist space lit by cool white light and the glow of the city below.

- Minimal white-walled room with sparse furniture
- Single window overlooking neon-lit city streets
- Rain streaks across the glass casting shifting neon light
