# Southeast Barrio

> Tags: urban

**District:** Southeast

## Overview

A vibrant working-class neighborhood with strong community ties. Murals cover every wall, telling stories of resistance and hope. Community members gather at the local park, and the neighborhood radio plays local music. A resilient, expressive atmosphere under warm afternoon sun with vivid painted walls and laundry lines.

- Vibrant murals covering every wall of the barrio
- Community park where neighbors gather under the sun
- Colorful painted façades and laundry lines overhead
