# Valentina Quan Relationship

Valentina's relationship arc unfolds across five acts chained at the airport: a first off-duty meeting at the lounge bar, the layover stories behind her curated travel life, the wall she throws up when asked what "this" is, the father wound she finally names — a Dragon Head middle-manager father, a trapped mother, an envelope economy — and one of four runways the relationship takes off from, or crashes on.
