# Isabella Vasquez — Intro

First meeting with Chronicle owner **Isabella Vasquez** at a society function or Chronicle-adjacent lounge. She clocks the player's status, usefulness, and looks, then offers a trade dressed as conversation. Branches differ for charm, suspicion, and pushback on her classism. Juan Pérez may be name-dropped as her star reporter; Valeria as the prima she does not want on the cover.
