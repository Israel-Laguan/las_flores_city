# Ana Villanueva Relationship

Ana Villanueva is an office assistant by day and an online seller by night, an entrepreneur in the making who is saving to open her own storefront. The arc asks one question: **what is the hustle for?** Ana's ambition is real and admirable, and so is the fear under it: parents who taught for thirty years and still count coins, and a vow never to depend on someone else's payroll. The hard part for the player is not persuading her to do less, but finding a way for her to rest without feeling that she lost a sale.

## Structure

- **Intro (office):** the player meets her in the import office while she sells serum on her lunch. Asking what the hustle is for, rather than buying or ignoring her, opens the door.
- **Midgame (rooftop night market date):** Ana arrives with a tripod and ring light and turns the date into content before the first bite. Three approaches:
  - play along and be scenery (she has a great workday, the Lover path closes);
  - snatch her phone (she hears "your ambition is a hobby" and the Enemy path opens);
  - speak her language and propose **closing hours** like any shop, with ninety minutes of "dead feed" in exchange for one photo of her choosing at the end of the night. She still takes any order that comes in, so she never feels she is giving up the business.
- **Vulnerable beat:** with her hands free she is bad at being still. Giving her something to do (guess the spice) or sitting with her in silence leads to the confession: she does not know who she is when nothing is for sale.
- **Endings:** Lover (a storefront sign reading "Closing hours, 6 p.m. sharp" and a dinner with no tripod), Friend (first-Friday dinners with phones in the bag), Ex/Tragic (she scales, eats alone while filming and leaves for the capital), Enemy (a two-star review and a closed door).

## Design notes

- Never mock the hustle. Every shop closes; closing hours are a business concept she respects.
- Romance is gated behind friendship 20 and only advances after the confession.
- She is unrelated to Alex's group and the Las Estrellas investigation; for that arc see [Ana Kim](../ana_kim_relationship/ana_kim_relationship.md).
