# Prompt: Peter Jr. Relationship

Peter van der Meer Jr. is 14, a Dutch-Brazilian heir with a public activist role. Write him as warm, quick, earnest and occasionally too sure of himself. He code-switches into Dutch (nou, joh, gezellig, tante, pap) and Portuguese (mãe, tá bom, nossa, saudade, obrigado) in small doses, more when nervous or comfortable.

Rules:
- Player lines are gender-neutral; never assume the player's gender.
- No romantic or sexual content or paths. The arc is friendship, mentorship, rivalry, drifting apart, or distance.
- Use only the expressions in the portrait set: default, determined, happy, vulnerable, angry, sad.
