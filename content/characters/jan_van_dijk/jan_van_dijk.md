# Jan van Dijk

**Title:** CEO, Global Lithium Corp (GLC)

**Physical Description:**
- Dutch man in his early 50s with a broad, heavy, prosperous build, thin receding gray-blonde hair, a square face, shrewd almond eyes and a small mole on his left cheek; wears a tailored suit with a GLC lapel pin.
- **Identifying detail:** the small mole on his left cheek and the GLC pin, which he straightens before speaking in public. *(the habit is invented)*

**Description (full):**

Dutch businessman and CEO of Global Lithium Corp. Launched the 'Futuro Brillante' scholarship program to support students from Old Las Flores and combat gang violence through education.

**Age (2077):** 52 (b. 2025)
**District:** Las Flores
**Role:** CEO
**Descendancy:** Dutch

**Not to be confused with:** [Jan van der Meer](../jan_van_der_meer/jan_van_der_meer.md) (b. 2019, d. 2060), the family member who led GLC from 2045 until his death. Jan van Dijk (b. 2025) is an unrelated professional executive who runs GLC in 2077. Also not [Jan van der Meer Jr.](../jan_van_der_meer_jr/jan_van_der_meer_jr.md), Peter's husband.

## Background

- **GLC** is the Van der Meer family's parent company, an international mining corporation. After Jan van der Meer's death in January 2060, it is run by a professional executive rather than a family member; Van Dijk holds the post by 2077. *(when he was appointed is not stated; any date or earlier career is invented)*
- **Futuro Brillante.** GLC launches the "Bright Future" program: full scholarships for high school seniors from Old Las Flores, with mentorship by GLC professionals and internships in the lithium industry. Priority goes to students from families with limited resources and a record of community service; the criteria are academic excellence, leadership potential and the ability to overcome adversity. The program is meant to counter gang recruitment, and GLC pledges further educational programming with local schools.
- **His public line:** "We believe that investing in the next generation is key to creating a brighter future for all residents of Las Flores."
- The launch was reported by Pedro Alvarez of *El Informador* ([GLC Launches Scholarship Program](../../lore/stories/glc_scholarship_program/glc_scholarship_program.md)).

## Want, Flaw, Fear

- **Want:** A GLC that is seen as part of the city's future rather than of its contamination. The scholarship is the public face of that goal. *(inferred)*
- **Flaw:** Corporate philanthropy as image management. The program arrives while GLC's mining base is the source of the harm that keeps Old Las Flores poor and, in the Garcias' case, poisoned. *(interpretation)*
- **Fear:** A scandal reaching GLC through the Van der Meer family, such as Dong's syndicate or the party footage, undoing the program's credibility. *(invented)*

## Habits and Contradictions

- Measured, watchful, speaks in the language of investment and "breaking the cycle". *(from his psychological profile)*
- The contradiction: a Dutch outsider who is the public benefactor of the community whose river and air the company's industry contaminates.
