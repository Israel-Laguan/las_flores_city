# Bruno Salvatierra

> Tags: `#figure` `#npc`
>
> **Role:** Club Promoter's Son

## Overview

A 19-year-old whose father promotes venues in Port. He brings VIP invitations and takes free access to the yacht. He talks loud and laughs along with Kiara at Peter Jr.'s expense.

## Physical Description

Broad-shouldered Latino young man of 19 with warm tan skin, a square jaw, dark brown eyes, dark brown hair swept back with gel, a loose cream silk shirt open at the collar, a thick gold chain and a confident, lazy grin.

## Related Lore

- [Peter van der Meer Jr.](../peter_van_der_meer_jr/peter_van_der_meer_jr.md)

## Background *(all invented unless noted)*

- **Born 2058** (19 in 2077). His father promotes venues in the Port district, so Bruno grew up in clubs, doorways and guest lists; he knows how a room can be bought. *(the father's business is from his description; the rest is invented)*
- **What he brings:** VIP invitations, and in return takes free access to the Van der Meer yacht. He believes in transactions, not friendship.
- **What the dialogues show:** he grins and nods a beat late, "already calculating his own angles"; he laughs along with Kiara at Peter Jr.'s expense (Act 3) and says nothing back when she mocks the boy.

## Want, Flaw, Fear

- **Want:** To become his father's equal in the Port scene: a promoter in his own right, not "the promoter's son".
- **Flaw:** Loyal to whoever is paying, which he treats as a virtue.
- **Fear:** That the yacht is the last room he gets into for free, and that his father's connections run out before his do.

## Habits and Contradictions

- Loose cream silk shirt, a thick gold chain, a lazy confident grin; gelled hair. *(from his description)*
- Loud in company; quiet when his father calls. *(invented)*

## Relationships

- **[Kiara Montenegro](../kiara_montenegro/kiara_montenegro.md).** Partner in the entourage; they split the credit for what the yacht gives them.
- **[Peter Jr.](../peter_van_der_meer_jr/peter_van_der_meer_jr.md).** A generous source of access, and a boy Bruno mostly ignores.
- **His father.** The Port venues he is expected to inherit.
- **[Adrián Beltrán](../adrian_beltran/adrian_beltran.md).** Controls the guest list.
