# Qiu Bo

**Title:** Head of Tech & Innovation, LW Group

**Physical Description:**
- Chinese man in his late 50s with a lean, wiry build and clear skin; square face with pronounced cheekbones, receding straight dark-gray hair, thick brows and a small mole on the left cheek.
- **Identifying detail:** the small mole on his left cheek.
- *Age revised to fit the timeline (see below); existing portrait assets were drawn for age 45 and may need a refresh.*

**Description (full):**

Drives the LW Group's ventures into next-generation lithium extraction and renewable energy technologies, keeping the conglomerate at the industry's forefront. In 2049 he proposed the containment system that might have prevented the Great Lithium Leak; investor Zhang Liang blocked it. After the leak, he executed the cover-up: he oversaw the fabrication of the "electrical failure" evidence.

**Age (2077):** 59 (b. 2018) *(birth year revised from 2032; see Timeline conflict)*
**District:** Las Flores
**Role:** Head of Tech & Innovation

## Timeline conflict (resolved: middle-ground birth year)

The data originally gave **45 in 2077 (b. 2032)**, which made him 17 in 2049 (already technology head) and 20 in 2052 (overseeing fabrication of evidence). Per your decision I moved the birth year to **2018**: he is about 31 when he proposes the containment system, 34 at the leak, and 59 in 2077. That is young for a department head but plausible, and keeps him a generation below Li Wei and Zhang Liang. His physical description is now "late 50s" and the portrait prompt text says 59; the existing portrait images still show a man of about 45 and may be regenerated.

## Want, Flaw, Fear

- **Want:** To be right as an engineer, to see his containment design built. The proposal he made in 2049 was the correct one. *(that he cares about being vindicated is an interpretation)*
- **Flaw:** Pragmatic loyalty. When the budget barrier killed his design, and later when the leak happened, he did not go public; he built the cover-up instead. He makes technology serve whoever holds the capital. *(his state of mind is invented)*
- **Fear:** That the paper trail, the emails about his proposal and the fabricated evidence, reaches the people who could expose him. He is on the list of men whose names the whistleblower chain carries. *(invented framing)*

## Habits and Contradictions

- Drives "next-generation lithium extraction" and renewables: the cleaner tech he sells is the thing his own earlier proposal could have supplied before the leak.
- The contradiction: the man who proposed the fix and the man who covered up its absence are the same person.
