## Overview

Councilwoman Petra Solis is a prominent figure in the reform bloc and a vocal advocate for justice in the Meridian case. She appears to be a champion of the people, but she is secretly part of the corruption network she claims to fight. Solis leverages her public image as a reformer to deflect scrutiny from the network's activities while quietly ensuring that investigations that get too close are redirected or stalled.

## Background

Solis rose through Las Flores politics on a platform of transparency and accountability. Her constituents in the eastern districts trust her because she speaks plainly about the city's problems — corporate overreach, police militarization, the erosion of civil liberties. What the public doesn't see is the careful web of favors and compromises that put her in office. She owes her seat to the same power brokers she criticizes in press conferences, and she repays that debt by controlling the tempo of reform: fast enough to look genuine, slow enough to never threaten the establishment.

## Role in the Meridian Case

When the Meridian scandal broke, Solis positioned herself as the city's conscience, demanding investigations and accountability. Behind closed doors, she funneled information about whistleblowers and witnesses to the network, ensuring that key testimony was delayed or discredited. Her involvement makes her a dangerous adversary — anyone who trusts her based on her public stance is walking into a trap.

## Physical Description

Solis is in her early fifties, with silver-streaked dark hair she keeps styled immaculately. She dresses in tailored suits that project competence and authority. Her expressions are carefully managed — warm and concerned in front of cameras, cold and calculating in private meetings. She has a habit of pausing before she speaks, a rhetorical device that makes every word feel deliberate and weighed.
