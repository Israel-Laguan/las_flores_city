## Overview

Zhang Liang is a long-time **LW Group investor** — not the CFO on the org chart, but the man whose capital and whispered “prudence” could stall a project as effectively as any veto. He helped shape the economic bridge between China and Las Flores, delayed the containment spend that might have softened the Great Lithium Leak, and then did **quiet shadow facilitation** during the cover-up: introductions, payments, advice to keep scandals off ledgers. In 2077 he is seventy-eight: semi-retired, still wealthy, doing **light shadow work advising the young ones** who inherit Li Wei’s machine without fully understanding its debts.

## Background

Chinese capital networks into LW Group bilateral ventures. Zhang’s gift was never public vision — it was knowing which numbers to starve and which juniors to tip. Li Wei held the emperor’s chair; Zhang held pockets of the purse and a reputation for saying *not yet* until the risk looked buryable.

He was never the night-shift operator. He was the investor who made sure operators had budgets — or didn’t.

## Role in the Great Lithium Leak

### Pre-Disaster (2049)
- **Qiu Bo** proposed a high-tech containment system
- **Zhang Liang**, as investor with leverage over spend and procurement politics, imposed **financial barriers / capital delays** that blocked timely approval
- Vault clue: *Email Chain: Zhang Liang Delays Containment Upgrade* — correspondence showing deliberate budget pressure that stalled the upgrade

### Cover-Up (Aug–Sept 2052)
After Li Wei approved the electrical-failure narrative, Zhang’s lane was **quiet and financial**, not theatrical:
- Steering money for muted coverage and cooperative officials
- Advising which witnesses could be bought vs. which problems needed “other hands”
- Keeping his own name off operational cables where possible

Timeline notes that place him on Sept 12 covert beats should read as **facilitation / counsel**, not him personally running wetwork.

### Shared Silence with Li Wei
Zhang and Li Wei share the era’s buried decisions — including pressure around **Cecilia Pérez**. Their bond is mutual knowledge and capital loyalty more than a public title. He did not need a CFO badge to be indispensable.

## Life in 2077 — Investor & Light Shadow Mentor

Officially: semi-retired major investor; appears at dinners, foundation tables, and “informal strategy teas.”
Unofficially: **advises younger LW executives and princelings** — Xiao Chen–era talent, finance juniors, family referrals — on what not to put in writing, which favors still matter, and how to survive scandals that outlive careers.

Tone of his mentoring:
- Soft voice, long silences
- “I invested through worse winters”
- Rarely gives orders; offers options with invisible strings
- Protects the young enough to keep the network alive; never enough to confess

He is lighter than a spymaster and colder than a kindly uncle.

## Personality & Mannerisms

- Speaks in margins, risk, and generational advice
- Personality: `prudent_strategist` → elder investor-mentor
- Compliments ambition while pruning it
- Flaw: still believes starvation of “wasteful” safety spend was wisdom; mentoring can become grooming

## Player Interaction Dynamics

### The Meet
Private club, investor salon, or a “coincidental” introduction through a young LW associate Zhang is mentoring — he prefers rooms he chose.

### Opening Posture
- Cordial elder; calls the leak an old tragedy of markets and weather
- Offers career advice or a soft investment tip before he offers truth
- Treats the player like a promising junior — or a liability

### Softening / Pressure Conditions
- The 2049 email chain proving he starved the upgrade
- Proof he moved cover-up money / advised silencing paths
- Threats to protégés he actually likes
- Rare sincerity if the player frames asks as protecting the next generation from repeating 2052

### Payoff
- Soft intel: who still owes whom; which junior knows which skeleton
- Introductions into LW-adjacent youth / investor circles
- Partial admissions framed as “allocations I would make differently now”
- Optional: he steers a mentee to help the player — or to watch them

### Risk
- He can close doors, redirect ambitious kids against the player, or buy silence without raising his voice

### Gameplay Role
Great Lithium Leak capital node + **mentor-gate** into younger LW stories. Distinct from Zheng Wuhao (guilty facility man) and Li Wei (emperor). Zhang is the polite hand on the purse and the shoulder of the young.
