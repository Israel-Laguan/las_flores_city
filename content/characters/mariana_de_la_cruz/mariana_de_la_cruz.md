# Mariana De La Cruz

**Title:** Bureau Chief, La Prensa (Las Flores)

**Physical Description:**
- Latina woman in her 40s with an athletic-compact, poised build; angular face, high cheekbones, wavy dark chin-length hair and a small mole near her lower lip, dressed in professional attire with press credentials.

**Description (full):**

Oversees La Prensa's operations in Las Flores. Pivotal in building the newspaper's presence and credibility in the city through insightful local coverage.

**Age (2077):** 44 (b. 2033)
**District:** Las Flores
**Role:** Bureau Chief
**Descendancy:** Latin American

**Outlet:** [La Prensa](../../lore/media/press/la_prensa/la_prensa.md), Las Flores bureau.

## Newsroom context

She runs the Las Flores operation and is "pivotal in building the newspaper's presence and credibility in the city." The bureau's problem is the one the outlet file names: the LW Group's influence over local media, through ad revenue, payments and personal connections. Mariana has to keep a bureau alive in a town where most local outlets will not touch the leak. *(her specific choices are invented)*

- **Born 2033** (44 in 2077). She was 19 at the 2052 leak, so the bureau she leads postdates it or she joined it young. *(her path is invented)*
- She is the bureau's local anchor while [Lina Kim](../lina_kim/lina_kim.md), an outsider, makes the headlines and then leaves. *(this framing is invented; the lore has Lina based at the main office by 2077)*

## Want, Flaw, Fear

- **Want:** A bureau that the city treats as its own paper, not as a visiting investigation team.
- **Flaw:** Caution that looks like control. She vets every story that names an LW-linked company and has slowed [Sofia Cortez](../sofia_cortez/sofia_cortez.md)'s pieces to protect the bureau's access. *(invented example)*
- **Fear:** Losing the bureau to a budget decision at the main office, or to a legal threat she could not match. *(invented)*

## Habits and Contradictions

- Professional dress and press credentials always visible; the small mole near her lower lip is her recognizable detail.
- A poised manager who carries the local risk that the main office does not see.
