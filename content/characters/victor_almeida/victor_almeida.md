# Victor Almeida

**Title:** Editor-in-Chief, La Prensa

**Physical Description:**
- A stocky Latino man in his late 50s to mid-60s with a square face, strong jaw, thick brows, weathered skin and thin gray receding hair. He has a faded scar on his right brow and wears veteran editor's attire with a press pass and reading glasses.

**Description (full):**

Veteran journalist with over three decades of experience. Known for uncompromising editorial stance and dedication to truth. Led La Prensa's resurgence in investigative journalism.

**Age (2077):** 62 (b. 2015)
**District:** Las Flores
**Role:** Editor-in-Chief
**Descendancy:** Latin American

**Outlet:** [La Prensa](../../lore/media/press/la_prensa/la_prensa.md), main office. The data lists his district as Las Flores, but the main office is "outside Las Flores", so he is based there.

---

## Newsroom context

La Prensa's independence rests on keeping advertising and editorial apart, and in the lithium years the LW Group leaned on local media through ad revenue, direct payments and personal connections. Victor is the person who has to say no. *(everything below about specific decisions is invented)*

- **Started ~2045** (age 30), and by 2077 has "over three decades" in the trade.
- **The 2052 call.** When [Lina Kim](../lina_kim/lina_kim.md) came back from Las Flores in September 2052 with evidence of a cover-up, Victor ran the series. LW-linked advertisers pulled out and he held the line. *(invented)*
- **Resurgence.** Under him the paper returns to investigations: [Alejandro Ruiz](../alejandro_ruiz/alejandro_ruiz.md)'s corporate exposés and [Laura Silva](../laura_silva/laura_silva.md)'s political-finance work.

## Want, Flaw, Fear

- **Want:** A paper that cannot be bought, and a staff that outlives him. *(invented motive)*
- **Flaw:** Print-era rigidity. He will hold a story for another source and another lawyer's read while a story goes cold, which is the opposite of [Soledad Ortega](../soledad_ortega/soledad_ortega.md)'s publish-and-update rhythm. *(invented example)*
- **Fear:** That a financial squeeze, rather than a threat, is what ends the paper's independence: he can resist a bribe and cannot make payroll on principle. *(invented)*

## Habits and Contradictions

- Wears his press pass in the office and reads copy with reading glasses pushed low.
- The contradiction: an uncompromising editor who depends on a younger digital team to keep the paper solvent.
- The faded scar on his right brow is from an old story he does not discuss. *(invented origin)*

## Relationships (with cost)

- **[Lina Kim](../lina_kim/lina_kim.md), star investigator.** He backed her and paid for it in ad revenue *(invented)*; she is the paper's reputation.
- **[Soledad Ortega](../soledad_ortega/soledad_ortega.md), managing editor.** Her multimedia push saves the paper's finances and pulls it away from his model of slow, rigorous print. *(invented tension)*
- **[Alejandro Ruiz](../alejandro_ruiz/alejandro_ruiz.md).** Victor's most reliable hard-news investigator. *(invented; the lore names him Senior Investigative Reporter)*
- **[Mariana De La Cruz](../mariana_de_la_cruz/mariana_de_la_cruz.md),** his Las Flores bureau chief, who carries the LW pressure day to day. *(invented reporting line)*

## Story Role

The paper's conscience: a reliable-information NPC and the person who approves or kills a lead the player brings. He is **not** part of the 2077 Alex/Evelyn main plot.
