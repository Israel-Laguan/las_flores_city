## Overview

Yī Sāng is Li Wei’s ruthless **Chief of Operations** and **Head of Corporate Security** — the ground fixer who prefers a café table and a wall of monitors to an alley. He coldly oversees black operations and public-relations crises from a safe distance, then lets specialists like **Viktor Reyes (“the Doctor”)** finish the wet geometry. In 2077 he is fifty: gray at the temples, scar on the jaw, still the man who decides which problems get a medic who never saves anyone.

## Background

Chinese–Korean heritage inside LW’s security pipeline. He came up in the post-leak years when the conglomerate needed people who could manage protest optics and disappearing evidence without putting Li Wei in the frame. He learned early: the cleanest kill is the one that looks like emergency care; the cleanest riot is the one that looks like someone else’s fault.

He is not Zhang Liang (capital and counsel). He is not Zheng Wuhao (facility guilt). He is the **operations middle distance** — close enough to aim, far enough to deny.

## Known Operations

### Liu Fang / alley protocol (2055)
As Viktor Reyes’s **handler**, Yi Sang owned the corporate side of intercepting whistleblower **Liu Fang** before trial. Viktor played doctor in the alley; Yi Sang owned the tasking, extraction of evidence priorities, and aftermath hygiene.

### The Student’s Flight (late 2050s)
Seated at **Café de las Mañanas** on Bulevar Progreso with mining agents (including negotiator **Nadia Al-Farsi**), Yi Sang oversaw the Doctor’s assassination op from a safe distance while managing the PR crisis of a massive anti-mining protest in Parque de los Pioneros. Classic Yi Sang: coffee, sightlines, deniability.

### Ongoing (2060s–2077)
- Corporate security deployments against activists and leaks
- Crisis PR coordination when black ops brush public air
- Mentoring/controlling field assets; cutting them loose when they become liabilities
- Remaining useful to Li Wei’s shadow and to whoever sits the CEO chair (Xiao Chen era) as the man who still knows where the bodies’ paperwork lives

## Personality & Mannerisms

- Personality: `ruthless_calculating`
- Speaks rarely; when he does, short operational sentences
- Humor is a thin cold smirk, never a laugh
- Treats people as risk ratings
- Flaw for writers: the rare crack is not remorse — it is **loss of control** (comms failure, an asset gone loud, a player who won’t fit a box). The `__afraid` beat is guarded unease at chaos, not conscience

## Player Interaction Dynamics

### The Meet
Ops-adjacent spaces: café with sightlines, security anteroom, tinted vehicle, protest perimeter “observer” post. He arrives before you notice the watchers.

### Opening Posture
- Polite vacancy
- Asks what you want as if filing a ticket
- Offers nothing; measures whether you are asset, obstacle, or noise

### Softening / Pressure Conditions
- Proof tying him to Viktor Reyes tasking / Liu Fang / Student’s Flight café overwatch
- Leverage over an asset he still needs
- A crisis he cannot solve with distance — then he may deal, never confess

### Payoff
- Controlled intel (a name, a route, a stand-down)
- Temporary ceasefire
- Or a redirected threat meant to look like chance

### Risk
- He can greenlight surveillance, frame-ups, or another “Doctor” visit
- Underestimating his distance is how people die helped by a medic

### Gameplay Role
Primary LW **security antagonist / handler boss** for investigation and flashback threads. Gate to Viktor Reyes. Foil to Adeyemi / Evelyn / street resistance who fight what he schedules.
