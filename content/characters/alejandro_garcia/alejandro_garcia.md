# Alejandro Garcia

**Title:** Flower Farmer, Construction Worker

**Description (full):**
Alejandro Garcia is the silent patriarch of a family shattered by corporate negligence. A widower who buried his wife Maria to lithium poisoning in January 2071, he tends the Garcia family's poisoned flower fields by the Río de las Flores until the soil itself betrayed him. After her death, grief stole his voice and the fields could no longer support the family. He left for construction work in the city, trading the slow poison of contaminated earth for the honest exhaustion of manual labor. He is the unspoken foundation upon which his children's lives—Isabella's art, Sofia's survival, and Alex's revolution—were built.

**Born:** ~2008 (age ~44 at the 2052 Great Lithium Leak; ~69 in 2077)
**District:** Old Las Flores (near the Río de las Flores)
**Role:** Flower Farmer, then Construction Worker
**Descendancy:** Latin American

## Physical Description

Latino man of about 69 with a stocky, sturdy build and heavy, slightly stooped shoulders earned from decades of labor. Sun-damaged leathery brown skin bears deep lines across the forehead and around the eyes—testament to years under the relentless sun. Thick curly hair gone white-grey spills from beneath a sweat-stained cap pushed back on his head. Hooded dark eyes, square face, wide nose, pronounced cheekbones, and short grey stubble. Calloused hands with faint grey-green chemical stains from years of working the poisoned soil. Faded work shirt with sleeves rolled to the elbows.

## Overview

Alejandro Garcia is the quiet heart of the Garcia family tragedy. Where his wife Maria was the first to notice the flowers turning red in September 2052, Alejandro was the one who kept working the poisoned land long after it was clear something was wrong. After Maria's death in January 2071, something in him broke. He went silent, the flower fields failed, and he retreated to construction work—anything to keep food on the table for Isabella, Sofia, and Alex. He is not a man of grand speeches; his love is expressed through stubbed toes, calloused hands, and the stubborn insistence that his children have opportunities he never did.

## Background

- **Early life:** Born into a flower-farming family in Old Las Flores, near the Río de las Flores. Worked the fields from childhood, learning the rhythm of soil and seasons.
- **Marriage to Maria:** United with a woman who shared his love for the land. Together they built a life and a family—Isabella (~2043), Sofia (~2045), and Alex (~2055).
- **The Great Lithium Leak (August 2052):** The disaster that would define his family. By September, the family's flowers turned deep red. Maria saw it first; Alejandro kept working, unable or unwilling to admit defeat.
- **Maria's illness:** She began coughing, fatigued, short of breath. Doctors couldn't find the cause. The medicines did little. Maria kept hauling contaminated water to the dying fields, hoping it would revive them.
- **The river incident:** On a September afternoon, Maria took Isabella and Sofia to the river. Isabella fell in. Maria went in after her. The contaminated water took the last of Maria's strength. Isabella survived with chronic respiratory problems; Maria never fully recovered.
- **January 2071:** Maria died quietly, "after nearly two decades of declining health." Alex was sixteen. The last red flowers wilted that day. Alejandro went silent.
- **After the loss:** The flower fields could no longer support the family. He took construction work in the city. The man who once spoke to his flowers now spoke to no one, not even his children.
- **Pushing Alex forward:** Despite his silence, he urged his youngest to apply for the Futuro Brillante scholarship. He could not say he was proud, but his actions—applying on Alejandro's urging, winning it, enrolling at university—spoke for him.

## Want, Flaw, Fear

- **Want:** To keep his family together. To give his children a life better than the one that killed their mother. To make the grief—his and theirs—mean something.
- **Flaw:** Silence as armor. He retreats into work and quiet, believing that providing for his children is love enough. He doesn't realize they need his voice, not just his labor.
- **Fear:** That he has already failed. That Isabella's illness, Sofia's homelessness, and Alex's eventual death are all consequences of his inability to protect them from the poison that took Maria.

## Habits and Contradictions

- **The cap:** Always on his head, sweat-stained and familiar. Pushed back when working, pulled low when lost in thought.
- **Hands that tell stories:** Calloused, stained, always moving. He expresses himself through action, not words.
- **Early riser:** Wakes before dawn, makes coffee, leaves for work before the others stir. Comes home, makes more coffee, sits in silence.
- **Contradiction:** A man who tends poisoned soil for years out of stubbornness, yet cannot bear to look at the river that took his wife.
- **The scholarship push:** Urged Alex to apply for Futuro Brillante—the GLC scholarship for Old Las Flores students. A rare moment of direct guidance from a man who usually speaks through silence.

## Story Role

Alejandro is the central figure of the [Garcia Family Story](../../lore/stories/garcia_family/garcia_family.md). His journey from flower farmer to grieving widower to silent construction worker mirrors the trajectory of Old Las Flores itself: once fertile and full of promise, now poisoned and struggling to survive.

- His wife's illness and death from lithium contamination provide the human face of the Great Lithium Leak.
- His children represent three different responses to trauma: Isabella's artistic isolation, Sofia's resilient survival, and Alex's revolutionary action.
- His home in Old Las Flores serves as a base for Isabella and Ariana, a place where the family's ghosts and hopes coexist.
- He is the reason Alex could pursue education and ultimately lead the 2077 movement—though he lived to see his youngest die in that same struggle.
