# Alejandro Garcia

**Title:** Flower Farmer, Construction Worker

**Physical Description:**
- Latino man of about 69 (2077) with a stocky build, sun-damaged brown skin with deep lines, thick curly hair gone white-grey and a square face with hooded dark eyes; worn work clothes with sleeves rolled, a sweat-stained cap and callused hands with faint chemical stains. In flashbacks (~2052) he is in his early 40s with dark curly hair graying only at the temples.

**Description (full):**

A hardworking father who lost his wife to lithium contamination. He tends the Garcia family's poisoned flower fields, then takes construction jobs to support his three children.

**Born:** ~2008 (age ~44 at the 2052 events; ~69 in 2077 if alive)
**District:** Las Flores
**Role:** Flower Farmer
**Descendancy:** Latin American
