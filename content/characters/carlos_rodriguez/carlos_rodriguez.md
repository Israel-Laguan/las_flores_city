# Carlos Rodriguez

**Title:** Progressive Alliance Leader and City Council Member

**Physical Description:**
- Latino man of 48 with an athletic-compact build, wavy dark hair graying at the temples, almond dark eyes, thick brows and a faint old scar on the chin; wears a well-fitted suit with an open collar and a city pin.

**Description (full):**

Carlos Rodriguez is a 48-year-old Progressive Alliance leader and city council member. A founding member of the Alliance, he is a career politician from a working-class Central City background who entered politics after the 2058 industrial riots and has served on the council since. *(his earlier mayoral role was removed to fit the endgame lore: the mayor in 2077 is Alfonso Vega)* His partnership with [Ana Silva](../ana_silva/ana_silva.md), the coalition's environmental policy architect, anchors his agenda: together they co-authored three contamination oversight bills, and two passed in weakened form under LW Group lobbying pressure.

His contested position defines him: a reformist legislator working in a city whose largest employer is LW Group and whose mayor, [Alfonso Vega](../alfonso_vega/alfonso_vega.md), serves the LW network. His statements invoke the 2052 Great Lithium Leak and its health impacts; his environmental legislation contains enforcement provisions analysts call toothless. He is pragmatic: he identifies the achievable within his constraints, achieves it, and calls it progress.

**Age (2077):** 48 (b. 2029)
**District:** Las Flores (resides in Central City)
**Role:** City Council Member; Progressive Alliance leader
**Descendancy:** Latin American

> "I legislate for the city that exists, not the city that should have been built. My job is to make them closer to the same thing, one bill at a time."

- **Want:** Working-class representation, transit equity and contamination legislation. His motivation as written: "environmental contamination legislation, transit equity, working-class constituency representation, political survival within structural constraints."
- **Flaw:** Compromise as a habit. Two of the three oversight bills passed in weakened form, and he calls them progress. The enforcement provisions are "toothless".
