# Pedro Escobar

**Title:** City Editor, La Prensa (Las Flores)

**Physical Description:**
- Latino man in his early 40s with a stocky, solid build, wavy dark hair threaded with gray, a slightly crooked nose and a small mole on his cheek, in smart-casual attire with a press lanyard.

**Description (full):**

City Editor for La Prensa's Las Flores bureau. Coordinates coverage of local news, from politics to cultural events, ensuring the newspaper reflects the city's diverse fabric.

**Age (2077):** 42 (b. 2035)
**District:** Las Flores
**Role:** City Editor
**Descendancy:** Latin American

**Outlet:** [La Prensa](../../lore/media/press/la_prensa/la_prensa.md), Las Flores bureau.

## Newsroom context

The city editor "coordinates coverage of local news, from politics to cultural events, ensuring La Prensa reflects the diverse fabric of the city." He is the desk where a tip becomes an assignment. *(everything beyond the role is invented)*

- **Born 2035** (42 in 2077); he was 17 at the 2052 leak.
- Keeps [Emilio Navarro](../emilio_navarro/emilio_navarro.md)'s cultural stories and [Sofia Cortez](../sofia_cortez/sofia_cortez.md)'s environmental reports in the same paper, an uneasy fit.

## Want, Flaw, Fear

- **Want:** A paper that covers every neighborhood, not only the stories that win awards.
- **Flaw:** Avoids confrontation. He passes hard calls up to [Mariana De La Cruz](../mariana_de_la_cruz/mariana_de_la_cruz.md) and has let a story drift because nobody wanted to decide. *(invented)*
- **Fear:** Missing the story that matters because it arrived in the wrong format or the wrong neighborhood. *(invented)*

## Habits and Contradictions

- Press lanyard, smart-casual dress; a slightly crooked nose and a small mole on his cheek.
- Calm in a crisis and indecisive in a quiet week. *(invented)*
