# Javier Mendoza

> **Role:** Farmer (Agricultural Valley), Former Safety Inspector (Minera Estrella)
> **Status:** Active

## Overview
Javier Mendoza is a resilient 47-year-old farm owner in the Agricultural Valley with a dark past. Standing at 5'10" with tanned, weathered skin and a strong build from years of outdoor work, he currently presents himself as a hardworking farmer battling corporate consolidation and gang violence. However, Javier's history is tied directly to one of the city's greatest tragedies.

## The Minera Estrella Payout
Javier was formerly a safety inspector at Minera Estrella. During the fallout of the Great Lithium Leak, he initially agreed to testify about the company's negligence and safety violations. However, just before the trial, he suddenly retracted his statement and refused to appear in court.

It is an open secret that he was offered a substantial payoff to remain silent. The guilt of this decision weighed heavily on him, prompting him to use the "blood money" to buy land in the Agricultural Valley, hoping to escape his past and start anew as a farmer.

## Life in the Agricultural Valley
Javier believes in hard work and perseverance, seeking redemption through honest labor. Facing challenges from automated mega-farms dominating the market, he adapted after a corporate partnership scam by selling his produce directly to restaurants and hotels in the city, seeking better prices and trustworthy relationships.

Despite tolls and road insecurity from roving gangs, Javier remains undeterred. His determination borders on stubbornness—he struggles to ask for help and often bears burdens alone, viewing his current struggles as karma for his past sins. Yet his strong sense of responsibility to his employees and community drives him forward. He dreams of expanding operations and showcasing the Valley's produce, hoping to one day feel he has truly earned his peace.

## Family
Javier is the son of **[Roberto Mendoza](../roberto_mendoza/roberto_mendoza.md)**, the Minera Estrella process and piping engineer, and of a foreign-born mother who built the Agricultural Valley farm from scratch; his uncle is **[Carlos Mendoza](../carlos_mendoza/carlos_mendoza.md)**, the structural engineer who kept the original calculations. Roberto steered him into the safety-inspector job, and the family's silence after the 2052 leak is tied to his retraction: his father stayed quiet for twenty-five years partly to protect him, and his uncle never forgave the payoff (invented family links, consistent with the Roberto and Carlos files). In 2077 both men come forward to the investigators.
