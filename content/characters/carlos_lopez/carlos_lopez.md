# Carlos Lopez

**Title:** Community Verifier, Humanity First

**Physical Description:**
- Eyes: deep brown, wide-set, calm and appraising
- Build: stocky, broad-shouldered
- Skin: warm olive-brown, weathered, sun-damaged texture
- Hair: thick, wavy, dark with gray streaks at the temples
- Face: long face shape, prominent jaw, wide flat-bridged nose, thick slightly asymmetric brows, slight asymmetry to the lower lip
- Clothing: worn practical collared shirt, sleeves rolled, weathered leather work-belt
- Accessories: small sport non-in-ear earbud clipped to earlobe

**Description (full):**

A small business owner who serves as a vital verifier for Humanity First's projects. His practical skepticism and keen eye are essential for ensuring funds are used transparently.

**Age (2077):** ~73 (b. ~2004; revised from 2032; the lore has him as a business owner in 2035)
**District:** Las Flores
**Role:** Community Verifier
**Descendancy:** Latin American

**Movement:** [Humanity First](../../lore/organizations/movements/humanity_first/overview/overview.md), Community Verifier

---

## Background

- **A small business owner**, "skeptical but intrigued" when Daniel recruits him (June 2035). *(his business is invented: given his tool belt, a hardware and repair shop that supplies the tool-sharing program)*
- **September 2036:** Mateo Rodriguez tries to raise money for a fictitious youth program. Carlos visits and finds nothing, and Mateo confesses; stricter verification follows. He is the movement's anti-fraud reflex ([Founding Era](../../lore/organizations/movements/humanity_first/timeline_founding/timeline_founding.md)).
- **2041:** the Community Oversight Committee, "respected locals verify project progress", is founded after the Diego Salazar and Carla Ruiz scandal. He is its natural model. *(that he sits on it is an assumption)*
- **2077:** about 73, still verifying. *(revised birth year)*

## Want, Flaw, Fear

- **Want:** Money that is spent where it was promised, and an organization whose books survive a hostile read.
- **Flaw:** Suspicion that becomes policy. He can be harsh on honest volunteers whose paperwork is sloppy. *(invented)*
- **Fear:** Missing the one fraud that matters: Diego Salazar and Carla Ruiz fooled the council with staged cleanups, and Tomas Herrera's scandal came in 2057–2058 anyway ([Rogue Incidents](../../lore/organizations/movements/humanity_first/rogue_incidents/rogue_incidents.md)). *(invented framing; the incidents are the lore's)*

## Habits and Contradictions

## Story Role

A verifier NPC: someone who can confirm or expose whether a project or donation is real. He is **not** part of the 2077 main plot.
