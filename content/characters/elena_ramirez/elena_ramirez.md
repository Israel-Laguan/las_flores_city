# Elena Ramirez

**Title:** Former Chair, Humanity First

**Physical Description:**
- Latina woman in her 60s with a soft, rounded build, weathered warm-brown skin and wavy gray-streaked dark hair in a silver bun; warm almond eyes, a faint burn scar on her right hand, modest professional attire with reading glasses on a lanyard and a Humanity First pin.

**Description (full):**

A former local teacher passionate about education reform who became the first locally elected Chair of the Humanity First council, guiding it through its crucial growth era.

**Age (2077):** 65 (b. 2012)
**District:** Las Flores
**Role:** Former Chair
**Descendancy:** Latin American

**Movement:** [Humanity First](../../lore/organizations/movements/humanity_first/overview/overview.md), first Chair (2036–2057)

---

## Background

- **A local teacher** passionate about education reform; one of Daniel's three first allies (June 2035).
- **January 2036:** appointed first Chair of the first Community Council. She is formally elected in August 2039, and the overview credits her with the Chair from 2039 to 2057 (see inconsistencies).
- **September 2036:** the Mateo Rodriguez fundraising fraud is caught by [Carlos Lopez](../carlos_lopez/carlos_lopez.md); stricter verification follows. **2037:** the council refuses bribes for community-center permits and wins through public pressure; in October she organizes digital-literacy, sustainable-living and leadership workshops whose graduates join the council. **January 2039:** the Community Center opens, with a library and classrooms.
- **June 2039:** she and Javier notice inconsistencies in Diego Salazar and Carla Ruiz's reports, which leads to their expulsion in December 2040.
- **November 2057:** hands the Chair to [Sofia Alvarez](../sofia_alvarez/sofia_alvarez.md) in the "generational shift", at 45.
- **2077:** a respected elder of 65, "Former Chair".

Timeline sources: [Founding Era](../../lore/organizations/movements/humanity_first/timeline_founding/timeline_founding.md), [Growth Era](../../lore/organizations/movements/humanity_first/timeline_growth/timeline_growth.md), [Influence Era](../../lore/organizations/movements/humanity_first/timeline_influence/timeline_influence.md).

## Want, Flaw, Fear

- **Want:** Education and civic learning as the movement's core: workshops that turn graduates into volunteers and, in time, leaders.
- **Flaw:** Believes in people, which makes her slow to see manipulators. Several rogue incidents (Diego Salazar and Carla Ruiz in 2038–2040, the Prosperidad Collective in 2041–2043) grew while she was Chair. She noticed, but only after the funds were gone. *(her self-blame is invented)*
- **Fear:** That the movement becomes a platform for people who want influence rather than a community; each scandal feeds the skeptics. *(invented)*

## Habits and Contradictions

- Reading glasses on a lanyard and a silver bun; a faint burn scar on her right hand, from a classroom or kitchen accident. *(origin invented)*
- A teacher who runs a council as a classroom: milestones, lessons, review. *(invented)*
- A founding figure who gave up the Chair at 45 and still shapes how the movement remembers its past.

## Relationships (with cost)

- **[Daniel Carter](../daniel_carter/daniel_carter.md).** Her partner in founding, who left her the council.
- **[Javier Torres](../javier_torres/javier_torres.md).** Vice-Chair beside her for nearly two decades.
- **[Sofia Alvarez](../sofia_alvarez/sofia_alvarez.md).** Successor; Sofia led the Youth Council under her.
- **[Carlos Lopez](../carlos_lopez/carlos_lopez.md).** Her verifier.

## Story Role

An elder NPC: the movement's memory and a source on its history, especially the rogue incidents. She is **not** part of the 2077 main plot.

## Known inconsistencies

- First Chair appointed January 2036 vs the overview's "2039–2057"; both appear in the lore.
- In February 2052 the lore calls Sofia Alvarez "Chair", though Sofia is elected in November 2057; Elena is the Chair on every other count.
