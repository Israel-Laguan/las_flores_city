# Lucas de Jong

**Title:** Las Flores Resident

**Physical Description:**
- Hair: Glossy black, stylishly cut and swept up and back
- Eyes: Piercing blue, under thick dark brows
- Build: Athletic, kept up with regular gym workouts
- Skin: Fair, with light stubble
- Distinguishing features: Angular face with a strong jaw; polished, fashionable dress (designer shirts, skinny jeans, elegant shoes)

**Description (full):**

Lucas de Jong is a 24-year-old Dutch man, raised in a well-to-do family that relocated to Las Flores for opportunities in the burgeoning lithium industry. He has a sleek, polished look: an angular face with a strong jaw, piercing blue eyes under thick dark brows, fair skin and glossy black hair swept up and back. He pays careful attention to his appearance, dressing in fashionable designer shirts, skinny jeans and elegant shoes, and keeps an athletic build through regular workouts at the gym.

**Age (2077):** 24 (b. 2053)
**District:** Las Flores
**Role:** Las Flores Resident
**Descendancy:** Dutch

## Context and Allegiances

**Allegiance:** to the Dutch business community and its influence. His faction is now `dutch_interest`, not `media`.

- **Born 2053** (24 in 2077). His family relocated for the lithium boom; the [Dutch community](../../lore/communities/dutch_community/dutch_community.md) in Las Flores is small but influential and centered on Voss & Co. (GLC's Van der Meer Mining operations), Neptune's Haven B.V. and the Van der Meer family's interests. *(that his father is an executive or engineer at Voss & Co. is invented)*
- **Social orbit.** The community's younger generation moves in the same circles as the Van der Meer cousins ([Sebastian](../sebastian_van_der_meer/sebastian_van_der_meer.md) and [Natalia](../natalia_van_der_meer/natalia_van_der_meer.md)), whose Punta del Este villa party leaked. Lucas could have been a guest. *(invented hook; not stated)*
- **How he lives.** He "carefully maintains an affluent lifestyle": designer shirts, gym workouts, an image he curates. The "cunning manipulator" label means he trades on connections and favors rather than a job.
- **Twin brother.** He is the fraternal twin of [Levi de Jong](../levi_de_jong/levi_de_jong.md): same birth year, same family, same move to Las Flores as teenagers, but different people. Lucas is the one who stays inside the family's world and wants a seat in the Dutch establishment; Levi left it for nightlife, influence and risky private deals. The two are easy to confuse at a distance (Lucas has glossy black hair and stubble and is the heavier-set of the two; Levi has dark brown hair, is clean-shaven and lean). *(the twin link and the contrast are invented)*
- **Name note:** the surname De Jong is common and unrelated to the Van der Meers; he is not [Lucas van der Meer](../lucas_van_der_meer/lucas_van_der_meer.md), the family's troubled youngest son, nor Lucas Morales.

## Want, Flaw, Fear *(all invented)*

- **Want:** A place at the center of Dutch society in Las Flores, such as a board seat or marriage into a family like the Van der Meers.
- **Flaw:** Manipulates by flattery and secrets; he cannot tell when he is being played in turn.
- **Fear:** His family's money or standing shrinking as the Dutch community's footprint declines from its peak.
