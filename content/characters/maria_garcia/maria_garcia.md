# Maria Garcia

**Title:** Flower Farmer

**Physical Description:**
- Age depicted: mid-forties, in the years before her lithium-related illness; she died in January 2071 at about 59 (no 2077 version)
- Build: Soft and rounded, with an oval face and grey-brown eyes under strong dark brows
- Hair: Curly black, falling to the shoulders
- Skin: Weathered and sun-damaged, freckled and blotchy across the cheeks and forehead, with fine lines at the eyes; weathered hands
- Clothing: Simple flower-farming clothes (beige collared work shirt, apron, sun hat)
- **Identifying detail:** green-brown soil worked permanently into the creases of her knuckles, even after scrubbing. *(invented)*

**Description (full):**

A loving mother who tended the Garcia family's flower fields beside the Río de las Flores. She was the first in the family to notice the flowers turning red, in September 2052, and the first to fall ill. She died in January 2071 from complications of lithium contamination, after years of declining health that the river incident in which she rescued her daughter Isabella made worse. She is the mother of Isabella, Sofia and Alex Garcia, and her death is the grief at the origin of Alex's 2077 movement.

**Born:** ~2012 (deceased; ~59 at death in January 2071, after years of lithium-related illness that began in 2052)
**District:** Las Flores (Old Las Flores, near the Río de las Flores)
**Role:** Flower Farmer
**Descendancy:** Latin American

## Want, Flaw, Fear

- **Want:** To keep the flowers, and with them the family, alive. After the river she keeps hauling water from the same river to the same dying fields, "hoping it would revive the flowers."
- **Flaw:** Stubbornness that looks like strength. She refuses to stop working while visibly declining, and pours contaminated water on the plants that are making the family sick. The loyalty that makes her admirable also keeps her in the poison.
- **Fear:** Losing the income and the unity the fields represent; for the family the flowers are livelihood and symbol of warmth. She cannot afford to stop selling even when the blooms turn red, and she cannot admit that she has no way to know what is wrong. *(the framing of this as fear of admitting ignorance is an interpretation)*

## Habits and Contradictions

- Whispers to the dying flowers: "Just hold on a little longer." The line is addressed as much to herself.
- Sells flowers she is afraid of, because the family needs the money.
- Cannot swim, or at least cannot swim well against the current, yet jumps into the river without hesitation. *(invented)*
- Gives her children warmth in a house under strain, and says nothing about her own pain. *(invented)*

## Relationships (with cost)

- **[Isabella Garcia](../isabella_garcia/isabella_garcia.md), eldest daughter.** The river rescue saved Isabella's life and cost Maria hers. Isabella lives with chronic respiratory illness, and the rescue is the root of her self-pity and sharpness.
- **[Sofia Garcia](../sofia_garcia/sofia_garcia.md), middle daughter.** Born deaf; was at the river that day. Sofia grows into the family's most resilient member.
- **[Alex Garcia](../alex_garcia/alex_garcia.md), youngest child.** Alex was born after the first signs of contamination and remembers her whispering to the flowers. Her death turns Alex's grief into the 2077 movement.
- **[Alejandro Garcia](../alejandro_garcia/alejandro_garcia.md), husband.** After her death he goes silent, and the fields can no longer support them; he leaves for construction work.

## Story Role

Maria is the human face of the Great Lithium Leak in the [Garcia family story](../../lore/stories/garcia_family/garcia_family.md). She does not appear in 2077, so she works as a backstory figure and a memory. Her illness and death can supply hooks for clue content: old medical visits, the red flowers, and the river. She is not part of the main plot beyond that.
