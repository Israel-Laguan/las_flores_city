# Alisha Morales

**Title:** 26-year-old Afro-Venezuelan professional

**Physical Description:**
- Hair: Long curly hair that she often styles elegantly

**Description (full):**

Alisha is of Venezuelan descent, born to parents of Afro-Venezuelan heritage. Raised in a modest household in the mountains, she learned the values of hard work and resilience from her family. Currently, Alisha lives and works in a wealthy Chinese neighborhood in Las Flores, where she faces the challenges of being overworked and underappreciated in her job. Alisha has a striking appearance, with long curly hair that she often styles elegantly.

**Age (2077):** 26
**District:** Chinese Enclave
**Role:** Professional

## Want, Flaw, Fear *(all invented)*

- **Want:** Recognition, a promotion that matches her workload, and eventually a firm of her own.
- **Flaw:** She absorbs overwork rather than refusing it, treating it as the price of belonging.
- **Fear:** Being a replaceable cog in a firm that would drop her quietly, and failing the family whose "hard work and resilience" she grew up on.
