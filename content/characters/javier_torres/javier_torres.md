# Javier Torres

**Title:** Vice-Chair, Humanity First

**Physical Description:**
- Stocky Latino man in his early 40s with sun-damaged warm-brown skin, short curly dark hair with gray streaks, a round face with strong jaw, hooded dark eyes, thick brows, and a faded scar on his right forearm; wears a Humanity First vest and work boots.

**Description (full):**

A dedicated environmental activist and Vice-Chair of the Humanity First council. He played a crucial role in responding to the Great Lithium Leakage and organizing cleanup campaigns.
