# Jan van der Meer Jr.

**Title:** Van der Meer Family Member

**Physical Description:**
- Dutch man in his early 40s with an athletic-compact build, neat straight blonde hair with faint gray at the temples, calm wide-set eyes, a strong jaw and a small scar near one ear; dresses in understated soft-collared shirt and light knit.
- **Identifying detail:** the understated soft-collared shirt and light knit, in contrast to [Liam's](../liam_van_der_meer/liam_van_der_meer.md) executive suits, and a habit of saying little. *(the contrast is an editorial choice)*

**Description (full):**

Cousin and husband of [Peter van der Meer](../peter_van_der_meer/peter_van_der_meer.md). One of his staunchest allies during his coming out in 2065; his support drove a wedge with other family members. He is the son of a Dutch cousin of the late Jan van der Meer, from Amsterdam.

**Age (2077):** 42 (b. 2035)
**District:** Las Flores
**Role:** Van der Meer Family Member
**Descendancy:** Dutch

---

## Background

- **From Amsterdam**, the son of a cousin of the elder Jan van der Meer. His sister is Anna-Louise, who heads public relations and community outreach for Van der Meer Industries. *(his father is unnamed in the files)*
- **Brought "fresh insights from Europe"** to the family business, and with Peter oversaw the development of a new state-of-the-art refinery.
- **Meets Peter at a charity gala** after Peter's 2065 coming out; a whirlwind romance ends in a lavish wedding that serves as "a public affirmation of acceptance." *(the wedding date is not stated; it is after 2065)*
- He is a step-father figure to [Peter Jr.](../peter_van_der_meer_jr/peter_van_der_meer_jr.md).

## Want, Flaw, Fear

- **Want:** A husband and a family that can stand in public. His whole role in the story is that he chose Peter openly. *(inferred)*
- **Flaw:** Loyalty that costs him the rest of the family: he accepts friction rather than distance himself from Peter. *(partly invented; the rift is in the lore)*
- **Fear:** That Peter Jr. grows up in the shadow of Rafaela's 2070 tell-all and the family's rift. *(invented)*

## Habits and Contradictions

- Dresses down in a family of designer suits and speaks quietly in meetings. *(invented)*
- The contradiction: a European outsider who is the family's most steady member.

A supporting family figure in the [LGBTQ+ legacy story](../../lore/stories/van_der_meer_lgbtq_legacy/van_der_meer_lgbtq_legacy.md). He is not part of the main 2077 plot.

## Known inconsistencies

- **Jan Jr. vs "Han Jr.":** ruled as two different people. Anna's husband is Han Jr. (son of a Dutch cousin who moved to Las Flores, married c. 2072); Peter's husband is Jan Jr. (from Amsterdam, sister Anna-Louise). Never merge them.
- **"Jr." naming:** he is "Jr." of a cousin, not of Peter's father Jan (b. 2019).
- His build, hair and ear scar match Liam's; see Liam's file.
