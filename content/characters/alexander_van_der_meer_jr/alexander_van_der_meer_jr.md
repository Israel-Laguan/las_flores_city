# Alexander van der Meer Jr.

**Title:** European Businessman, Van der Meer Family Cousin

**Physical Description:**
- Dutch man in his mid-70s with a soft, rounded, prosperous build, thin white hair receding at the temples, thick white-gray brows and deep-set eyes; weathered fair skin with age spots, a well-tailored European suit with a pocket square and a signet ring.
- **Identifying detail:** the signet ring, which he turns when he is deciding something. *(the habit is invented)*

**Description (full):**

Alexander van der Meer Jr. is the son of Pieter van der Meer's brother Hendrik, making him first cousin to Elisabeth, Alexander, and Jan. Born and raised in Europe, he maintained close ties with the Las Flores branch of the family. In his youth, he had a brief romantic relationship with his cousin Elisabeth before she married Carlos Alberto Hernandez — a connection that led to confusion in later family records. Alexander Jr. built a successful import-export business in Rotterdam, occasionally visiting Las Flores for family events and business partnerships with GLC. He is the father of Nico van der Meer, and of Sebastian, CEO of Van der Meer Industries.

**Age (2077):** 75 (b. 2002)
**District:** Las Flores (visits only; based in Rotterdam)
**Role:** European Businessman
**Descendancy:** Dutch

## Name warning

He is **not** [Alexander van der Meer](../alexander_van_der_meer/alexander_van_der_meer.md) (b. 1992), Pieter's eldest son and the father of Xiu Li, Dong and Liam. The two are first cousins; "Jr." does not mean he is Alexander's son.

## Want, Flaw, Fear

- **Want:** A lasting European-South American business bridge for his sons, built on his Rotterdam import-export firm and GLC partnerships. *(inferred)*
- **Flaw:** Sentiment about the family: he kept ties to Elisabeth after their romance, and treats family business as family affection. *(invented)*
- **Fear:** That the branch he represents is seen as the poor European relation, and that the confusion in "later family records" erases him. *(the fear is invented)*

## Habits and Contradictions

- Visits Las Flores for family events, not for business alone. He is cosmopolitan and composed.
- The contradiction: a polished merchant whose most notorious chapter is a youthful romance with his own cousin.
