# Ana Villanueva

> Tags: `#figure` `#npc` `#student` `#undercover`

## Overview

**Born:** ~2055
**Age (2077):** 22
**Role:** University Student & Undercover Operative
**Status:** Alive

## Physical Description
- Hair: Dark brown, worn down or in a loose ponytail
- Eyes: Dark brown, sharp and watchful
- Build: Average height, unimposing
- Skin: Warm olive
- Distinguishing features: Tends to dress casually to blend in (jeans, oversized sweaters, a practical messenger bag). She relies on looking unassuming so people ignore her.

## Background
Ana grew up in Barrio Norte, a mid-income district. Her parents are teachers who raised her with a strong sense of civic duty, though they always warned her to keep her head down. She is a sociology and journalism student at the Universidad de Las Flores, frustrated by the lack of real reporting in the city's corporate-owned media. She works a part-time job where she met Alex Garcia.

## Personality
Ana is not a natural thrill-seeker. She is cautious and initially reluctant to get involved in anything dangerous. However, she has a quiet, razor-sharp ability to observe. She notices things others miss precisely because she is used to being underestimated or ignored. Once she understands the stakes of a situation, her caution transforms into a quiet, stubborn bravery.

## Role in the Story
Ana is drawn into the narrative by circumstance. After surviving a high-speed chase that results in the death of Carlos Medina, she can no longer look away. She ends up working with investigator Ken, using her unassuming appearance to go undercover and monitor the corrupt building owners involved in the Residencial Las Estrellas scandal. The operation fundamentally changes her; she begins seriously considering dropping out of the university to pursue full-time investigative work.

## Connections
- **Alex Garcia**: A friend from her part-time job.
- [Carlos Medina](../carlos_medina/carlos_medina.md): The student whose death pulls her into the conspiracy.
- **Investigator Ken**: Her mentor in undercover operations.
