# Nico van der Meer

**Title:** European Business Heir, Van der Meer Family Member

**Physical Description:**
- Dutch man in his early 40s with an athletic-compact build, clear fair skin, straight dark-blonde hair graying at the temples and a small scar on his left brow, in sharp business-casual attire.
- **Identifying detail:** a thin pair of reading glasses pushed up into his hair, which he puts on only to read contracts. *(invented; his scar on the left brow is close to his brother Sebastian's, see the note below)*

**Description (full):**

Nico van der Meer is the son of [Alexander van der Meer Jr.](../alexander_van_der_meer_jr/alexander_van_der_meer_jr.md), part of the European branch of the van der Meer family. Born and raised in Rotterdam, he grew up hearing stories of the Las Flores lithium boom and the family's South American adventures. In his twenties, Nico moved to Las Flores to help manage the family's import-export operations between Europe and South America. A sharp, ambitious businessman, Nico navigates the complex dynamics between the legitimate van der Meer enterprises and his cousin [Dong](../dong_van_der_meer/dong_van_der_meer.md)'s shadowy Flowers Syndicate operations. He maintains a cordial but cautious relationship with the Las Flores branch, aware that his father's past romantic history with Elisabeth adds an undercurrent of tension to family gatherings.

**Age (2077):** 42 (b. 2035)
**District:** Las Flores
**Role:** European Business Heir
**Occupation:** Import-Export Manager, Van der Meer European Trading (Las Flores operations)
**Descendancy:** Dutch

## Want, Flaw, Fear

- **Want:** An independent identity: his stated goals are expanding European–South American trade routes and building a life separate from family drama.
- **Flaw:** Caution that turns into fence-sitting. He is "cordial but cautious" with everyone, and he stays inside a business whose routes are exactly what a smuggling syndicate would want. *(that the routes attract Dong is invented)*
- **Fear:** Being pulled into the Flowers Syndicate's orbit, or becoming the family scandal of his own generation, as his brother's and cousin's names already are. *(invented)*

## Habits and Contradictions

- Reads every contract himself and watches the room while others talk. *(invented, from his "observant, diplomatic" traits)*
