# Diego López

**Title:** Business & Economics Editor, El Informador

**Physical Description:**
- Latino man in his 40s with a stocky, comfortable build, olive-tan skin, wavy dark hair threaded with gray, thick brows and narrow appraising eyes; wears business-casual editor attire with reading glasses looped on a chain.

**Description (full):**

Covers economic development and investment in Las Flores. Began his career covering local business news for a regional newspaper before joining El Informador, where he has taken on increasing responsibility and now oversees the paper's coverage of economic development and investment.

**Age (2077):** 45 (b. 2032)
**District:** Las Flores
**Role:** Business & Economics Editor
**Descendancy:** Latin American

**Outlet:** [El Informador de Las Flores](../../lore/media/press/el_informador/el_informador.md), industrial section of the city.

---

## Newsroom context

El Informador's mission includes "economic development and its impact", examining how growth and foreign investment affect working-class communities. Diego owns that desk. He is the paper's translator between the balance sheet and the street: the person who can read a lithium offtake contract and explain what it does to a rent.

- **Born 2032**, so he was 20 at the 2052 leak; [Juan Rodríguez](../juan_rodriguez/juan_rodriguez.md) co-founded the paper in 2030, before Diego was born, so Diego joined an established paper. By 2077 he is 45. *(his career dates are invented; the lore states only "regional newspaper, then El Informador")*
- The paper survives without corporate or government advertising, so his desk covers the money without taking any of it. *(follows from Juan Rodríguez's ad policy)*

## Want, Flaw, Fear

- **Want:** Business reporting that working people can use: who is getting the investment, who is paying for it, and who is left out. *(invented)*
- **Flaw:** Methodical to the point of slowness. He will not run a figure he has not checked twice, and has lost scoops to faster, sloppier outlets. *(invented)*
- **Fear:** Being wrong in print about a number, handing a powerful company the one error it needs to discredit the paper. *(invented)*

## Habits and Contradictions

- Reading glasses on a chain, which he puts on to read and takes off to listen; the narrow, appraising eyes make people feel audited. *(habit invented)*
- A business editor at a paper that distrusts business. He is comfortable talking to executives and uncomfortable being seen to do it. *(invented)*
- Stocky and comfortable, a man who takes the long lunch and the long read.

## Relationships (with cost)

- **[Juan Rodríguez](../juan_rodriguez/juan_rodriguez.md), editor-in-chief.** Diego's work is where the paper's mission meets the investors it criticizes. Juan gives him freedom; the cost is that a wrong call lands on Juan's name.
- **[María Suárez](../maria_suarez/maria_suarez.md).** Her corruption stories often turn on money; he supplies the paper trail. *(invented)*
- **[Elisa Pérez](../elisa_perez/elisa_perez.md).** She turns his dense economic pieces into shareable content; he distrusts anything that fits in a caption. *(invented)*
- **[Martín López](../martin_lopez/martin_lopez.md).** Shares a surname but no stated kinship. Their beats overlap on port money and syndicate extortion. *(overlap invented)*
- **Foreign investors and the Business Coalition.** Sources and targets at once; they return his calls because he is fair.
- **[Pedro Escobar](../pedro_escobar/pedro_escobar.md).** City editor at rival La Prensa, who competes for the same local scoops. *(invented)*

## Story Role

An informant NPC for money trails: investment, contracts, property, and port finance. He can confirm whether a company's story adds up and point the player to the right public record. He is **not** part of the 2077 Alex/Evelyn main plot.
