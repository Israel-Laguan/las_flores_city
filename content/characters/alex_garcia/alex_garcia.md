# Alex Garcia

**Title:** Architecture Student, Futuro Brillante Scholar, Leader of the 2077 Grassroots Movement

**Physical Description:**
- 22-year-old Latino person of lean, wiry, medium-height build with straight, shaggy dark hair, hooded dark eyes with faint dark circles and a heart-shaped face; ink-stained hands, jeans, a hoodie, old sneakers and an overstuffed backpack of sketchbooks.
- **Identifying detail:** pencil and ink smudges on the side of the right hand, from sketching with the heel of the palm on the page. *(invented)*

**Description (full):**

The central protagonist of the 2077 endgame arc. Born in the Garcia family's flower-growing village by the Río de las Flores, youngest child of Alejandro and Maria Garcia and younger brother of Isabella and Sofia, Alex grew up knowing the cost of corporate greed intimately — their mother Maria died in January 2071, when Alex was sixteen, from respiratory complications caused by lithium-contaminated water. Alex enrolled at Universidad Nacional de Las Flores in 2075 to study sustainable urban design on a Futuro Brillante scholarship, the GLC program for Old Las Flores students. After moving into Residencial Las Estrellas, Alex noticed a pattern of suspicious deaths and began investigating, eventually discovering Evelyn Ruthenberg's vigilante campaign. The revelation that Evelyn was targeting the people responsible for the contamination that killed their mother transformed Alex's grief into directed action. Alex built a grassroots movement that brought the fight into the light, and died in 2077 detonating an abandoned mine to create an improvised dam — saving the city from a catastrophic flood and buying Evelyn and the others the cover they needed to escape.

**Age (2077):** ~22 (b. ~2055)
**Family:** Youngest child of [Alejandro Garcia](../alejandro_garcia/alejandro_garcia.md) and [Maria Garcia](../maria_garcia/maria_garcia.md); younger brother of [Isabella Garcia](../isabella_garcia/isabella_garcia.md) and [Sofia Garcia](../sofia_garcia/sofia_garcia.md); uncle to Sofia's daughter Ariana.
**District:** Las Flores (raised in Old Las Flores; lives in Residencial Las Estrellas, Sector Norte)
**Role:** Architecture Student
**Descendancy:** Latin American

## Want, Flaw, Fear

- **Want:** To make the grief useful. For years Alex carried their mother's death "in the quiet way of someone who had not found a way to make it useful yet." The movement is that use. They also want a city worth living in, one that is designed so that nobody else's mother dies of its water.
- **Flaw:** Self-consumption. Alex does not stop; [Miguel Jhonson](../miguel_jhonson/miguel_jhonson.md) has to remind them to eat and sleep, and Ana Kim's role is to prevent Alex burning out. The flaw ends in the mine: Alex tells no one and drives there alone.
- **Fear:** Hesitating. Alex hesitates before helping and never forgives themselves for it, and the fear is being "frozen while others act". This is why Alex spots the gasoline car in the Week-Four chase and acts before anyone has agreed on a plan, and [Carlos Medina](../carlos_medina/carlos_medina.md) dies in it. The guilt from that chase never leaves.

## Habits and Contradictions

- Sketches buildings when nervous, and is terrible at accepting compliments.
- Warm, funny and self-deprecating in small groups, yet capable of a "clear, directed" fury that runs underneath rather than replacing the warmth.
- Studies water and power systems for fun: how water moves through a city, redundancy in electrical grids. Loves the city's infrastructure while being behind on rent. This is how they design the movement, as distributed nodes rather than a leadership that can be decapitated.
- **Contradiction:** a person who insists on nonviolence and calls property destruction "tactically stupid and morally corrosive", who then blows up a mine. The only explosion is the one that saves the city.
- Forgetful with their own notebook: Ana Kim returns a forgotten one to a café, which is how the two start working together (see [Ana Kim](../ana_kim/ana_kim.md)).
- Their line, later carved on a bench near Residencial Las Estrellas: *"This neighborhood will be better built next time."*

## Relationships (with cost)

- **[Maria Garcia](../maria_garcia/maria_garcia.md), mother.** Alex remembers her whispering to the dying red flowers, "Just hold on a little longer." Her death is the origin of everything. The cost is that Alex was sixteen and could not help.
- **[Alejandro Garcia](../alejandro_garcia/alejandro_garcia.md), father.** Withdrawn in grief; he pushed Alex to apply for the scholarship. Alex owes their education to a man too silent to say he is proud, and leaves him a second loss in 2077.
- **[Isabella Garcia](../isabella_garcia/isabella_garcia.md) and [Sofia Garcia](../sofia_garcia/sofia_garcia.md), sisters.** Isabella is chronically ill from the river and resents Sofia; Sofia is deaf, crippled in the 2063 mine accident and the community's "saint". Alex stands between them as the youngest, and the scholarship is a way out that neither sister had. *(that Alex feels survivor's guilt over this is invented)*
- **[Evelyn Ruthenberg](../evelyn_ruthenberg/evelyn_ruthenberg.md), uneasy ally.** They begin as enemies, negotiate for weeks, and settle into mutual respect. Evelyn supplies intelligence and the target list; Alex supplies what Evelyn lacks, a reason to believe the list will be used for something other than killing. Alex dies so Evelyn can escape and expose Li Wei.
- **[Miguel Jhonson](../miguel_jhonson/miguel_jhonson.md), [Ana Kim](../ana_kim/ana_kim.md), [Isabella Vargas](../isabella_vargas/isabella_vargas.md), the core group.** Miguel is the logistics backbone and the one who restrains Alex's risk-taking; Ana is the honest mirror and the link to the press; Isabella Vargas is the intellectual anchor. Not to be confused with Alex's sister Isabella Garcia.
- **[Carlos Medina](../carlos_medina/carlos_medina.md), friend.** Joins through Miguel in Week Three and dies in the Week-Four chase Alex started.

## Story Role

Alex is the 2077 protagonist and, by the end, a martyr. The scholarship makes Alex a beneficiary of GLC, the Van der Meer family's company, whose mining the leak came from. *(treating that irony as a theme is an editorial reading, not stated in the stories)* The movement outlives Alex: a studio at the architecture school, a community center in Sector Norte and a bench near Las Estrellas carry their name.
