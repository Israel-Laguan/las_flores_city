# Viktor Reyes (The Doctor)

> Tags: `#figure` `#npc` `#lw_group` `#assassin`

## Overview

**Born:** ~2020 (Eastern Europe)
**Age (2077):** 57
**Role:** Covert Operative / Medical Cover Asset
**Status:** Alive

## Physical Description
- Hair: Ash blond, graying significantly, cut meticulously short, military style
- Eyes: Pale gray, very still, lacking warmth
- Build: Solid but not bulky; moves with practiced, silent economy
- Skin: Pale
- Distinguishing features: Always impeccably clean-cut. He carries a pristine, high-end medical bag. His hands are exceptionally steady.

## Background
Born in an unnamed Eastern European nation, Viktor served as a military field surgeon where he participated in operations that were later covered up. Following a dishonourable discharge for "irregularities" during these black ops, he was recruited by intermediaries for LW Group. He relocated to Las Flores and adopted the Spanish surname "Reyes" to blend in slightly better, operating under the licensed physician identity of "Dr. Marcos Vidal." For over 20 years, he has specialized in a very specific kind of wet work.

## Personality
Viktor is a cold, meticulous professional. He does not hate his targets; he simply finishes the job with terrifying efficiency. What makes him especially dangerous is that his medical knowledge is entirely genuine. He plays the role of a desperate, heroic responder flawlessly. He has only one tell: a psychological compulsion to collect coins from the countries of his targets, a habit he cannot explain even to himself.

## Role in the Story
When LW Group arranges an 'incident' (a car crash, a mugging gone wrong), Viktor is deployed as the first medical responder. His job is to ensure the target does not survive and to remove any incriminating evidence, all while appearing to provide emergency care. 

In 2055, during the crucial period before the Minera Estrella trial, Viktor was deployed to intercept the whistleblower Liu Fang. In a dark alleyway, he arrived just after she was stabbed. He smoothly displaced an off-duty nurse (Cecilia Rodríguez) who was trying to save her. With chilling calmness, he took over the triage, subtly ensuring Liu Fang bled out while ostensibly performing CPR, and recovered the primary evidence she was carrying (though she had already passed a memory card to a student).
