# Camila Santander

**Title:** Tourism Company Owner, Outdoor Adventure Guide

**Age (2077):** 32
**District:** Las Flores
**Role:** Tourism Company Owner
**Faction:** Independent

## Backstory & Personality

Camila Santander is the face of outdoor tourism in Las Flores. If you book a hiking tour, river rafting excursion, or mountain trek, you're likely paying her company. On the surface, she is charismatic, engaging, and deeply passionate about the region's natural beauty—a perfect host who knows exactly how to read a room and charm her clients. 

However, this bright "Gemini" exterior is entirely cultivated for profit. In reality, Camila is deeply misanthropic. She loves the mountains precisely because people *aren't* there, and she views her wealthy clients as nothing more than walking wallets. Her exceptional social skills are a tool she uses to extract maximum profit, which she intends to use to build a highly exclusive, high-end rural lodge where she can cater to a few rich clients and otherwise be left alone.

## The Threat

Her pristine business model is currently under threat from aggressive urban expansion. A rival real estate developer is attempting to buy up the land around her most lucrative trails to build a massive, cookie-cutter resort hotel and housing development. To fight them off, she needs money and influence, pushing her to take increasingly ruthless or morally questionable jobs to secure her territory.

## Player Interaction Dynamics

- **The Meet**: The player encounters Camila flawlessly charming a group of complaining, wealthy tourists. Once they're out of earshot, her smile instantly vanishes and she coldly calculates her profit margin for the day.
- **The Dynamic**: Informant / Facilitator. She knows the wilderness and off-grid paths better than anyone.
- **The Gameplay Loop**: The player helps her sabotage the rival hotel construction or secure land deeds. In return, she provides safe passage, rare off-grid resources, or access to secluded areas. 
- **The Subversion**: The player expects a "pure" nature lover fighting for the environment, only to realize she's fighting for her own monopoly and doesn't care about the people of Las Flores at all.

## Possible Endings

- 💖 **Lover — The Shared Silence**: The player breaks through her misanthropy. They secure the lodge, and she genuinely shares her mountain solitude with them, valuing the player more than the money.
- 🤝 **Friend — The Business Partner**: A mutually beneficial alliance. She gets her land, the player gets a permanent safe haven in the mountains, but it's always transactional.
- 💔 **Ex / Tragic — The Golden Cage**: Camila's greed consumes her. She sells out to the developers for an exorbitant sum, getting rich but losing her beloved mountains and any connection to the player.
- 🔥 **Enemy / Antagonist — The Ledger**: The player becomes a threat to her profit margins or land. She uses her knowledge of the wilderness to lead the player into a deadly trap, writing them off as a business expense.
- 🌫️ **Distant / Forgotten — The Brochure**: The player ignores her questline. She is eventually bought out or pushed out by the hotel developers, remaining just a fading smile on an old tourism ad.
