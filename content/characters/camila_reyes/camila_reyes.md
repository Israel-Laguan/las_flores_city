# Camila Reyes

**Title (full):** Environmental Science Student from Bahía de las Olas

**Description (full):**

Camila Reyes is a spirited 19-year-old from Bahía de las Olas, a picturesque coastal village known for its stunning beaches and tight-knit fishing community. Her life changed when Van Der Meer Mining began surveying her hometown's coastline. Because the inland lithium mines require massive export logistics and water, Van Der Meer plans to bulldoze Bahía de las Olas to construct a deep-water mega-port for their transport freighters, alongside a highly toxic brine-processing discharge facility. It's a calculated, logistical decision that would completely annihilate the local marine ecosystem and the village's livelihood. Unlike reckless cartels, Van Der Meer is highly sensitive to negative press and political pressure—especially terrified of public backlash pushing the government to side with rival Chinese-backed politicians in Las Flores. Camila realizes that empirical data alone isn't enough; she must win a public relations and political proxy war to save her home.

As the first in her family to attend university, Camila moved to the sprawling city of Las Flores to pursue a degree in Environmental Science. She is driven by a desperate, ticking-clock motivation: to gather enough evidence and political leverage to halt the mega-port construction before the dredging begins. 

While she radiates a warm-hearted enthusiasm, this positivity masks severe imposter syndrome. Furthermore, her idealism has calcified into an intense, single-minded obsession. Her cause is the center of her universe, and she tends to view relationships—including with the player—as secondary or utilitarian. She often inadvertently treats allies as mere props or support units for her activism, a trait that makes her romance path uniquely challenging and subverts typical expectations.

**Appearance:**

Camila has long, wavy dark hair that cascades down her back, often held out of her face by a colorful woven headband. She has bright brown eyes that sparkle with curiosity and a sun-kissed complexion from her childhood spent at the beach. Her style is a direct contrast to the neon and chrome of Las Flores: she favors casual, comfortable clothing like flowy white dresses over swimwear, paired with worn leather sandals and a woven village bracelet on her wrist. A camera is almost always strapped to her, capturing both beauty and environmental evidence.

**Personality & Flaws:**

*   **The Sunny Idealist:** Adventurous, passionate, and sociable. She acts as a grounding, moral compass to those around her.
*   **Imposter Syndrome:** Deeply insecure about her place in the city and at the university, fearing she isn't smart or ruthless enough to save her home.
*   **Single-Minded Obsession:** She is so focused on her goal that she frequently neglects the emotional needs of those around her. Allies (and the player) often feel like secondary props in her life rather than equal partners.
*   **Reckless Passion:** Quick to anger when the ocean or her village is threatened. Her desperation can lead her to take foolish risks against powerful corporate entities.

**Background:**

Born and raised in Bahía de las Olas, Camila grew up surrounded by the rhythms of coastal life—the early morning fishing boats and the bustling harbor markets. She spent her childhood learning about marine life from local fishermen. The announcement of Van Der Meer Mining's offshore extraction plans shattered her peaceful adolescence, transforming her from a carefree beach kid into a highly motivated, if inexperienced, eco-activist.

**Challenges:**

Beyond the threat of Van Der Meer Mining, Camila faces the crushing weight of homesickness. She must navigate a city where corruption is currency, learning the hard way how to exploit Van Der Meer's fear of Chinese-backed political rivals and negative PR. 

**Interests:**

Marine biology, ecological surveying, photography, coastal culture, beach volleyball, community organizing, and uncovering corporate environmental violations.

**Goals:**

To expose the ecological dangers of Van Der Meer Mining's offshore project, weaponize political pressure against them, and eventually return to Bahía de las Olas to implement sustainable coastal preservation practices.

**Visual Novel Dynamics & Player Paths:**

*   **Lover (The Mended Bond):** The player acts as her stalwart support, but the romance is rocky; players are initially shocked to find themselves treated as a secondary priority. The route requires a delicate "mending job" where the player helps her achieve her goals while gently forcing her to realize she must make emotional space for a partner. Ends with a peaceful, balanced life in Bahía de las Olas.
*   **Friend (The Academic Ally):** The player accepts being a useful prop for her cause, helping her secure legal protection for the village but remaining in the city. She becomes a lifelong, trusted contact for environmental data.
*   **Ex / Tragic (The Cynical Betrayal):** Frustrated by being treated as a secondary priority, the player uses her for lab access and ultimately takes a bribe from Van Der Meer to bury her research. She is left shattered, cutting all ties as her village is destroyed.
*   **Enemy (The Radicalization):** If the player mocks her idealism and supports the village's destruction, her positivity burns away. She drops out and joins a violent eco-terrorist cell, becoming a hardened antagonist later in the story.
*   **Distant (The Faded Spark):** Ignored by the player, the city chews her up. Overwhelmed by imposter syndrome, she quietly drops out and returns home defeated.

**Quote:**

"They look at the ocean and just see a balance sheet. I have to prove to them it's a heartbeat before they stop it forever."
