## Overview

Ana Kim is the **honest mirror** of Alex's investigation group, and its quiet link to the press. Her directness, organization and practical competence keep the group functional. She grew up in the orbit of her aunt, the investigative journalist Lina Kim, and fell for the idea of being the one who researches the story. Las Estrellas taught her that investigating shady things is not glamorous and not a detective novel: the real world has informants who sell you out and friends who die in car chases.

Her arc is about **sustainable resolve**. Carlos Medina's death and Ken's betrayal harden her. After the City Hall injury she steps back from fieldwork, but she does not leave: she works the paper, the contacts and the informant from a distance, double-crosses the double agent, and after Alex's death gets the evidence to the press through her aunt's network.

### Cautious by Nature
Ana is not a thrill-seeker. She is cautious and initially reluctant to get involved in anything dangerous, but she has a quiet, razor-sharp ability to observe. She notices things others miss precisely because she is used to being underestimated. Once she understands the stakes, her caution turns into stubborn bravery.

### Fatal Flaw: Clinical Detachment
To cope with danger she retreats into observation mode, treating human tragedy as a puzzle to be solved rather than a trauma to be felt. In the past this caused her to emotionally abandon a close friend during a family emergency because she was too busy "investigating solutions" to offer comfort. She is warm in low-stakes moments, but goes unreachable when people need her most.

### Texture: Habits and Contradictions *(invented, marked for veto)*
- **Verifies by reflex.** She checks a claim against a second source before she repeats it, even at dinner, even about the weather. Her family has learned to say "that's a first source" when they want to tease her.
- **Dry, deadpan humor.** She makes jokes with a perfectly straight face and waits for people to realize. The humor is how she says warm things she cannot say plainly.
- **Feeds people.** She notices who has not eaten and puts a paper bag in their hands without comment. Care is her love language; speeches are not.
- **Pens, not phones.** She keeps a pocket notebook and writes in a tiny, neat hand, because a notebook cannot be remotely wiped.
- **Counts exits.** Since Carlos's death she sits where she can see the door, and she hates that she does it.
- **Contradictions.** She is the group's most honest person and also the one keeping the most secrets (Ken, her aunt's channel). She prizes safety yet keeps volunteering for the one task nobody else can do. She says "I'm not brave" in the voice of someone who is.
- **What she fears.** Not danger, exactly, but being the reason someone else gets hurt, as she believes she was for Carlos by saying yes to the chase. She fears that being useful and being safe cannot be the same thing.

## Auntie Lina

[Lina Kim](../lina_kim/lina_kim.md) (b. 2025) is the investigative journalist at *La Prensa* who led the coverage of the Great Lithium Leak, and Ana's aunt: one parent of Ana is Lina's younger sibling. Their relationship is the emotional spine of Ana's story.

### The Leak Baby *(invented)*
- Ana was born in 2052, the year of the Great Lithium Leak. Lina began her investigation in September 2052, a few weeks after the leak, while Ana was a newborn. Lina missed most of Ana's first year chasing the story, and the family jokes that Ana's first words were a source's name.
- Ana grew up with the story: the leak was the background noise of her childhood, and Lina was the person on television explaining it.

### What Lina Is Like at Home *(invented)*
- **Sunday lunches** at her grandparents' house, where Lina arrives late and tired, and Ana sits next to her. Lina takes phone calls in the stairwell and returns with a face Ana learns to read.
- **The rules.** Lina's rules, delivered like family proverbs: "Two sources, then a third." "A story is a promise." "Never tell a source's name, not even to the people who love you." Ana memorized them as a girl and does not realize until later that the last one means her aunt has been keeping her own silences.
- **The clippings.** Lina keeps her bylines in a shoebox. Ana read them as bedtime stories.
- **What Ana learned from her:** how to build an airtight case, how to treat a source as a person, how to stay calm while someone lies to your face.

### Where They Pull Apart *(invented)*
- **Lina never wanted Ana in the field.** She has buried colleagues' stories and has seen what happens to people who hold the evidence (Cecilia Pérez, Liu Fang). She taught Ana to research precisely so Ana would never have to stand where she stood.
- **Ana hid Las Estrellas from her.** She did not tell Lina about the car, Ken or the break-in until after Carlos died, partly to protect her and partly because she did not want to be told to stop. When Lina found out, she did not say "I told you so". She sat in silence and then asked for Ana's notes.
- **Ana's disillusion with the glamour.** Ana had admired the fearless journalist on the clippings. She learned that her aunt's fearlessness is mostly fear managed well, and that the work leaves people alone at night with a phone.
- **Lina's guilt.** She believes she gave Ana the taste for this and is responsible for Carlos's death in a way no one has said aloud.

### After Alex's Death: Both Break Their Rules
- Ana breaks "never involve family": she brings the evidence to her aunt because she has no one else who can reach seventeen newsrooms at once.
- Lina breaks "never publish what you haven't verified in person": she publishes on a source she knows better than anyone, her niece.
- The two sit up all night with the data, and Lina says the thing she has never said: that she is proud, and that she is sorry.
- **Afterwards:** Lina still gives Ana the same advice, "Two sources, then a third", but now asks her how she is sleeping.

## The 2077 Investigation Arc

### Joining the Investigation
- **Catalyst:** Comes to return Alex's forgotten notebook at the café; Alex is distracted and asks for her help
- **Early weeks:** Alex is living in Residencial Las Estrellas and has begun investigating with Carlos Medina and Ana. Carlos and Ana, both careful and quietly funny under pressure, grow close during these weeks
- **Nature:** Accidental entry that becomes deliberate

### Meeting Ken
- **When:** Around the same month, while the building mystery deepens
- **Who:** Ken, an undercover investigator stationed by Chief Inspector Adeyemi in the building's vacant apartment to watch Alex and the owners; he has no surname and no character file yet
- **Cover:** To keep the vacant unit credible, Ana poses as Alex's relative and Ken as her fiancé (per the source dump; the canonical story leaves the relative as an unnamed police investigator)
- **Texture:** Professional and uncomfortable; Adeyemi controls what the team knows. Ana asks the questions everyone else sidesteps, and she is intelligent enough to notice Ken's inconsistencies

### Role in the Movement
- **Archival Work:** Works with Isabella on research
- **Key Contribution:** Uses library employee access to retrieve archived press databases
- **Major Discovery:** Finds the newspaper article identifying four of the building's victims as former Minera Estrella PR staff
- **Practical Support:** Brings food to planning sessions, notices when Miguel hasn't slept

### The Chase and Carlos's Death
- **Context:** Alex spots the old gasoline car and acts before anyone agrees on a plan; Ana, who happened to be meeting Alex that day, says she isn't going anywhere
- **Position:** In Alex's car, following south; Carlos drives the other. A motorcycle cuts across his lane
- **Impact:** Carlos's death breaks the detective-novel idea of the work. It is the moment the investigation stops being abstract for her

### Hardening
- **Reflection:** For some days she goes quiet and reflects, then returns having decided to keep helping, with no illusions
- **Resolve:** She will not be the person in the corridor again, but she will be the one who knows things

### The Archive Break-In
- **Context:** Group decides to break into City Hall archives for more evidence
- **Participants:** Isabella (leader), Ana, Miguel
- **Ana's Role:** In the corridor with Miguel when guards find them
- **Physical Confrontation:** Guard grips Ana, bruises her (swollen eye, bruised ribs, wrist injury)
- **Isabella's Action:** Pulls Ana out of the guard's grip, then continues to the records room
- **Significance:** Ana realizes the cost and decides to step back from fieldwork

### Stepping Back from the Field
- **Timing:** In the car on the way back from the archive break-in
- **Statement:** *"I'm done with this part. Not because I don't believe it. Because I have to be able to live my life, and fieldwork is beyond what I can give it."*
- **Acknowledgment:** Thanks Isabella specifically for pulling her out
- **Reaction:** No one holds it against her
- **What changes:** She leaves the physical operations, not the investigation

### Information and Connections
- **Role:** Supplies Alex with information and connections, above all her contact with Ken
- **Ken as informant:** As Alex gets close to Evelyn Ruthenberg, Ana keeps contact with Ken and uses him as an informant
- **The double agent:** Ken is working both for Adeyemi and for the corporation. Ana discovers it
- **The double-cross:** Rather than cut him off, she uses him, feeding him information that misleads his handlers while taking what he leaks. The betrayal costs her her trust in the police and any remaining belief in tidy morality

### Sidelined, Not Absent
- **During the Evelyn and Alex events:** She is sidelined as events speed up, but keeps helping with information, contacts and paperwork
- **After Alex's death:** She distributes the data to the press, using her aunt Lina Kim's contacts to reach the news organizations
- **Public:** Comes forward publicly in the week after the broadcast and, with Miguel and Isabella, is brought in for questioning and then joins the transitional council

### Testimony
- **Provided to:** International forensic review team
- **Content:** Investigation's early phase and the Ken double-cross
- **Style:** Thorough, accurate, delivered without drama
- **Framing:** Does not frame herself as hero or coward

### Where the Player Finds Her
- Studying sociology and journalism, and working freelance as a background investigator to afford finishing her degree, with a foot still in investigative information
- She assesses newcomers with a measured, cynical eye: she learned fast, and at great cost, that visibility is a liability
- She feeds verified, airtight evidence to established reporters (above all her aunt) or publishes under encrypted pseudonyms

### Ongoing Impact
- **Movement Philosophy:** Alex builds a movement full of people who give what they can and stop when they have to
- **Sustainability:** Ana's step back from the field becomes a model: not failure, but what keeps the movement going
- **Legacy:** Her honesty about limits becomes part of the group's strength
