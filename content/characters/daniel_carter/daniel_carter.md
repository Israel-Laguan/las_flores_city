# Daniel Carter

**Title:** Pioneer, Humanity First

**Physical Description:**
- Canadian man in his early 70s with a broad, heavy build, weathered wind-crinkled skin and a square face; thin receding gray-white hair, thick brows, warm round eyes and a faded scar across one brow; wears a practical weathered jacket with a Humanity First lapel pin.

**Description (full):**

The founding Pioneer of the Humanity First movement in Las Flores. Arriving from Canada in 2035, he laid the groundwork for the community councils and the movement's core values.

**Age (2077):** 72 (b. 2005; revised from 2012 to match the 30-year-old Pioneer of 2035)
**District:** Las Flores
**Role:** Pioneer
**Descendancy:** Canadian

**Movement:** [Humanity First](../../lore/organizations/movements/humanity_first/overview/overview.md), founding Pioneer and first Chair (2035–2039)

---

## Background

- **March 2035: arrives from the Canada branch** as a 30-year-old Pioneer with a small grant and a mission to understand the city. He is well-versed in community organizing and fluent in Spanish. *(his Canadian life before 2035 is invented)*
- **April–June 2035:** listening tours in squares, markets and cafés; learns about pollution, the lack of public space and corruption; recruits three allies: [Elena Ramirez](../elena_ramirez/elena_ramirez.md), [Javier Torres](../javier_torres/javier_torres.md) and [Carlos Lopez](../carlos_lopez/carlos_lopez.md).
- **August 2035:** first project, the community garden on an abandoned lot. In June 2036 he introduces "community milestones", breaking projects into phases with celebrations.
- **2036–2038:** Council founded (January 2036); Elena is appointed first Chair. He steps back as local leaders take over; the Youth Council begins in November 2038.
- **June 2039:** public farewell. He officially steps down, stresses that the council is self-sufficient and remains available as a resource.
- **2077:** The lore does not say where he is. Treat him as alive and in touch from outside Las Flores. *(invented)*

Timeline source: [Founding Era](../../lore/organizations/movements/humanity_first/timeline_founding/timeline_founding.md).

## Want, Flaw, Fear

- **Want:** A movement that does not need him. His whole method is to make himself unnecessary, and by 2039 he does.
- **Flaw:** He builds trust by listening, then leaves, and the council inherits his rules without his judgment. The later rogue groups take root in the years after he withdraws (Diego Salazar and Carla Ruiz from December 2038). *(the framing is mine; the dates are the lore's)*
- **Fear:** That the movement is "his", and that local ownership is a performance. He repeatedly insists the council is self-sufficient. *(invented)*

## Habits and Contradictions

- Wears a Humanity First lapel pin on a practical weathered jacket; a faded scar crosses one brow, origin unrecorded. *(origin invented)*
- A Canadian who becomes more local than the locals by speaking Spanish and sitting in markets.
- The contradiction: a founder who is celebrated for leaving.

## Relationships (with cost)

- **[Elena Ramirez](../elena_ramirez/elena_ramirez.md).** His first local ally; she becomes the Chair he hands the council to.
- **[Javier Torres](../javier_torres/javier_torres.md).** Mentored; later Vice-Chair.
- **[Carlos Lopez](../carlos_lopez/carlos_lopez.md).** The skeptic he wins over; Carlos's verification is what protects the movement from fraud after Daniel leaves.
- **[Sofia Alvarez](../sofia_alvarez/sofia_alvarez.md).** First head of the Youth Council, created as he stepped back.
- **Luis "El Tigre" Peralta.** The first rogue incident arrives in his time (late 2037, banished February 2038): see [Rogue Incidents](../../lore/organizations/movements/humanity_first/rogue_incidents/rogue_incidents.md).

## Story Role

A founding-myth NPC and a source of movement history. He is **not** part of the 2077 Alex/Evelyn main plot.
