## Newsroom context

La Prensa moved from print to a "multi-platform news provider" with podcasts, video documentaries and interactive pieces, plus educational workshops on media literacy. Soledad is the person who built that. *(her specific tactics below are invented)*

- **Background in digital media** before joining; she was in her early 40s by 2077 (b. 2035).
- Her multimedia model matters for survival: LW's ad pressure hurts print revenue, so subscriptions, podcasts and creator partnerships are how the paper stays independent. *(invented link to the LW pressure)*

## Want, Flaw, Fear

- **Want:** A paper that younger readers trust and can afford. She wants the investigations to reach people who never read a newspaper.
- **Flaw:** Speed and format over depth. She pushes stories into video and social posts before [Victor Almeida](../victor_almeida/victor_almeida.md) is ready, and a premature clip once ran ahead of the facts. *(invented example)*
- **Fear:** That the paper's brand becomes content for platforms, so its credibility is the cost of reach. *(invented)*
