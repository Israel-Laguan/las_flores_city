# Cecilia Rodríguez (The Nurse)

> Tags: `#figure` `#npc` `#independent` `#medical` `#witness`

## Overview

**Born:** ~2030
**Age (2077):** 47
**Role:** Community Nurse / Off-duty Witness
**Status:** Alive

## Physical Description
- Hair: Dark, usually tied back in a practical, no-nonsense bun
- Eyes: Dark, deeply empathetic but often tired, alert to her surroundings
- Build: Average height, strong from long shifts on her feet
- Skin: Olive, worn by the stress of city life and long hours
- Distinguishing features: Always wears practical shoes; often carries a well-worn medical bag even when off-duty; wears a faded zip-up jacket over her scrubs.

## Background
Cecilia grew up in San Miguel del Monte, a working-class district of Las Flores, alongside her older brother Juan. While Juan turned to journalism to expose the city's systemic rot, Cecilia chose a more immediate form of care: nursing. She attended medical school on a corporate scholarship but famously turned down a lucrative position at a downtown private hospital to return to her neighborhood. She now runs a chronically underfunded but vital health clinic on Calle Morales, providing care to those who fall through the cracks of the city's privatized health system.

## Personality
Calm under pressure, pragmatic, and deeply committed to her community. She does not share her brother's fiery, public-facing idealism; instead, she believes in quiet, localized action. She is protective of her patients and her family, and while she avoids the spotlight, she is highly observant. Years of working in an under-resourced clinic have made her excellent at improvising and reading people.

## Role in the Story
In 2055, during the lead-up to the Minera Estrella trial, a young Cecilia was walking home after a grueling late shift when she heard a commotion in an alleyway. She found the Whistleblower (Liu Fang) bleeding out and immediately shifted into triage mode, attempting to stabilize her. Before she could save Liu Fang, a corporate "cleaner" posing as a doctor (Viktor Reyes) arrived. Cecilia recognized immediately that something was wrong—his approach was too calculated, his medical bag too pristine, and his focus entirely on the victim rather than securing the scene. He displaced her with false authority, and she watched helplessly as he ensured the victim died under the guise of failed resuscitation. 

Unable to prove what she saw, the encounter haunted her for decades. She later contacted her brother, Juan, about what she witnessed, helping to spark his deep dive into the city's corruption, which eventually connects to the player's broader narrative in 2077.
