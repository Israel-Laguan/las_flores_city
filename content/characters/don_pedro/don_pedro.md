# Don Pedro

**Title:** La Familia Gang Leader

**Physical Description:**
- Latino man of about 62 with a broad, heavy build and weathered tan skin marked by fine scars; thin, severely receding grey hair combed back, thick dark brows, deep-set watchful blue-grey eyes, a broken-healed nose, a faded knife scar across the jaw and short grey stubble
- Wears an open-collared guayabera or dark collared shirt and heavy rings

**Description (full):**

Don Pedro — Pedro Vargas Castellano, 62 — has controlled La Familia's territory across the Industrial Zone and Old Las Flores border for over two decades. Born in the district, he runs his organisation as a substitute state: security where police response averages forty-seven minutes, protection for ignored vendors, credit where formal lenders refuse. The rackets funding these services are, in his framing, a tax for maintaining order where government under-governs. His uneasy co-existence with El Caimán's Cartel and Dragon Head's Chinese Mafia is his most durable achievement — 2055 and 2063 conflicts changed nothing; his 2063 settlement has held fourteen years. His paternalistic style is genuine and extractive: he attends events, resolves disputes, supports families in crisis, and runs rackets against the same people.

**Age (2077):** 62 (b. 2015)
**District:** Industrial Zone and Old Las Flores border
**Role:** La Familia Gang Leader

## Background

- **Born 2015, raised in the Industrial Zone**; he enters La Familia in his twenties (2035–2045) *(his early rank is invented)*.
- **2055:** takes leadership after his predecessor's incarceration. A territorial conflict that year changes nothing.
- **2063:** negotiates the territorial settlement with **El Caimán's Cartel** and **Dragon Head's Chinese Mafia**. It has held fourteen years; the uneasy coexistence is his most durable achievement.
- **The Flowers Syndicate.** The [Flowers Syndicate](../../lore/organizations/criminal/flowers_syndicate/flowers_syndicate.md) lists La Familia as a "fragile alliance" based on shared anti-Chinese sentiment, mutual protection and some territorial cooperation. Dong van der Meer runs the Syndicate. *(personal contact with [Dong](../dong_van_der_meer/dong_van_der_meer.md) is not stated)*
- **His model.** He runs La Familia as a "substitute state": security where police response averages forty-seven minutes, protection for ignored vendors, credit where formal lenders refuse. The rackets that fund this are, in his framing, a tax for keeping order where the government under-governs. It is both paternalistic and extractive: he attends events, resolves disputes and supports families in crisis, and runs rackets against the same people.

## Want, Flaw, Fear

- **Want:** Stability and legacy: a territory that stays orderly and a neighborhood that remembers him as its patriarch.
- **Flaw:** He cannot see the contradiction between care and extortion; he is genuinely generous to the people he squeezes. *(from the lore's "genuine and extractive")*
- **Fear:** The 2063 settlement breaking, or the city's crisis, such as the leak, the Business Coalition and the police, upsetting a balance he holds together by hand. *(invented)*

## Habits and Contradictions

- Open-collared guayabera, heavy rings, thin gray hair combed back, a faded knife scar along the jaw and a broken, healed nose.
- Shows up at funerals, weddings and disputes in person. *(consistent with his style, invented example)*
- Contradiction: the patriarch of an organization whose protection is also its threat.
