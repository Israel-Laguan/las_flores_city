# Rafaela

>
> **Role:** Ex-Wife of Peter van der Meer; mother of Peter van der Meer Jr.

## Overview

**Born:** 2022
**Age:** ~55 in 2077
**Family:** Ex-wife of Peter van der Meer; mother of Peter van der Meer Jr.
**District:** Las Flores
**Personality:** `expressive_socialite`

Rafaela is a Brazilian woman who married into the Van der Meer family as a marriage of convenience, then found herself in a city that stared at her and never quite made room. She did not shrink. She is loud in the way people are who learned that being quiet gets you ignored: she laughs with her whole body, touches your arm when she talks, calls everyone *querido* or *querida*, and fills a room before she has finished walking into it. Las Flores' elite called it too much. She decided "too much" was a brand.

## Physical Description

- Build: Athletic-compact, expressive hands and posture
- Face: Heart-shaped, high cheekbones, almond eyes, full lips
- Hair: Wavy dark styled hair with gray streaks she refuses to hide
- Style: Well-to-do socialite, bold jewelry, always dressed for a camera

## How She Sees Her Marriage

In her own frame, she and Peter were never *namorados*. In Brazil, people *ficam*, they date, and only when two people formally agree to *namorar* does exclusivity become the rule. Betrayal, *traição*, is breaking that agreement, and she and Peter never made one. Las Flores high society heard "marriage of convenience" and "partners outside the marriage" and pronounced it scandal. She heard it as the first honest thing anyone said.

## The 2070 Interview

Her tell-all in 2070 was both a confession and a career move. She told the truth about the marriage and voiced fears about Peter Jr. ("I worry he will face the same struggles his father did, hiding who he is"). It also made her a name in the city's media, which she has not let go of. She still gives interviews. She still attends galas. She is a regular on society pages.

## Her Son

She loves Peter Jr. fiercely and physically: hugs that last too long, kisses on both cheeks in front of cameras. She also treats his public life as an extension of her own. She encourages the yacht events, the content calendar, and the invitations, and frames all of it as protecting him ("he is safer if everyone loves him"). Whether she is protecting him or using him depends on the day, and she is not always sure herself.
