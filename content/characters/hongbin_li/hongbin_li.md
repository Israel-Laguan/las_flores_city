**Title:** Senior Manager for a Beijing-based Public Relations Firm

**Physical Description:**

- Eyes: Dark brown, sharp and calculating
- Build: Average height, lean and fit
- Skin: Fair with a healthy glow
- Distinguishing features: Perfect white teeth from a recent veneer, always impeccably dressed in designer clothing, subtle cologne, Apple Watch on wrist

**Description (full):**

Hongbin Li is a charismatic and highly strategic early 30s professional from Shanghai, China, now based in Las Flores as part of a government initiative to expand Chinese influence and investment in the region. He earned his degree in Communications from Fudan University and works as a senior manager for a Beijing-based PR firm specializing in digital media and influencer marketing.

He oversees a team of social media managers who work with influencers, bloggers, and content creators across Las Flores and South America to promote Chinese culture, products, and political agendas. Hongbin collaborates with local newspapers, television stations, and mainstream media outlets to ensure favorable coverage of China-related news and events, while coordinating with Chinese-owned businesses to increase their visibility and market share.

Despite his charming demeanor, he can be ruthless when protecting China's image and advancing its geopolitical goals. He currently lives in an upscale high-rise apartment in Las Flores' financial district and is actively working on a major campaign to showcase Chinese investments and development projects, aiming to sway public opinion in favor of closer ties between China and the region.
