# Adrián Beltrán

> Tags: `#figure` `#npc`
>
> **Role:** Talent & Brand Manager

## Overview

Peter Jr.'s manager. Runs his content calendar, brand deals and 'Sunset Socials' for the agency Lumen Studio. Polished and personable; he treats the boy's public life as a campaign with a launch date. Openly gay, and he understands exactly why the Amor Verdadero angle sells.

## Physical Description

Slim Latino man of 36 with warm brown skin, dark brown eyes, close-cropped dark hair with a neat fade, clean-shaven, a sharp tailored slate-blue jacket over a white open-collar shirt, a smartwatch he checks often and a relaxed professional smile.

## Background *(all invented unless noted)*

- **Born 2041** (36 in 2077). He grew up in the Port district, a visible, queer kid in a conservative neighborhood. *(invented)* He came up through event promotion before joining **Lumen Studio**, a talent-management agency. *(Lumen Studio is from his description; his path is invented)*
- **Why he sells the angle.** He is openly gay and "sincerely convinced that visibility is a form of protection." The [Amor Verdadero](../../lore/organizations/families/van_der_meer/van_der_meer.md) angle works commercially, and he also thinks a famous queer boy is harder to hurt than an anonymous one.
- **What the dialogues show:** in Act 2 he scripts Peter Jr.'s reaction to Rafaela's 2070 quote: "Post a response at five. Caption attached. Keep the pin visible. Warm, not angry." He tells the boy "the mystery sells" about the Tomás rumor. In Act 5, after Peter skips a Tuesday post, "Adrián was furious for a week and then he sort of... saw the numbers": he is persuadable by data.

## Want, Flaw, Fear *(all invented; the Flaw echoes his description)*

- **Want:** A client who becomes a brand that outlasts him, and his own agency.
- **Flaw:** Treats a child's life as a campaign with a launch date; he confuses protecting Peter with managing him.
- **Fear:** That the visibility he sold will hurt the boy it was meant to protect, or that Rafaela finds a more compliant manager.

## Habits and Contradictions

- Smartwatch he checks often, sharp slate-blue jacket, a relaxed professional smile. *(from his description)*
- Warm in public, exact in private. A man who truly believes his own pitch.
