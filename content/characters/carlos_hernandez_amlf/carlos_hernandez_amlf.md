Former miner who leads the Asociación de Mineros Las Flores (AMLF). Negotiated improved safety standards and better compensation for mining workers. Distinct from the politician Carlos Hernandez.

**Age (2077):** 55 (b. 2022)

**Role:** Union Leader

- **Union leader.** Leads the AMLF and negotiates "improved safety standards and better compensation" for mining workers. The AMLF belongs to the Progressive Alliance, alongside Manuel Reyes' Union del Trabajador, to counterbalance corporate power.
- **The trial era** (Jan 2055 – Mar 2057) saw former safety inspectors such as [Javier Mendoza](../javier_mendoza/javier_mendoza.md) testify about routine neglect, some later retracting amid intimidation. Whether Carlos was involved is not stated. *(connection invented)*

## Want, Flaw, Fear

- **Want:** Safe mines and fair pay; the specific victories he has won are safety standards and compensation. His loyalty is to the workers' bodies, not to any party.
- **Flaw:** Pragmatic bargaining. He wins improvements by negotiating, which means accepting less than full accountability; and he allies with the Progressive Alliance whose own record is mixed. *(invented framing)*
- **Fear:** Another accident that his standards did not prevent, and members who are intimidated into silence, as witnesses were after the trial. *(invented)*
