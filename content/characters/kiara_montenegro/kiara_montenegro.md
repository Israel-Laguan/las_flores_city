# Kiara Montenegro

> Tags: `#figure` `#npc`
>
> **Role:** Socialite & Content Creator

## Overview

A 17-year-old content creator who attaches herself to Peter Jr.'s yacht events for the reach. Public affection, private contempt; she calls him 'the pin kid' when he isn't around.

## Physical Description

Tall, slim Latina girl of 17 with smooth youthful skin, long glossy dark hair worn loose, a bright filtered smile, designer sunglasses pushed up on her head like a hairband, and a fitted coral knit top chosen for the camera.

## Related Lore

- [Peter van der Meer Jr.](../peter_van_der_meer_jr/peter_van_der_meer_jr.md)

## Background *(all invented unless noted)*

- **Born 2060** (17 in 2077). Her family is upwardly mobile but not Van der Meer-rich: a mother who sells property and a father rarely home. Her feed is her currency. *(invented)*
- **How she got in:** she met [Peter Jr.](../peter_van_der_meer_jr/peter_van_der_meer_jr.md) at a beach-club launch where his manager's agency was seeding content, and treated his orbit as a career ladder.
- **What the dialogues show:** on the yacht she poses for the lens without looking at the boy next to her; when he is out of earshot she says "the pin kid pays for everything and posts three times a day," and tells [Bruno](../bruno_salvatierra/bruno_salvatierra.md) to get the VIP code before it changes (Act 3).

## Want, Flaw, Fear

- **Want:** A follower count that does not depend on anyone's yacht. She wants her own brand.
- **Flaw:** Flatters people she is using, and cannot tell when she is no longer using them.
- **Fear:** Being forgotten the day Peter Jr.'s feed moves on, and Adrián dropping her from the guest list. *(invented; Adrián's control of the list is invented)*

## Habits and Contradictions

- Designer sunglasses pushed up like a hairband; a coral knit top picked for the camera. *(from her description)*
- Public affection, private contempt. She is warmer to Tomás than to anyone, because he does not matter to her feed. *(invented)*

## Relationships

- **[Bruno Salvatierra](../bruno_salvatierra/bruno_salvatierra.md).** Co-conspirator; they laugh together at Peter Jr.'s expense.
- **[Adrián Beltrán](../adrian_beltran/adrian_beltran.md).** The gatekeeper whose approval she courts.
- **[Peter Jr.](../peter_van_der_meer_jr/peter_van_der_meer_jr.md).** Her access; she may end up as a rival ending if the player sides with him against the entourage.
- **[Tomás Quispe](../tomas_quispe/tomas_quispe.md).** The friend she does not understand.
