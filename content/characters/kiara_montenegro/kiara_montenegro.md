# Kiara Montenegro

> Tags: `#figure` `#npc`

> **Role:** Socialite & Content Creator

## Overview

A 17-year-old content creator who attaches herself to Peter Jr.'s yacht events for the reach. Public affection, private contempt; she calls him 'the pin kid' when he isn't around.

## Physical Description

Tall, slim Latina girl of 17 with smooth youthful skin, long glossy dark hair worn loose, a bright filtered smile, designer sunglasses pushed up on her head like a hairband, and a fitted coral knit top chosen for the camera.
