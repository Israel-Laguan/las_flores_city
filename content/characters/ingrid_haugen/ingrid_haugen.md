# Ingrid Haugen

> Tags: `#figure` `#npc` `#nm_ltd` `#handler` `#liaison`

## Overview

**Born:** ~2038
**Age (2077):** 39
**Role:** Field Liaison, N&M LTD
**Status:** Alive

## Physical Description
- Hair: Blonde, usually pulled back in a severe, immaculate style
- Eyes: Pale blue, intelligent, often flicking between the person she's speaking to and a tablet
- Build: Tall and sharp-featured
- Skin: Fair
- Distinguishing features: Never seen without her company-issued tablet; her posture is unnervingly perfect.

## Background
Ingrid is of Norwegian origin and moved to Las Flores specifically for her placement with Nakamura & Morgan LTD. She is the external, client-facing handler—often the first human face that new arrivals or behavioral subjects see after their initial processing. She was selected for her high emotional intelligence, which she uses not for empathy, but for control.

## Personality
Ingrid is not warmhearted, but she is highly skilled at *professional empathy*. She knows exactly what to say to make a subject feel heard, validated, and calm, without ever actually caring about their well-being. She speaks in clipped, efficient sentences. Internally, Ingrid sometimes harbors doubts about the ethics of N&M's behavioral conditioning methods, but she suppresses these through strict compartmentalization and a focus on her performance metrics.

## Role in the Story
Ingrid manages the player's awakening process during the `dialogue_welcome_handler` sequence and gives early missions. She is reachable via SMS, functioning as a primary quest giver and corporate guide. She represents the smiling, polished face of corporate control. 

## Connections
- [Vance Nakamura](../vance_nakamura/vance_nakamura.md): Her superior at N&M LTD. He handles the internal behavioral metrics, while she handles the subjects directly.
- [Alex (Player)](../alex/alex.md): Her assigned subject.
