# Clara Torres

**Title:** Technology & Innovation Editor, La Prensa

**Physical Description:**
- Lean, sharp-featured Latina woman of about 35 with an angular face, high cheekbones, brown eyes and a small mole near the left eye
- Long straight black hair with a centre part; modern smart-casual journalist attire, often carrying a tablet

**Description (full):**

Leads reporting on technology, science, and their societal impacts. Instrumental in engaging younger audiences through cutting-edge digital storytelling techniques.

**Age (2077):** ~35 (b. 2042)
**District:** Las Flores
**Role:** Technology & Innovation Editor
**Descendancy:** Latin American

**Outlet:** [La Prensa](../../lore/media/press/la_prensa/la_prensa.md), main office. The data lists "Las Flores" as her district.

## Newsroom context

She leads the paper's technology and science desk and is "instrumental in engaging younger audiences through cutting-edge digital storytelling techniques." In a city where the product is lithium, her beat is the technology behind it: batteries, extraction, surveillance. *(her exact stories are invented)*

- **Born 2042**, so she is about 35 in 2077 and the youngest editor on the main-office list. *(her age is invented; Victor is 62 and Soledad Ortega 42)*

## Want, Flaw, Fear

- **Want:** To make technology and science readable, and to prove the audience La Prensa wants is the one that reads her.
- **Flaw:** Enthusiasm for the format can outrun the facts. She is quick to adopt a new tool and slower to check what it hides. *(invented)*
- **Fear:** Being the "young person" token, with her desk treated as a marketing arm of the paper. *(invented)*

## Habits and Contradictions

- Always has a tablet; the small mole near her left eye is her most noticeable feature.
- Covers cutting-edge tech while the paper's real money and risk sit with print-era investigations.
