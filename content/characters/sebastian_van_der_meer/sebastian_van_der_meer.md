# Sebastian van der Meer

**Title:** CEO, Van der Meer Industries

**Physical Description:**
- Dutch man in his 40s with an athletic-compact build, angular face, strong jaw, straight blonde hair threaded with gray and a small scar near the left brow; wears CEO business attire with a company pin.
- **Identifying detail:** the small scar near the left brow, which he turns to the light in photographs. *(the habit is invented)*

**Description (full):**

Ambitious CEO of Van der Meer Industries. Elder son of [Alexander van der Meer Jr.](../alexander_van_der_meer_jr/alexander_van_der_meer_jr.md) (European branch) and brother of [Nico](../nico_van_der_meer/nico_van_der_meer.md); a cousin once removed of [Liam](../liam_van_der_meer/liam_van_der_meer.md), [Xiu Li](../xiu_li_van_der_meer/xiu_li_van_der_meer.md) and [Dong](../dong_van_der_meer/dong_van_der_meer.md). Together with Liam, he has driven exponential growth through strategic decisions and identifying profitable investment opportunities.

**Age (2077):** 45 (b. 2032)
**District:** Las Flores
**Role:** CEO
**Descendancy:** Dutch

---

## Background

- Born 2032 in the European branch (Hendrik's line); raised in Rotterdam alongside his younger brother Nico. *(the Rotterdam upbringing is inferred from his father's business and Nico's file)*
- Joined Van der Meer Industries relatively recently: press and social posts describe Liam and "his cousin Sebastian" as a dynamic team "since the latter joined the family business a few months ago" and credit the company's "unprecedented growth" to the pair.
- Becomes CEO while [Anna van der Meer](../anna_van_der_meer/anna_van_der_meer.md) remains Chair of the board and executive director. Anna sets direction and values; Sebastian runs corporate strategy.

## The scandal

He is an alleged attendee, with [Natalia van der Meer](../natalia_van_der_meer/natalia_van_der_meer.md), of the [Punta del Este villa party](../../lore/stories/van_der_meer_orgy_scandal/van_der_meer_orgy_scandal.md): a guest secretly filmed a drug-and-alcohol party organized by younger Van der Meers, and the footage circulated among Las Flores' elite. The family denied "any claims of wrongdoing" in an identical statement. In-game, he is a gossip and clue NPC.

## Want, Flaw, Fear

- **Want:** Growth, and proof that the European branch can run what the Las Flores branch built. He arrives as an outsider to a company whose family founders were in Las Flores long before him. *(motive inferred from his position)*
- **Flaw:** Ambition outrunning discretion. He is the one the footage shows; the same appetite that makes him an effective investor leaves him exposed to scandal. *(invented framing)*
- **Fear:** Being removed by the family, especially by Anna, who holds the Chair and the family's confidence, and being seen as a hired figurehead. *(invented)*

## Habits and Contradictions

- Frames every conversation in terms of return on investment. *(invented)*
- A public face of discipline ("young and successful leadership") and a private record that the press has filmed.
- Wears the company pin even at private events. *(invented)*

## Relationships (with cost)

- **[Liam van der Meer](../liam_van_der_meer/liam_van_der_meer.md), cousin and partner.** They share credit for the growth. Liam is the local, Sebastian the newcomer, and the cost is who is seen as the real driver. *(invented tension)*
- **[Anna van der Meer](../anna_van_der_meer/anna_van_der_meer.md), cousin and Chair.** She sits above him; the family's real direction comes from her. *(their personal relationship is not described)*
- **[Nico van der Meer](../nico_van_der_meer/nico_van_der_meer.md), younger brother.** Sebastian is the elder son; Nico is the one who manages the European import-export link.
- **[Alexander van der Meer Jr.](../alexander_van_der_meer_jr/alexander_van_der_meer_jr.md), father.**

## Story Role

A mid-level corporate antagonist and gossip source. He is not part of the 2077 Alex/Evelyn main plot, though the family's business links him to the mining backdrop.

## Known inconsistencies

- The press says Sebastian "joined a few months ago" but also credits him with "exponential growth" and with already being CEO; the family file gives no arrival date. Ruling: he joined the Las Flores business a few months before 2077 and was installed as CEO on arrival; the "growth" credit refers to the strategy he and Liam are driving, not to a long tenure.
