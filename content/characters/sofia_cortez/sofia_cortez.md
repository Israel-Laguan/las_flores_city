# Sofia Cortez

**Title:** Environment Correspondent, La Prensa

**Physical Description:**
- Latina woman in her 30s with a lean, wiry build, sun-freckled skin, angular face, high cheekbones and wavy dark hair; dresses in practical field-reporter attire with a press badge.

**Description (full):**

Covers environmental issues affecting Las Flores, including the aftermath of the Great Lithium Leak. Her reporting has raised awareness and prompted policy changes.

**Age (2077):** 35 (b. 2042)
**District:** Las Flores
**Role:** Environment Correspondent
**Descendancy:** Latin American

- **Fear:** Becoming the correspondent who only documents decline, because the contamination does not end. *(invented)*
