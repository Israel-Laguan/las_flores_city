## Newsroom context

She wrote the story "[How the Business Elite Manipulate Local Politics](../../lore/stories/business_elite_manipulate_politics/business_elite_manipulate_politics.md)", exposing the Business Coalition's shadow government: an unregistered lobby of the city's wealthiest individuals and corporations, campaign-finance manipulation, and corporate control of Las Flores politics. The piece names figures such as [Li Wei](../li_wei/li_wei.md) and the Van der Meer family, and quotes Councilwoman [Ana Silva](../ana_silva/ana_silva.md).

- **Born 2038** (39 in 2077). She was 14 at the leak. *(her path is invented)*
- She holds a political-finance beat that overlaps [Alejandro Ruiz](../alejandro_ruiz/alejandro_ruiz.md)'s. *(invented overlap)*
- **Name note:** unrelated to Ana Silva; the shared surname is a coincidence. *(assumption; the lore does not say)*

## Want, Flaw, Fear

- **Want:** To make the Business Coalition's influence visible, with a paper trail a court or a council can act on.
- **Flaw:** Stubborn about her own framing: once the pattern is clear she is slow to hear evidence against it. *(invented)*
- **Fear:** That the story has no consequence, because the people it names write the rules that would act on it. The piece's message is that campaign money flows to sympathetic politicians. *(her fear is invented)*
