# Lin Xiu

**Age:** 20
**Title:** University Student, Port Business Worker

**Appearance & Heritage:**
Like her sister, Lin Xiu's appearance reflects her working-class roots rather than delicate "idol" standards. She has a shorter, stockier build and hands that are still slightly calloused from working the docks. She has a rounder face, sun-tanned skin, and the family's signature sharp, "swallow-tail" eyebrows, which give her a naturally intense resting expression. She sports a chic bob cut.

Proud of her Peranakan (Straits Chinese) heritage, her playful style switches between vibrant colors and elegant traditional prints. She loves bold earrings and statement bags, often featuring intricate Nyonya beadwork and bright batik patterns mixed into her modern university attire.

**Personality & Quirks:**
Bubbly and charming on the surface, Lin Xiu hides insecurities that drive a superiority complex similar to her sister's. She can quickly judge others based on superficial traits to validate herself.
*Shared Quirk:* Like Lin Mei, her default defensive posture is crossing her arms and lightly tapping her opposite elbow when assessing someone.

**Background:**
She grew up working the heavy cargo and stalls in her parents' port business. She now studies literature at the university and writes as a deep emotional outlet. Though eager to connect through her writing, her critical, judgmental attitude sometimes sabotages her real-life relationships.

**Dynamic with Sister (Lin Mei):**
They share a bond of mutual support mixed with fierce competition. Reflecting their deep-seated insecurities, they close ranks against outsiders. 
*The Secret Language:* Whenever they need to talk privately in public, or when they are in a screaming match, they completely drop standard phrasing and argue in a rapid, impenetrable mix of Hokkien and local port slang.
