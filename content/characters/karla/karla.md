## Overview

Karla was a local farmer and activist who was murdered in February 2053 by Minera Estrella's corporate enforcers. She had been trying to demand the mining company compensate for contaminating her Carinata oil plantation with lithium hydroxide — the waste had escaped from a pipe explosion at the mining facility, reached a river affluent, and contaminated her crop. Before dying, she passed crucial evidence to Evelyn Ruthenberg and asked her to find Nubia.

## Background

Karla was a university graduate in agricultural science. The family had stretched its savings to pay for her degree, and after graduating she worked for several years before putting everything she had earned into her Carinata plantation, with one goal: to finally repay her family for their sacrifice and give her younger sister Nubia the chance to rest. The lithium contamination destroyed that plan just as the family was struggling most.

Karla had been contacted by **Cecilia Pérez**, a safety auditor turned researcher who needed a Carinata grower, and the two worked together on Cecilia's Carinata study (they knew each other but were not classmates). After Cecilia was murdered in 2049, Karla visited Cecilia's small experimental field, connected what she saw there with what she observed on her own plantation, and found the preprint of Cecilia's Carinata study at the university. She continued the study on her plantation — and after the August 2052 leak, her poisoned fields confirmed Cecilia's idea that Carinata resists lithium. Karla never had the audit context: she had the Carinata science, not the containment reports.

## Physical Description

Karla was approximately 28 years old at the time of her death. She had dark hair styled in neat braids, and a face that — even in her final moments — radiated defiance despite the pain. She was found wearing a once-vibrant yellow dress, now stained crimson. She was frail in her dying state, with no strength to move her right arm, which had been severed in several places by a long knife.

## The Evidence

Karla's own field notes confirmed, on her contaminated plantation, what Cecilia Pérez's preprint had predicted — proof that Carinata resists lithium, and that the contamination had been affecting local agriculture. She carried three items hidden in her left shoe:
1. **A Key** — to a small wooden box in her apartment
2. **A Small Green Pouch** — containing Carinata seeds that had surprisingly resisted the contamination, and money
3. **A Note** — for her sister Nubia, instructing her to take the money and flee the city

## Relationship to Nubia

Karla and Nubia were **sisters** (they shared the same surnames). They lived together in a small apartment in Las Flores, where they had been tenants for a long time. Nubia, six years younger, had gone to work instead of university so the family's savings could go to Karla's degree, and she held a steady job that covered most of the household costs once Karla's plantation was poisoned and her investigation cost her income. One of them was vegetarian. After Karla's murder, Nubia was also killed in their apartment — the killers searched the place but missed the small wooden box containing the evidence.

## Legacy

Karla's death set Evelyn on the path of investigation that would eventually lead to the exposure of the Great Lithium Leak cover-up. The Carinata seeds she preserved became a key piece of evidence — plants that showed lithium resistance a full year before the public disaster, proving pre-leak contamination had been documented and ignored.
