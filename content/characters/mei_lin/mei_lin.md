# Lin Mei

**Title:** 22-year-old Business Student & Older Sister

**Appearance & Heritage:**
Lin Mei has a solid, broad-shouldered build and sun-tanned skin—lasting physical reminders of years spent hauling crates at the family's port business. Her face is defined by a strong, angular, and slightly asymmetrical jawline, topped with striking, naturally arched "swallow-tail" eyebrows. She keeps her sleek black hair in a high, practical ponytail.

Coming from a Peranakan (Straits Chinese) background, Lin Mei's fashion is a unique blend of her heritage and modern trends. She favors modern, chic clothing that combines high-fashion pieces with streetwear, but always incorporates subtle traditional Nyonya batik prints or intricate beadwork into her outfits.

- **Identifying detail:** a worn leather wristwatch from the warehouse, rotated so the face sits on the inside of her wrist, where she checks it without uncrossing her arms. *(invented)*

**Name note:** the character's slug is `mei_lin`, but she is addressed in text and dialogue as **Lin Mei** (family name first, as is conventional); her sister is Lin Xiu. Do not rename ids.

**Age:** 22 (b. ~2055)
**Occupation:** Business student at the university; works at the family's port warehouse
**Heritage:** Peranakan (Straits Chinese)

## Personality & Quirks

Lin Mei exudes a confident and sometimes intimidating presence. She feels the heavy weight of her responsibility as the eldest and often compensates for her insecurities by acting superior to her peers. While she has a sharp tongue and can be condescending, she deeply longs for acknowledgment.

- *Shared Quirk:* When dealing with strangers or feeling defensive, she adopts a specific posture: arms tightly crossed, one hand lightly tapping the opposite elbow. The tapping slows when she is thinking or worried rather than angry (see the parents dialogue).
- Reads people for their usefulness. Her first move with a newcomer is to offer a task: "If you're actually willing to learn our logistics grid, my father might at least stop complaining."

## Want, Flaw, Fear

- **Want:** Recognition, above all from her father, who respects anyone who understands logistics. She also wants to hold the family business and the heritage together. *(the claim that the recognition she wants most is her father's is partly invented; the stories only say she "longs for acknowledgment")*
- **Flaw:** Condescension as armor. Acting superior to her peers is how she covers inadequacy, and it makes her cold to the one person she is close to. When Xiu says their parents treat them like "museum pieces", Lin Mei looks at the player coldly and blames them for putting ideas in Xiu's head.
- **Fear:** That she is not as good as her academic success suggests, and that the heritage will end with her generation. She says it as duty: "We are curators of our heritage, Xiu. If we don't hold onto it, who will?"

## Background

Lin Mei grew up near the port, helping her parents sell and move goods alongside her sister from childhood. The family's port business operates at the coast facility that grew with the lithium trade; the port itself dates from the Minera Estrella construction years (see [timeline](../../lore/timeline.md)). *(the family's arrival and how their business came to be there are not stated; any link to the lithium trade is invented)* Her mother worries the sisters will forget their Hokkien and that the Nyonya recipes will stop with them; her father thinks about logistics and the warehouse. She now studies business at the university and is academically successful, but wrestles with feelings of inadequacy.

## Dynamic with Sister ([Lin Xiu](../lin_xiu/lin_xiu.md))

Lin Mei and Lin Xiu share a complex relationship built on mutual support yet underpinned by competition. They reassure each other in private but turn against others when threatened.

- *The Secret Language:* When they want to exclude outsiders, or when they are furiously arguing with each other, the sisters instantly switch into a rapid-fire, hyper-specific mix of Hokkien and local port slang that nobody else at the university understands.
- **The cost:** the sisters disagree over the parents. Xiu, who studies literature, chafes at being treated like "museum pieces"; Mei, the eldest, defends the duty and sees herself as the family's curator. When Xiu wavers, Mei is hurt, not angry.
- Both share the crossed-arms tap, the swallow-tail eyebrows and a superiority complex. The difference is that Xiu is bubbly where Mei is intimidating.

## Other Relationships

- **Parents (unnamed):** The mother disapproves of her daughters spending time with locals, and thinks a "Latino boy is just a distraction from the family business". The father is practical. They are protective of the heritage rather than hostile to locals ("they're just afraid of fading away in this city").
- **The player:** a possible romantic interest alongside her sister. Mei tests the player with work first, and softens ("slowly uncrosses her arms") when the player shows respect for her culture.

## Story Role

Lin Mei is a social-storyline character: the older half of the Lin sisters in four dialogues (a classroom argument, an Old Town Café encounter, the parents' expectations and a Hokkien language scene). She is **not** part of the main 2077 plot. She is a student and port-family contact whose arc is about heritage versus assimilation in a multicultural city.
