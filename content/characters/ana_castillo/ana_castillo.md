# Ana Castillo

**Title:** Community Organizer, Sector Norte

**Physical Description:**
- Latina woman of about 45 with a soft, rounded build, warm brown skin with fine lines around the eyes, a round face and a dimple on the right cheek
- Wavy dark hair held back with a clip; wears a practical apron over a plain cotton top, carries a canvas tote, faint burn scar on the left forearm

**Description (full):**

A community organizer in Sector Norte, Ana Castillo runs a local food cooperative that provides affordable groceries to the neighborhood. She was one of the early supporters of Alex's movement, allowing the cooperative's back room to be used for the first community meetings. She never joined the frontlines but was instrumental in building trust between the movement and the neighborhood's residents. After the 2077 broadcast, she served on the transitional council's food security committee.
