# Ana Castillo

**Title:** Community Organizer, Sector Norte

**Physical Description:**
- Latina woman of about 45 with a soft, rounded build, warm brown skin with fine lines around the eyes, a round face and a dimple on the right cheek
- Wavy dark hair held back with a clip; wears a practical apron over a plain cotton top, carries a canvas tote, faint burn scar on the left forearm

**Description (full):**

A community organizer in Sector Norte, Ana Castillo runs a local food cooperative that provides affordable groceries to the neighborhood. She was one of the early supporters of Alex's movement, allowing the cooperative's back room to be used for the first community meetings. She never joined the frontlines but was instrumental in building trust between the movement and the neighborhood's residents. After the 2077 broadcast, she served on the transitional council's food security committee.

**Age (2077):** ~45
**District:** Sector Norte
**Role:** Community Organizer
**Descendancy:** Latina

**Name warning:** not [Ana Kim](../ana_kim/ana_kim.md), the library employee and member of Alex's investigation group (an older story draft mislabelled that Ana as "Ana Castillo"; see [The Moral Fracture](../../lore/stories/the_moral_fracture/the_moral_fracture.md)). Not [Ana Silva](../ana_silva/ana_silva.md), the councilwoman, nor Ana Ramirez of Fundación Esperanza.

## Background

- **Born ~2032 in Sector Norte** (45 in 2077) *(her origin is invented; the lore gives her only the neighborhood)*. She runs a food cooperative that sells affordable groceries to the neighborhood, so her trade is the daily trust of people who count what they can afford.
- **Early 2077:** when [Alex Garcia](../alex_garcia/alex_garcia.md)'s movement needs a place to meet that is not an LW-watched room, she lets them use the co-op's back room for the first community meetings. She never joins the frontline but is "instrumental in building trust between the movement and the neighborhood's residents."
- **The community-center raid.** Mayor Vega's raid on the Sector Norte community center (ordered directly by him, executed on Adeyemi's command) injured fourteen people. Her co-op stands in the same neighborhood. *(that she was nearby and helped with the injured is invented)*
- **After the 2077 broadcast:** serves on the transitional council's food security committee.

## Want, Flaw, Fear

- **Want:** A neighborhood that can feed itself without owing anyone. The co-op is her answer to a city that left Sector Norte "written out of the city's story".
- **Flaw:** She cannot say no to anyone who asks to use the back room. The same openness that makes the co-op a trusted meeting place makes it a risk if the movement draws attention. *(invented example)*
- **Fear:** That the co-op is closed or raided and that the people who depend on it are the ones who pay for the movement's risk. *(invented)*

## Habits and Contradictions

- Practical apron over a plain cotton top, a canvas tote, a faint burn scar on her left forearm from the co-op kitchen. *(the origin of the scar is invented)*
- Remembers every regular's usual order and what they cannot afford, and quietly adds it to the bill. *(invented)*
- Contradiction: an organizer who never wanted to be political, whose shop became a movement's first meeting place.
