# Peter van der Meer Jr.

> **Role:** Young LGBTQ+ Advocate, Public Heir & Reluctant Influencer
> **Faction:** `van_der_meer`

## Overview

**Born:** 2063
**Age:** ~14 in 2077 (game present)
**Family:** Son of Peter van der Meer and Rafaela; stepfather-figure Jan van der Meer Jr.; nephew of Anna van der Meer; grandson of Jan and Maria van der Meer
**District:** Las Flores
**Personality:** `activist_heir`

Peter Jr. is the only child of a marriage of convenience, the Van der Meer heir, and the youngest public face of **Amor Verdadero**. He is outspoken and warm, and says he wants to study law. He is also a brand. A management agency runs his content calendar, his mother pushes him toward glamour, and his feed is full of yacht days, VIP invitations, an entourage and old gossip about girlfriends and boyfriends. The central question around him is **who is this for?**

## The Contradiction

- **Public:** He hosts the yacht "Sunset Socials", smiles for the ring light, and the rainbow pin is in every photograph.
- **Private:** He is often alone with his phone, follows a schedule someone else wrote, and goes quiet when the cameras stop.
- **His circle:** Most of the "friends" are hangers-on who like the access and talk behind his back. His one real friend, Tomás Quispe, is the one the feed keeps calling his "namorado".
- **Management:** His manager Adrián Beltrán sells the Amor Verdadero angle because it works.
- **His mother:** Rafaela encourages a very public life and gets visibility from it.

Whether the cause is his, his father's, his aunt's, his mother's or the agency's is a question he cannot answer himself. The gossip and the feed are made by adults. He only knows he does not remember choosing the schedule.

## Physical Description

- Build: Lean and wiry, slim adolescent frame with narrow shoulders and a long neck
- Skin: Fair to clear olive-fair
- Hair: Wavy dark blonde, usually slightly tousled
- Eyes: Round, warm brown (from Rafaela); thick brows, the right one a fraction higher
- Face: Angular, high cheekbones, straight nose, full lips; jaw not yet fully defined
- Distinguishing features: A small scar near the right ear; a rainbow-enamel pin on his collar; a sport earbud clipped to one ear

## Family & Background

Born in 2063 a year after his parents married to please their families. He was about two at his father's 2065 press conference and about seven when Rafaela's 2070 tell-all aired, including the line "I worry that Peter Jr. will face the same struggles his father did, hiding who he is." His father later married Jan Jr. His aunt Anna co-founded Amor Verdadero with his father.

## Personality & Traits

- **Warm performer:** Easy with strangers, great at a crowd, and slightly too good at it.
- **Detached in private:** The performance switches off when the cameras do.
- **Earnest:** Genuinely wants to be useful, and does not know how to tell if his motives are his own.
- **Observant:** Reads rooms for a living; reads himself less well.
