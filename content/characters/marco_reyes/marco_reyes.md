# Marco Reyes

**Title:** Retired Dockworker, Union Organizer

**Physical Description:**
- Age (2077): 67 (born 2010)
- Build: Broad and heavily built, with wide shoulders and a thick neck
- Face: Square, deeply weathered and scarred, with heavy dark brows over dark eyes
- Hair: Thick gray-white, combed back from a high receding forehead, with a thick gray-white mustache
- Skin: Olive-tan, with rope-burn scars on his hands
- Clothing: Worn dockworker jacket, union cap and heavy boots

**Description (full):**

A retired dockworker from the Port District, Marco Reyes spent thirty years organizing labor at the port facilities. He knew Miguel Jhonson's father from the union and became an unexpected ally of the 2077 movement, providing access to the port's storage facilities for supply caches. He was instrumental in smuggling evidence documents out of the city to international journalists when the local media was compromised. After the transition, he advised on labor policy reform.

## Want, Flaw, Fear

- **Want:** Dockworkers who are not disposable. Thirty years of organizing, and still the same fight about safety and pay.
- **Flaw:** Gruff and mistrustful of outsiders, especially the young and educated. He tests people by giving them heavy work first. *(invented)*
- **Fear:** That the caches he hides lead the LW Group to the union's members and their families. *(invented)*

## Habits and Contradictions
