# Carlos Mendoza

**Title:** Retired Minera Estrella Structural Engineer ("el Ingeniero")

**Physical Description:**
- Elderly, lean and wiry Latino man of about 68 with a long face, thin gray-white receding hair combed back, weathered skin, a deep worry line between the brows and a slight tremor in his hands; wears old engineer's clothes with spectacles on a chain.

**Description (full):**

The younger of the two Mendoza brothers. Carlos was a structural engineer at Minera Estrella: embankments, tank foundations and the supports and anchoring of the pipe runs beside the Río de las Flores. After the 2052 leak, he was told to re-run his calculations so that they would match the story of an electrical failure and heavy rain; he refused to sign, and quietly kept the originals. In 2077 he quietly provided crucial technical information to the investigators about the structural failures at the processing facility. He was not among the building owners at Residencial Las Estrellas, but his drawings and calculations helped prove the cover-up. He lived in fear of retaliation for decades, and only came forward when it became clear the movement had the strength to protect him.

**Age (2077):** 68 (b. 2009)
**District:** Las Flores (river side, downstream of the old plant)
**Role:** Retired Minera Estrella Structural Engineer
**Descendancy:** Latin American

## Career

- Studied engineering at Universidad Nacional de Las Flores; joined Minera Estrella in the mid 2030s after his brother Roberto vouched for him (invented).
- Structural lead for riverbank embankments, tank foundations and pipe supports near the river.
- His calculations showed the supports on the riverside run were undersized for the load and the weakened ground. This matches the existing lore that the court narrative shifted focus away from pre-existing structural weaknesses near the river.
- Excluded from the 2062 Las Estrellas buy-in (never senior enough, never asked), so not a building owner (invented reason; the "not an owner" fact is existing).

## Why he came forward first

Carlos is the one who kept the originals: dated drawings, load calculations, and his own refusal in writing. He lived with that folder for twenty-five years. After Liu Fang's death he was convinced someone would come for it. He first approached the 2077 investigators through the student movement, then persuaded his brother to follow (invented sequence).

## What he gave the 2077 investigators

The structural calculations, riverbank survey drawings and pipe-support details that show the breach pattern fits ground and support failure plus corrosion, and that a "controlled explosion from electrical wiring" cannot explain it.

## In 2077

- Lives in a small house by the river, where he can see the water he blames himself for. Walks the embankment most mornings and measures it by eye (invented habit).
- Carries a slight tremor in his hands; folds and refolds his spectacles on their chain when nervous.
- Colleagues called him "el Ingeniero" (invented).
- More talkative and more frightened than his brother; the one who breaks and also the one who decides.
