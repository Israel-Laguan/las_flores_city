# Nadia Al-Farsi

**Title:** Corporate Negotiator

**Physical Description:**
- Athletic-compact composed build, clear olive skin, and a persuasive composed bearing. Oval face with a prominent jaw, narrow nose, deep-set eyes, and arched brows.
- Often wears polished corporate-negotiator attire with subtle LW Group branding and a sport earbud.

**Description (full):**

Nadia Al-Farsi is a highly skilled lawyer and negotiator working for a powerful investment group based in the Middle East. Her group has partnered with Li Wei's company (LW Group) to secure a stake in the Las Flores lithium market. She works closely alongside Yī Sāng to manage crises and control the public narrative during protests.

Fluent in Arabic, English, and Spanish, Nadia is known for her sharp intellect, her ability to navigate complex legal and political landscapes, and her outwardly unwavering loyalty to her employers. However, beneath her polished corporate exterior, she is secretly investigating the rumors of illegal activities by some of the mining companies, including human rights violations.

**Age (2077):** ~47 (b. ~2030; revised from ~2037, which would make her about 22 at the late-2050s café scene as an established negotiator)
**District:** Las Flores
**Role:** Corporate Negotiator
**Descendancy:** Middle Eastern

**Employer:** a Middle Eastern investment group partnered with [LW Group](../li_wei/li_wei.md) *(the group is unnamed in the lore)*

## Want, Flaw, Fear

- **Want:** To know what her employers' partners have done, and what she is a party to, before she has to decide where she stands. *(invented motive)*
- **Flaw:** Complicity by position. Her job is to control the narrative of the very protests and incidents she is quietly investigating; every polished statement she writes helps the cover. *(from the lore's setup)*
- **Fear:** Being exposed by her own file: Yī Sāng "treats people as risk ratings", and a negotiator who asks questions is a risk. *(invented)*
