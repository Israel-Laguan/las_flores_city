## Overview

**Born:** ~2025
**Age (2077):** 52
**Role:** Behavioral Supervisor, N&M LTD
**Status:** Alive

## Physical Description
- Hair: Black, graying evenly, kept extremely short and neat
- Eyes: Dark, expressionless, analytical
- Build: Average height, rigidly straight posture
- Skin: Fair
- Distinguishing features: Minimal facial movements or gestures; wears severe, perfectly tailored dark suits; speaks with absolute, quiet precision.

## Background
Vance shares a distant family connection with the founding Nakamura family, though he is not an executive decision-maker. He earned his position through relentless, dispassionate efficiency. He serves as an internal behavioral supervisor for Nakamura & Morgan LTD's conditioning programs in Las Flores. Unlike client-facing liaisons, Vance rarely interacts directly with subjects after their initial processing. Instead, he monitors them through data: compliance rates, heart rate variance, deviation flags, and productivity metrics. 

## Personality
Cold, methodical, and profoundly indifferent. Vance is not cruel for cruelty's sake; rather, he genuinely views human beings as biological systems to be optimized. He is dispassionate and precise. If a subject requires "recalibration," he orders it with the same emotional investment one might have when rebooting a server.

## Role in the Story
Vance appears primarily during the `dialogue_awakening` sequence, overseeing the initial conditioning and metric baselining of the player. After this, he fades into the background, operating through liaisons like Ingrid or communicating via internal N&M channels. He is NOT reachable via SMS.

## Connections
- [Ingrid Haugen](../ingrid_haugen/ingrid_haugen.md): His subordinate. She handles the messy human interactions, while he handles the data.
- **Nakamura Family**: Distant relation, providing him a measure of untouchability within the corporate structure.
