# Manuel Ortega

**Title:** Former Smuggler, Cartel Link

**Physical Description:**
- Latino man in his early sixties with a lean wiry build, long weathered face, thin gray-white receding hair, deep-set eyes and a faded smuggling scar across his right cheek; dresses in practical fisherman's layers.

**Description (full):**

Manuel Ortega, commonly known as "El Viejo" (The Old Man), is a 63-year-old former smuggler who has now transitioned into a pivotal link for a powerful cartel operating in Old Las Flores and the city center. Originally from San Pedro de los Pescadores, Manuel grew up in a fishing family but was lured into the world of smuggling as a young man seeking excitement and fortune.

**Age (2077):** ~63
**District:** Las Flores
**Role:** Former Smuggler
**Descendancy:** Latin American

## Context and Allegiances

**Allegiance:** to the Las Flores Cartel of [El Caimán](../el_caiman/el_caiman.md). His `faction` was changed from `independent` to `criminal`.

- **Born ~2014** in San Pedro de los Pescadores, a river fishing village in the Far South district on the Río de las Flores (it is not on the sea coast). His smuggling started on the river and its docks. *(the river specifics are an assumption from the village's description)*
- **The cartel.** The Flowers Syndicate lore lists the cartel as a "tense cooperation" partner, with joint drug trafficking routes. Manuel is the link in Old Las Flores and the city center: a man the cartel trusts because he knows how things move without being seen.
- **La Familia.** [Don Pedro](../don_pedro/don_pedro.md)'s organization keeps an uneasy 2063 settlement with the cartel; Manuel's role as a link is exactly what keeps that settlement quiet. *(invented dynamic)*
- **Old ties.** He is of the fishing community that [Isabella Quispe](../isabella_quispe/isabella_quispe.md) and [Diego Sulca](../diego_sulca/diego_sulca.md)'s mother belong to; the village knows him as "El Viejo". *(invented)*

## Want, Flaw, Fear *(all invented)*

- **Want:** A quiet end to his career: the cartel's trust and no surprises.
- **Flaw:** Calculated cool, but sentimental about the river that raised him; he cannot pass up a debt owed to a neighbor.
- **Fear:** A younger cartel man or an informant replacing him, and the contamination of the river his village lives on.
