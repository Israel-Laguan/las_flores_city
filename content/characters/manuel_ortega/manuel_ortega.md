# Manuel Ortega

**Title:** Former Smuggler, Cartel Link

**Physical Description:**
- Latino man in his early sixties with a lean wiry build, long weathered face, thin gray-white receding hair, deep-set eyes and a faded smuggling scar across his right cheek; dresses in practical fisherman's layers.

**Description (full):**

Manuel Ortega, commonly known as "El Viejo" (The Old Man), is a 63-year-old former smuggler who has now transitioned into a pivotal link for a powerful cartel operating in Old Las Flores and the city center. Originally from San Pedro de los Pescadores, Manuel grew up in a fishing family but was lured into the world of smuggling as a young man seeking excitement and fortune.

**Age (2077):** ~63
**District:** Las Flores
**Role:** Former Smuggler
**Descendancy:** Latin American
