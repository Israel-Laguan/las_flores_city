# Diego Reyes

**Title:** Former Luz del Río Plant Operator

**Physical Description:**
- Latino man in his mid-50s with a compact, heavy-shouldered build worn by years of floor work, warm-brown skin, close-cropped curly hair going grey at the temples, thick brows and tired, steady dark eyes; wears a plain work jacket over a faded crew-neck shirt.

**Description (full):**

A former Luz del Río plant operator who ran machinery on the facility floor. In 2054 he lost his wife and younger daughter to the contaminated river water; his surviving son has a documented neurological condition associated with lithium exposure. He was cited for disorderly conduct at two protests outside the Luz del Río corporate offices (2057 and 2059), which gave him a motive that looked almost too clear.

Chief Inspector Adeyemi arrested him in public for the Residencial Las Estrellas murders, on an evidence package assembled from Luz del Río's internal records, including a psychological assessment added to his file in 2056 under circumstances no one could explain. Diego had never worked in records and could not have built that file himself. The framing collapsed when the 2077 broadcast exposed the true nature of the case. He was released within seventy-two hours of the broadcast, gave a single brief press statement and declined further interviews.

**Age (2077):** 55 (b. 2022)
**District:** Las Flores
**Role:** Former Plant Operator / Falsely Arrested Protester
**Descendancy:** Latin American

**Not to be confused with:** [Diego](../diego/diego.md), the Mina Escondida guide, or any other Diego. Diego Reyes is the man Adeyemi arrests in *The Reyes Incident* dialogue and in the *Retirement Complex Murders* story.
