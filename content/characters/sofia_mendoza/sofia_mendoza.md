# Sofia Mendoza

**Title (full):** Student Activist
**Title (short):** Sofia Mendoza, Student Activist

**Description (full):**
**Age:** 18
**Origin:** Working-class neighborhood in a secondary city in South America (migrated to Las Flores)
**Occupation:** Student Activist / Community Youth Organizer

Sofia Mendoza navigates Universidad del Valle with a worn copy of vintage hero comics tucked into her canvas jacket and eyes that still believe in community solidarity. Having lost a close childhood friend to street violence in her hometown, she understands the real price of unchecked lawlessness and institutional apathy. She carries a stack of job applications and community organizing flyers, striving to protect local youth from gang recruitment and corporate exploitation without resorting to cruelty or violence.

Challenges: Economic hardship, survivor guilt, balancing family obligations with student advocacy, and resisting the urge to seek dark vigilante revenge.

**Born:** ~2059 (18 in 2077)
**Birth name:** Graciela Ramírez. She adopted "Sofia Mendoza" on migrating to Las Flores (see [Real Heroism in Latam](../../stories/real_heroism_in_latam/real_heroism_in_latam.md)).
**District:** South Las Flores (Old Las Flores) and Universidad del Valle

## Physical Description

Young Latina woman of 18 with slightly olive skin and brown curly hair in a ponytail; wears street clothes and a worn canvas jacket with vintage hero comics tucked inside.

- **Identifying detail:** a cheap superhero T-shirt worn under the canvas jacket, faded from washing, the one item she kept from home. *(invented)*

## Background

- **Hometown:** A secondary South American city, a working-class house with the football on a small TV and a father's quiet worry. At home her love of superhero stories earned her the label "she likes that boys stuff".
- **Camilo's death** (date not stated; before she migrates, so shortly before 2077 *(invented timing)*): her closest friend [Camilo Orozco](../camilo_orozco/camilo_orozco.md) dies. She loses her "safe fantasy" and, for a time, becomes a street-level vigilante: she trains in street survival, carries a knife or a stolen pistol, and intervenes in robberies and domestic violence anonymously.
- **What it cost:** fights are fast and pathetic, people freeze rather than cheer, and the criminals are teenagers. She almost kills someone by accident. She leaves with paranoia, insomnia and numbness, and she notices she is becoming less like Camilo.
- **Migration:** moves to Las Flores with [Alberto Ramírez](../alberto_ramirez/alberto_ramirez.md), changes her name and builds a civic form of heroism: flyers, youth work, community organizing. The shift away from violence is the story's thesis, and it is not complete.

## Want, Flaw, Fear

- **Want:** To keep Alberto, and the neighborhood's youth, out of the gangs, and to prove goodness can survive in a city that punishes it. *"How does someone remain good in a place that punishes goodness?"*
- **Flaw:** She tries to carry everyone. She stays close to the gang world to protect Alberto, and each step gets her closer to the violence she says she has renounced. An unspoken temptation to "seek dark vigilante revenge" is always there.
- **Fear:** Becoming someone Camilo would not recognize. Her survivor guilt is not just that he died; it is that she is slowly less like him.

## Habits and Contradictions

- Pauses near neighborhood soccer fields and outdoor bakeries, taking comfort in small moments of everyday community life.
- Hands out community flyers in South Las Flores, where gang members harass her for it. She keeps handing them out anyway.
- Carries a stack of job applications she does not expect to succeed. *(that she has not yet had a reply is invented)*
- **Contradiction:** an idealist who knows exactly how ugly violence is, and who trained in it first.

## Story Role

Sofia is the lead of the branching storyline [Real Heroism in Latam](../../stories/real_heroism_in_latam/real_heroism_in_latam.md), a five-beat arc: the encounter with gangers handing out flyers, the brother's orbit, the confession, the discovery that Los Culebras is funded by Minera Estrella to destabilize South Las Flores for cheap real-estate buyouts, and the finale. Depending on trust she either stays and builds a community network (the player gains a faction leader), or she is disillusioned and leaves or dies. She is **not** part of the 2077 Alex/Evelyn main plot; she is a civil-society ally in the side arc. Her faction in the data is `civil_society`.

Her state is tracked by `sofia_status`, `sofia_trust` and `alberto_status`, and five dialogues are attached to her; do not change her id.
