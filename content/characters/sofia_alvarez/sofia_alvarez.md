# Sofia Alvarez

**Title:** Chair, Humanity First

**Physical Description:**
- Latina woman in her late 50s with an athletic-compact build, oval face, pronounced cheekbones, voluminous curly dark hair and a small mole on the right cheek.

**Description (full):**

Once the energetic leader of the Humanity First Youth Council, she has risen to become the current Chair of the movement, representing a new generation of leadership in Las Flores.

**Age (2077):** 59 (b. 2018; revised from 2042 to match the 20-year-old Youth Council leader of 2038)
**District:** Las Flores
**Role:** Chair
**Descendancy:** Latin American

**Movement:** [Humanity First](../../lore/organizations/movements/humanity_first/overview/overview.md), Chair since November 2057

---

## Background

- **Born 2018.** At 20 (November 2038) she leads the new Youth Council: film screenings and art workshops focused on movement values, which quickly attract students. In August 2039 she is formally head of the Youth Council.
- **October 2045:** leads workshops on leadership, activism and sustainable development; the Youth Council works with schools.
- **August 2052:** the lore has her organizing the volunteer response to the Great Lithium Leakage with [Javier Torres](../javier_torres/javier_torres.md), as head of the Youth Council: barriers, warning signs, an emergency relief fund, expert collaboration.
- **November 2057:** elected Chair at 39. Her first year overlaps the **Tomas Herrera scandal** (2057–2058): a contractor who inflated costs and hired relatives; it becomes public in late 2058 and leads to mandatory third-party audits and anti-nepotism rules.
- **2077:** still Chair, 59, representing "a new generation of leadership".

Timeline sources: [Founding Era](../../lore/organizations/movements/humanity_first/timeline_founding/timeline_founding.md), [Growth Era](../../lore/organizations/movements/humanity_first/timeline_growth/timeline_growth.md), [Influence Era](../../lore/organizations/movements/humanity_first/timeline_influence/timeline_influence.md), [Rogue Incidents](../../lore/organizations/movements/humanity_first/rogue_incidents/rogue_incidents.md).

## Want, Flaw, Fear

- **Want:** To keep the movement from calcifying: the Youth Council leader who became its senior figure, with "fresh ideas and renewed commitment to founding principles".
- **Flaw:** She inherits a system built to prevent the last scandal, and Tomas Herrera arrived in her first year. Each reform answers the previous failure. *(her sense of this is invented)*
- **Fear:** Becoming the establishment she once challenged. *(invented)*

## Habits and Contradictions

- Voluminous curly dark hair and a small mole on her right cheek.
- Still talks to students the way she did at 20, and is accused by the old guard of favoring the young. *(invented)*
- A former youth organizer who now enforces the audit rules.

The movement's public face and the contact for civil-society scenes. She is **not** part of the 2077 Alex/Evelyn main plot. *(whether the movement joins Alex's cause is not stated)*
