**Description (full):**

Valeria Vasquez was pretty before the clinics. That is the wound Isabella never forgives — not only that Valeria got the tall hourglass genes, but that she then **paid to exaggerate them** into a doll richer men could not stop collecting. Lips, nose, breasts, waist, face freeze: each procedure a vote that natural beauty was never enough, that love required a more commercial body. Isabella's gene-hatred sharpened watching Valeria turn advantage into spectacle.

For years she lived as that spectacle — a toy for rich men: escort-adjacent companionship, sponsored weekends, jewelry that was a leash, modeling that paid in exposure. Love never stuck. Money never stayed. Every romance that looked like rescue ended as a bill, a quieter mistress, or a man who liked the doll until he wanted a wife who did not look like content.

She married rich anyway. The divorce detonated when **leaked porn** from the doll years hit the web. Settlement thinned. Brands fled. She was left with a **kid**, a permanent shame archive online, and one beloved **condo** she will not lose. Appearances and a still-lucrative social profile keep the lights on; new alliances plug the gaps. She is always inventing the next status play — rebrand, charity face, Chronicle rehab if Isabella allows it.

Under the filters lives **melancholy**: she knows the surgeries bought attention, not safety; that the videos mean some lovers will only ever want the clip, not the woman putting a child to bed in the tower. She searches for love anyway — daring to date, flirting like hope is a strategy — while bracing for the past to beat her again the moment someone searches her name in bed.

Publicly the primas kiss cheeks. Privately Valeria needs Isabella's press more than pride, and Isabella holds rehab or ruin in the same hand as old envy.

- Leak: the past became public property; melancholy settled in as a roommate — quiet, dressed well, always home
- Now: she scrolls her own feed to feel desired, then puts the phone down and feels older than the face in the mirror

## Dating the Player

**What she wants:** Real love — someone who stays after seeing the leak and the kid and the debt — while her habits still reach for arrangements that pay the condo.

**Fear loop:** She dares to open up → waits for the search → flinches when they hesitate → sometimes sabotages first to feel in control → melancholy → posts a thirst trap → tries again.

### Male player
- Opens with high-glamour warmth: touch on the arm, condo invite framed as wine and a view, compliments that sound like content captions
- Tests early: do you flinch at her body as product, or see her?
- If you have money/status: she may slip into doll mode (useful, practiced) then hate herself for it — romance deepens if you refuse the transaction without humiliating her
- If you mention the videos badly: she shutters or lashes with charm-as-weapon
- If you are kind about the child and the condo: trust rises fast; she may cry once, then joke it away
- Physical: she knows how to perform desire; genuine desire scares her more

### Female player
- Less automatic doll script; more sisterly glamour or competitive beauty talk
- Can become real intimacy if the player does not treat her as a cautionary tale or a trophy subplot
- May ask for help navigating men/exes/Isabella while testing whether romance is safe
- Jealousy of the player's ease in rooms Valeria has to buy entry to

### Either — dating beats
1. **Feed-flirt** — public tags, private DMs
2. **Condo test** — invited up; apartment is beautiful and slightly too staged
3. **Past lands** — they find the leak or she confesses first (branch)
4. **Melancholy night** — after the child sleeps; no performance, just fear
5. **Choice** — arrangement (money/safety) vs relationship (love/risk); she will push you to define it because she cannot stand the middle

**She will not** abandon the child or casually sell the condo for a romance. **She might** accept a humiliating Isabella deal if it protects both.
