# Juan Pérez

**Title (full):** Star Journalist, The Las Flores Chronicle
**Title (short):** Juan Pérez, Chronicle Star Reporter

**Age (2077):** 32 (b. 2045)
**District:** Las Flores (Chronicle offices; elite party circuit)
**Role:** Star journalist / elite scandal hunter
**Faction:** Media
**Descendancy:** Latin American

**Physical Description:**
- Charismatic presence; dresses like someone who expects to be photographed at galas
- Sharp eyes; practiced smile that opens doors and then inventories the room
- Moves easily between newsroom grit and high-society charm

**Description (full):**

Juan Pérez is the star reporter at *The Las Flores Chronicle* — an ambitious journalist known for exposing the secrets and scandals of Las Flores' elite. Sharp wit, uncanny nose for weakness, and a devoted following among socialites who fear and quote him in the same breath. His reporting blurs journalism and gossip by design: the city's powerful read him to see who is bleeding.

He works under owner **Isabella Vasquez**. To the public they are a duo — her connections open doors, his bylines make noise. The truth is asymmetric. Juan digs; Isabella greenlights, delays, or kills. He still tells himself they share a crusade against hypocrisy. She uses that belief. Some of his best exclusives become printed spectacles. Others vanish into her favor economy — traded with **Petra Solis**, buried for **Dong** / Flowers, or held against **Chen Wei**'s circle. Juan notices the pattern late, or pretends not to, because the Chronicle is still the biggest stage in town.

The **2069** Flowers near-miss taught Isabella that Juan's speed can get her killed. Since then every "sensitive" name routes through her desk. He resents the leash and thrives on the access it still buys. He does not know the full garage story. Finding out would rewrite how he sees her — and whether he stays her blade or becomes her problem.

**Outlet:** [The Las Flores Chronicle](../../lore/media/press/las_flores_chronicle/las_flores_chronicle.md), owned by [Isabella Vasquez](../isabella_vasquez/isabella_vasquez.md).

## Want, Flaw, Fear

- **Want:** The scandal that makes the Chronicle's pages impossible to ignore, and a name that outlives Isabella's masthead.
- **Flaw:** Speed over caution. He prints first and calculates later; the 2069 near-miss was his byline, and he still half-believes the leash was an overreaction. He also mistakes access for friendship. *(his self-image as a "crusader" is invented)*
- **Fear:** That he is only a brand Isabella rents out, and that the archive he keeps is the only proof he ever did real work. *(invented)*

## Habits and Contradictions

- Collects off-record confessions at bars after galas; flirts for access, not always for romance. *(invented)*
- Keeps a private archive of spiked stories. It is his insurance and, if Isabella learns how complete it is, his death warrant. *(archive is established; its completeness is not)*
- Preaches against hypocrisy while living off the hypocrites' parties. The chipped front tooth is a story he tells differently each time. *(origin invented)*
- Charm as research: warm in the room, an inventory by the time he leaves. *(invented; the lore says only "charismatic")*

## Relationships (with cost)

- **[Isabella Vasquez](../isabella_vasquez/isabella_vasquez.md), owner.** She greenlights, delays, or kills his work and keeps him "hungry and almost-famous" so he never becomes independent enough to publish her. He believes they share a crusade; she lets him. Cost: his best exclusives are traded away without his knowledge.
- **[Petra Solis](../petra_solis/petra_solis.md), [Dong van der Meer](../dong_van_der_meer/dong_van_der_meer.md) / the Flowers Syndicate, and [Senator Chen Wei](../senator_chen_wei/senator_chen_wei.md)'s circle.** Subjects of stories that were spiked, softened, or held as leverage. He sees the pattern only in his archive. *(that he suspects it is invented)*
- **[Valeria Vasquez](../valeria_vasquez/valeria_vasquez.md).** Isabella's cousin and his most tempting story: a rehab feature or a leak exposé. How Isabella rules on it shows what the family is to her.
- **Chronicle staff.** Juniors want his desk and feed him tips; he trades credit for leads and has made enemies of the ones he stiffed. *(invented)*
- **The Las Flores elite.** Audience and prey; they leak on each other through him and try to buy or ruin him.
- **El Informador and its staff.** The grassroots counterpoint to everything he does; a rival outlet's offer to "burn Isabella" could come from the same media ecosystem. *(the offer's source is not stated)*

## Story Role

A gossip-source and elite-dirt quest-giver NPC. The player can feed him stories, use him as cover, or push him toward Isabella's 2069 garage file. He is **not** part of the 2077 Alex/Evelyn main plot, but he is the likeliest person to accidentally hand the player the garage notes.

**Story Hooks**
- Juan asks the player to verify a lead Isabella told him to drop.
- His spiked-story archive includes the 2069 garage notes.
- A rival outlet offers him a chair if he burns Isabella.
- He wants the **Valeria Vasquez** story; Isabella's choice is the tell, and Valeria may beg the player to tip her first.

## Known inconsistencies

- The Chronicle lore calls it a "glossy lifestyle magazine"; Isabella's file calls it both "the paper" and "the magazine". Resolved as a digital-first glossy magazine with a small luxury print run, so "printing" a story means publishing it.
