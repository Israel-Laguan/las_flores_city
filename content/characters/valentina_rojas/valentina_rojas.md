# Valentina Rojas

**Title:** Curious Girl from the Mountains

**Description (full):**

Valentina Rojas is an 11-year-old girl living with her parents near a small hotel situated in the Andean Mountains of Las Flores. Her parents, who are hardworking individuals, manage various tasks at the hotel, such as cleaning, maintenance, and cooking. They come from a long line of mountain dwellers and understand the importance of education for breaking the cycle of hard labor.

Valentina has a bright smile and sparkling brown eyes that reflect her curiosity about the world. She has wavy black hair that she often wears in two ponytails, allowing her playful spirit to shine. Her clothing is typically modest but well cared for, often featuring colorful patterns.

Valentina is energetic, clever, and full of dreams. She is determined to learn and make a difference in her family's life, often spending her free time reading books and drawing. She is well-spoken for her age, and her inquisitive nature often leads her to ask questions about the world beyond her village.

She attends her small village school, where she stands out as one of the brightest students. Her parents have made sacrifices to keep her there, believing education will be her ticket to a better future. When the family travels to Central Las Flores, the crowds and the size of the city amaze her, and she quickly starts missing her abuela and her mountain home.

Valentina loves drawing and storytelling, often sketching scenes from her life in the mountains, such as the little house where her family lives, and creating imaginative tales about her adventures. She also enjoys exploring nature and dreams of becoming an environmental scientist or an artist who can advocate for preserving the beauty of her mountain home.

Valentina hopes one day to study at the university in the city, and to return to her village as a professional, using her knowledge to improve the living conditions of her community. She dreams of helping her parents live an easier life without them having to work so hard in the hotel.
