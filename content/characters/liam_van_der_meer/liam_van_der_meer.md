# Liam van der Meer

**Title:** Executive, Van der Meer Industries

**Physical Description:**
- Dutch man of about 42 with an athletic-compact build, angular face, high cheekbones, thick brows and blue-grey eyes
- Straight slicked-back blond hair threaded with gray, light stubble; small scar near his left ear
- Executive business attire with a Van der Meer Industries pin (executive outfit)
- **Identifying detail:** light stubble and slicked-back hair, which, with the blue-grey eyes, set him apart from [Jan van der Meer Jr.](../jan_van_der_meer_jr/jan_van_der_meer_jr.md), who has a near-identical description (see the note below). *(the contrast is an editorial choice)*

**Description (full):**

Cousin of [Sebastian van der Meer](../sebastian_van_der_meer/sebastian_van_der_meer.md). Together they have led strategic decisions at Van der Meer Industries, driving exponential growth and identifying profitable investment opportunities. He is also the illegitimate son of [Alexander van der Meer](../alexander_van_der_meer/alexander_van_der_meer.md), and half-brother of Xiu Li and Dong.

**Age (2077):** ~42 (b. 2035)
**District:** Las Flores
**Role:** Executive
**Descendancy:** Dutch

---

## Background

- **Born ~2035** from Alexander's infidelity, while Alexander was still married to Wang Mei Li (he married her in 2025 and divorced publicly around 2046). So Liam grows up as the unacknowledged son of a famous family. *(how openly he was acknowledged is not stated; his upbringing is invented)*
- **Executive at Van der Meer Industries.** Advocates better EU–Las Flores relations and works closely with European ambassadors; also works with [Xiu Li](../xiu_li_van_der_meer/xiu_li_van_der_meer.md) on attracting European investment in sustainable development.
- **Marries [Sofia Rodriguez Krol](../sofia_rodriguez_krol/sofia_rodriguez_krol.md) in 2068**, a union that creates "powerful alliances". Three young children: twins Miguel and Maria (b. ~2070) and Antonio (b. ~2073), with future aspirations of diplomacy, law and engineering.

## Want, Flaw, Fear

- **Want:** Legitimacy: to be a Van der Meer by merit, as an executive and a diplomat, in a family that did not give it to him by birth. *(invented motive; supported by the facts of his birth)*
- **Flaw:** He over-trades on connections: ambassadors, a politically useful marriage, a cousin partner. Competence and alliance-building blur. *(invented framing)*
- **Fear:** That his standing depends on someone else's goodwill, now that [Anna](../anna_van_der_meer/anna_van_der_meer.md) holds the Chair and Sebastian the CEO post. *(invented)*

## Habits and Contradictions

- Takes meetings with ambassadors personally and at length. *(from his role; the habit is invented)*
- The contradiction: the illegitimate son whose job is to present the family's respectable face to Europe.

## Relationships (with cost)

- **[Alexander van der Meer](../alexander_van_der_meer/alexander_van_der_meer.md), father.** The affair that produced Liam also broke his father's marriage.
- **[Xiu Li](../xiu_li_van_der_meer/xiu_li_van_der_meer.md) and [Dong](../dong_van_der_meer/dong_van_der_meer.md), half-siblings.** Xiu Li is a close working partner; Dong runs the criminal Flowers Syndicate and is the family's open wound.
- **[Sebastian](../sebastian_van_der_meer/sebastian_van_der_meer.md), cousin and partner.**
- **[Sofia Rodriguez Krol](../sofia_rodriguez_krol/sofia_rodriguez_krol.md), wife.**

## Story Role

A family executive and a diplomatic contact. He is not part of the main 2077 plot.

## Known inconsistencies

- Liam and Jan van der Meer Jr. share the same age (b. 2035), build, hair and scar-by-the-ear description. Resolved in text by wardrobe (Liam in executive suits, Jan Jr. in soft knits); keep that contrast in the portrait pass.
