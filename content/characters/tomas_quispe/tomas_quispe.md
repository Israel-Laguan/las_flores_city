# Tomás Quispe

> Tags: `#figure` `#npc`
>
> **Role:** Peter Jr.'s School Friend

## Overview

A quiet 14-year-old from Los Andes on a scholarship at Peter Jr.'s school. The feed keeps calling him Peter Jr.'s 'namorado'. In truth he is the only person who asks Peter Jr. how he is, and he doesn't care about the cameras. He is unbothered by the rumor and a little embarrassed by it.

## Physical Description

Short, sturdy Andean Latino boy of 14 with dark straight hair, a round face, smooth brown skin and thick-framed glasses; wears an old knit school jumper under a borrowed, oversized jacket.

## Related Lore

- [Peter van der Meer Jr.](../peter_van_der_meer_jr/peter_van_der_meer_jr.md)

## Background *(all invented unless noted)*

- **Born 2063** (14 in 2077), from the Los Andes district, where [Amara Vargas](../amara_vargas/amara_vargas.md)'s mining community lives. His family is modest; his parents may work in or around the Andes mine. *(invented)* "Quispe" is a common Andean surname; he is not stated to be related to [Isabella Quispe](../isabella_quispe/isabella_quispe.md) of San Pedro.
- **The school.** He is on a scholarship at the exclusive school Peter Jr. attends, among children of money. His oversized borrowed jacket and old jumper mark him as the scholarship boy, and he does not hide it.
- **The rumor.** The feed calls him Peter Jr.'s "namorado". He is "unbothered by the rumor and a little embarrassed by it." In truth he is the only person who asks Peter how he is. Peter, in Act 3, says Tomás "is the only person who tells me when I'm being weird."

## Want, Flaw, Fear

- **Want:** To finish school and send something home, and to keep the one friendship that has nothing to do with his scholarship.
- **Flaw:** Too honest to lie for anyone, including to protect his own reputation, which sometimes makes him seem cold. *(invented)*
- **Fear:** That the rumor costs him the scholarship, or that Peter Jr.'s adult handlers push him out of the picture. *(invented)*

## Habits and Contradictions

- Thick-framed glasses, a dry sense of humor, steady manners. *(from his description)*
- Unimpressed by status, yet careful with a borrowed jacket. *(invented)*
