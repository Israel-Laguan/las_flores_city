# Tomás Quispe

> Tags: `#figure` `#npc`
>
> **Role:** Peter Jr.'s School Friend

## Overview

A quiet 14-year-old from Los Andes on a scholarship at Peter Jr.'s school. The feed keeps calling him Peter Jr.'s 'namorado'. In truth he is the only person who asks Peter Jr. how he is, and he doesn't care about the cameras. He is unbothered by the rumor and a little embarrassed by it.

## Physical Description

Short, sturdy Andean Latino boy of 14 with dark straight hair, a round face, smooth brown skin and thick-framed glasses; wears an old knit school jumper under a borrowed, oversized jacket.
