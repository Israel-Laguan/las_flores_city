# Xiao Chen

**Title:** CEO of LW Group

**Physical Description:**
- A Chinese woman in her early 50s with an oval face, almond eyes, a straight dark elegant bob threaded with gray and a tiny mole near her lower lip. She has an athletic-compact build and wears CEO executive attire with a small sport earbud.
- **Identifying detail:** the small earbud, which she keeps in even in meetings, and the tiny mole near her lower lip. *(that she keeps the earbud in during meetings is invented)*

**Description (full):**

Appointed in 2065, she is the first woman to lead the LW Group conglomerate. Known for her forward-thinking approach, diplomatic persuasion, and emphasis on sustainable tech and social responsibility. Before running the company she led the defense of Minera Estrella in the lithium leak trial, which is how she first appears in the story.

**Age (2077):** 52 (b. 2025, Chongqing)
**Education:** Peking University
**District:** Las Flores (Northeast District; LW Group's base)
**Role:** CEO of LW Group
**Descendancy:** Chinese

## The central tension

She built her reputation by arguing the "natural causes" story that was a cover-up: the staged electrical failure Li Wei approved in 2052 and Qiu Bo executed. The lore does not say whether she knew. It is the ambiguity that defines her. *(whether she knew is deliberately left open; any answer is an invention)*

## Want, Flaw, Fear

- **Want:** To make LW Group a respectable, sustainable-technology company, one that earns the legitimacy its past cannot. Under her, the group expands CSR in education, healthcare and environmental conservation.
- **Flaw:** Persuasion as a substitute for accounting. Her diplomacy lets her carry the company's history without ever looking at it, and her mentor Li Wei ensures she "remains anchored to the state interests he spent his life securing". She is a modern face over old machinery.
- **Fear:** The past breaking through the polish: that the trial testimony she once argued unravels, and that the sustainable company she built turns out to rest on a staged disaster. *(framing as a fear is an interpretation)*

## Habits and Contradictions

- Diplomatic persuasion is her instrument. She wins by agreement rather than orders, in contrast to Li Wei, who "doesn't issue orders; he suggests directions."
- The contradiction: an advocate of social responsibility whose career started by denying responsibility for a poisoned river, and who promotes sustainability while the company's "inner circle" includes the people who fabricated the evidence.
- Uses a small sport earbud as part of her executive dress. *(invented as a habit)*

## Relationships (with cost)

- **[Li Wei](../li_wei/li_wei.md), mentor and predecessor.** He sees in her "the modern version of himself", but remains "the ghost in her machine". The cost: she is CEO in title and anchored in practice.
- **[Zhang Liang](../zhang_liang/zhang_liang.md), inner-circle investor.** Provides financial prudence and "light shadow mentoring" and advises the Xiao Chen-era executives on what not to put in writing.
- **[Qiu Bo](../qiu_bo/qiu_bo.md), Head of Tech & Innovation.** Her technology lead and part of the inner circle; he also oversaw the 2052 fabrication of evidence her defense later relied on.
- **[Yi Sang](../yi_sang/yi_sang.md).** Remains useful "to whoever sits the CEO chair" as the man who knows where the paperwork lives.

## Story Role

An antagonist-adjacent executive. She is the public face of the LW Group during the 2077 endgame, and the person through whom the group's respectable image is maintained. The group's real power sits with Li Wei, the "Emperor in the Shadows". She is not a direct player-facing contact unless a scene requires one.
